<objective>
Compute the phase's actual question: does crossmodal HSC-vs-Legacy-Survey alignment differ between
the two curvature-direction regions, and is that difference distinguishable from noise?

Eight cells — two regions by `k` in {5, 10, 20, 50} — each with its own MKNN score, its own
region-scoped permutation null at 1,000 permutations, its own 95% percentile bootstrap CI at 1,000
resamples, and its own hubness statistic. Then the verdict, applied exactly as pre-registered in
plan `04-03` and not one word otherwise.

Purpose: MKNN-03 through MKNN-08. This is the first plan in the phase permitted to see a regional
number, and the guard added in `04-03` enforces that: `--mode regional` refuses to run unless the
pre-registration and the frozen split both already exist.

Output: `--mode regional` in the runner; eight regional rows in
`notebooks/.cache/04_region_partition_mknn.jsonl`; notebook sections 7-8 executed.
