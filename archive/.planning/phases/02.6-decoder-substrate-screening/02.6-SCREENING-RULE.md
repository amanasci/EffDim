# Phase 02.6 Screening Rule

This document gates nothing, seals nothing, and produces no verdict artifact. It records the
bars Phase 02.6 screens candidates against, chosen and ratified before any Phase 02.6 number was
measured. It is not a pre-registration and carries no ancestry proof, because this phase screens
rather than gates.

Ratified at plan `02.6-02`'s blocking `checkpoint:decision`, 2026-08-10. The user reviewed all
five option groups individually and selected: **A3, B1, C3, D1, E1** — declining `A1`, `A2`,
`A4`, `B2`/`B3`, `C1`, `C2`, `D2`, and `E2`. Every disposition is recorded explicitly in the
table at the end of this document; nothing is silently inherited.

## The four axes and their bars

All four axes are evaluated and reported **separately** and are never weighted, summed,
multiplied or ranked into one score. No single figure combining all four exists anywhere in this
phase's screening.

### 1. Direction

**Comparative, no absolute bar.** Median cosine similarity of `H_est` to analytic `H`, no worse
than the raw-point arm's median cosine similarity recomputed on the identical fixture in the
same run. No source document proposes an absolute bar for this axis, so none is adopted. (Raw-
point arm's own measured value, already on the record: `0.9996`.)

### 2. Magnitude

**Comparative, no absolute bar,** on both required sub-statistics: `|median magnitude ratio − 1|`
no worse than the raw-point arm's `|median ratio − 1|` in the same run, **and** per-point CV no
worse than the raw-point arm's CV in the same run. (Raw-point arm's own measured values: median
ratio `0.8934` → `|ratio − 1| = 0.1066`; CV `0.2925`.) Neither sub-statistic alone suffices — a
clean median hides a destroyed CV and vice versa (per `chart_curvature.curvature_fidelity_report`'s
own design intent); both must clear.

### 3. Calibration — **B1, ratified**

**Comparative ONLY.** `|calibration slope − 1|` no worse than the raw-point arm's `|slope − 1|`
in the same run. (Raw-point arm's own measured slope: `0.7061` at `R² = 0.3828` → `|slope − 1| =
0.2939`.) **There is NO absolute bar of slope ≥ 0.5 / R² ≥ 0.25.** `B3` (which would have added
that absolute component) was explicitly declined — see the disposition table.

### 4. Rank — **A3, ratified**

**Purely comparative, no absolute floor.** A candidate's Spearman `rho` against analytic `H`
must beat the raw-point arm's Spearman `rho`, both recomputed on the identical fixture in the
same run. This is RESEARCH's own stated step 2(c) as written. **No absolute floor of 0.50 or of
0.6712 is used** — `A1` (the `0.50` literal transferred from stage 1) and `A2` (the `0.6712`
literal frozen from the raw-point arm's own measurement) were both explicitly declined.

**Caveat that travels with the raw-point comparator every time it is invoked, on this axis and
every other:** the raw-point arm itself cleared only 3 of its own notebook's 4 sanity lines — it
missed a `> 0.90` bar (`02.5-09-SUMMARY.md`, "Reported, not absorbed"). "Beats the raw-point arm"
is a real bar but a weaker one than "recovers the analytic answer," and this sentence is the
caveat's one canonical statement — cite it, do not restate it with different wording elsewhere.

## The deliberate sensitivity reduction — recorded plainly

**B1 and C3 together remove both guards that caught the CAE negative control.** The CAE's
failure was invisible to the rank axis (seed 2 scored `rho = 0.8665`, beating the raw-point arm
outright) and visible only in calibration, where its measured slope was `−0.1856` at `R² =
0.0533`. Under `B1`, a candidate clears calibration by being merely **no worse than the raw-point
arm in absolute terms** — the raw-point arm's own `|slope − 1| = 0.2939` is the bar, not any
absolute floor near zero-slope. Under `C3` (below), a single bad seed does not disqualify a
candidate whose other three seeds are clean.

This reduction was surfaced to the user before ratification, and they confirmed it deliberately.
It is recorded here as an explicit, deliberate disposition — **not an oversight** — so a later
reader of this document, or of `02.6-FINDINGS.md`, knows the screen's sensitivity was reduced on
purpose and by whom (the user, at the plan `02.6-02` checkpoint, 2026-08-10).

## The seed protocol

**Seed set — D1, ratified:** torch/split seeds `0, 1, 2, 3` — the CAE negative control's own
set, directly comparable seed-for-seed against `02.5-09-SUMMARY.md`'s published four-seed table.
`D2` (a fifth seed, `4`, matching `curvature_probe.SEED_REPLICATE_N`) was explicitly declined.

`FIXTURE_SEED = 20260807` and `N_POINTS = 3000` are held fixed across all four seeds; only the
torch/split seed varies, exactly as `02.5-09`'s own protocol did.

**Required per-seed table columns** (extends RESEARCH § Seed Protocol with median cosine so all
four axes appear):

`torch seed | reconstruction rel. err | median cosine | rho | median magnitude ratio | magnitude CV | calibration slope | calibration R² | max metric condition number`

**Spread statistic:** `SPREAD = max(values) − min(values)` over the four seeds' Spearman `rho`,
reported alongside the mean and median. This is the same statistic RESEARCH proposed and the
same one already computed for the CAE's own table (`SPREAD = 0.8665 − (−0.1444) = 1.0109`).

**Disqualifying condition — C3, ratified: NONE.** No single-seed excursion and no magnitude of
`SPREAD` disqualifies a candidate at this screening stage. `SPREAD` (and the full per-seed table
it is computed over) is **reported, and weighed in FINDINGS prose only** — a human judgment
made with the full table in view, not a mechanical gate. `C1` (RESEARCH's own proposed rule:
disqualify on any one seed missing a floor, OR `SPREAD > mean_rho − floor`) and `C2` (SPREAD-only
disqualification, no single-seed clause) were both explicitly declined.

## The promotion procedure — E1, ratified

RESEARCH § Promotion Rule's five steps, as written, **with steps 2(a) and 2(b) amended** to match
the axis bars and seed-spread disposition ratified above (see the disposition table for what
changed in each):

1. Every screened candidate gets the full per-seed, four-axis table (above), never an
   averaged-only summary.
2. A candidate clears Stage A only if all three hold: **(a) — amended, C3:** there is no
   disqualifying condition to fail; the seed table and its `SPREAD` are reported for FINDINGS to
   weigh, not gated on here. **(b) — amended, B1:** its calibration `|slope − 1|` is no worse
   than the raw-point arm's `|slope − 1|` in the same run (not RESEARCH's original absolute
   "not close to zero" reading). **(c) — ratified, A3:** it beats the raw-point arm on the rank
   axis, recomputed in the same run, with the raw-point-arm caveat above stated wherever invoked.
3. If zero candidates clear: "no promotion" is a complete, reportable outcome for this phase,
   consistent with this project's standing posture (Phase 2's `GATE_VERDICT=FAIL`, 02.2's
   `CAE_VERDICT=FAIL`, 02.4's `TOPOAE_VERDICT=FAIL`, 02.5's `CURVATURE_VERDICT=FAIL` — none
   treated as an error to route around). Recommend naming Ollivier-Ricci/intrinsic curvature as
   the next step rather than inventing a new fallback.
4. If exactly one candidate clears: promote it, with the promotion argument stated against the
   two effect sizes below — not merely asserted as "it passed."
5. If more than one candidate clears: prefer, in order — (a) a candidate requiring no new
   implementation and no new PU-side training over one requiring either (plain AE and TopoAE
   both qualify; RTD-AE/Witness AE/GRAE do not); (b) among tied-tier candidates, the one whose
   PU-side context gives an independently argued reason to prefer it, stated explicitly rather
   than by default ordering. A genuinely new implementation is only preferred over an
   already-built, already-PU-fitted candidate if its expected gain is argued to exceed the two
   named effect sizes explicitly — "a better topological loss" alone is arguing inside the 6%.

**At most one candidate is promoted (SC-5), always.** Zero candidates clearing is a complete,
reportable result, not a stall.

## The two effect sizes any promotion argument must be made against

- **~3.4x** on H0 retention, single-chart versus multi-chart.
- **~6%** on the topological-loss axis alone (TopoAE `0.668` vs plain AE `0.628`).

A candidate justified only by a stronger topological loss is arguing inside the 6%.

## The three inferences that must not be drawn

Transcribed from `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` Section 4, verbatim in substance:

1. **Swiss roll topology does not transfer.** The roll is contractible as a manifold, yet its
   ambient Vietoris-Rips diagram carries a genuine `beta_1 = 1` from near-touching spiral arms.
   Scoring a `d=2` latent on "preserve the ambient H1" penalises correct unrolling.
2. **Reconstruction quality validates nothing about curvature.** A decoder learning
   `y = 0.7 a x^2` where the truth is `y = a x^2` has tiny reconstruction error and a second
   derivative 30% wrong. The CAE reconstructs the roll 2.96x better than a matched plain AE and
   still produced structureless curvature.
3. **H0 preservation is not curvature fidelity.** They are different properties measured by
   different instruments, and nothing yet measured connects them.

is never written into this document as a bar. For the record: `curvature_probe.py:1457` cites
`0.50` only inside an f-string/log message, and it is **not** an importable module constant
(confirmed by direct grep, per `02.6-PATTERNS.md`). Had `A1` been ratified instead of `A3`, this
document would have been required to write `0.50` in as a literal with this provenance stated,
never imported. Since `A1` was declined, no such literal appears here.
