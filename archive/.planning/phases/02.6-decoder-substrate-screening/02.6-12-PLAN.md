<objective>
Deliver the CLAUDE.md-mandated Swiss roll sanity notebooks for the two plain-autoencoder-family
candidates on the persistent-homology axis (SC-6; D-07, D-20, project CLAUDE.md).

CLAUDE.md's rule is not optional and not a follow-up task: a model that fails on real data has two
possible causes — the data has no structure to find, or the implementation is broken — and only a
manifold whose answer is known in advance separates them. On this axis the instrument is new even
though the models are not, so each candidate's notebook is what establishes that a persistent-homology
number computed on a real trained model means what it is read as.

These notebooks prove the instrument runs on one seed with pictures a human can read. They do not
carry the read-out matrix: CLAUDE.md caps a sanity notebook at about fifteen cells and bars threshold
tables inside it, which is why the 192-number matrix lives in `notebooks/diagnostics/` — the same
split Phase 02.4 made for its lambda sweep and Phase 02.5 for its feasibility sweep.

Purpose: without these, a persistent-homology FAIL on PU would be uninterpretable and every
downstream conclusion drawn from it unsupported.
Output: `notebooks/02.6_swiss_roll_plainae_ph_check.ipynb` and
`notebooks/02.6_swiss_roll_topoae_ph_check.ipynb`, both executed with outputs.
