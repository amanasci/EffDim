<what_this_phase_must_not_conclude>
Read `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` Section 4 before writing a word of the argument. It records
three inferences that must **not** be drawn from a Swiss roll result, because the inverse error has
already been made twice in this milestone:

1. **Swiss roll topology does not transfer.** The roll is contractible as a manifold, yet its ambient
   Vietoris-Rips diagram carries a genuine `beta_1 = 1` from near-touching spiral arms.
2. **Reconstruction quality validates nothing about curvature.** A decoder learning `y = 0.7 a x^2`
   where the truth is `y = a x^2` has tiny reconstruction error and a second derivative 30% wrong.
   The CAE reconstructs the roll 2.96x better than a matched plain AE and still produced structureless
   curvature.
3. **H0 preservation is not curvature fidelity.** Different properties, different instruments, and
   nothing yet measured connects them.

Point 3 bites hardest here, because the strongest thing known about the plain AE is an H0 result
(`~0.53-0.63` retained versus the CAE's `0.183`, at one eighth the parameters). That is not evidence
about curvature and must not be used as any part of a promotion argument. Transcribe all three into
the document and check the finished argument against them.
</what_this_phase_must_not_conclude>
