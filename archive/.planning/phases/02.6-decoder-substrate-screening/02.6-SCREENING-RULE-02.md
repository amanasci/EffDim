# Phase 02.6 Screening Rule — 02 (the persistent-homology axis)

This document is **non-gating**: its bars gate nothing, seal nothing, and promote nothing. It
records the persistent-homology agreement criterion, its 16-cell read-out matrix, its seed
protocol, and its provenance conventions — chosen and ratified before any Phase 02.6 number on
this axis was measured. The promotion decision belongs wholly to `02.5-10`, under its own
seal-before-measure discipline, once every number this document produces is disclosed there per
`02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §6. This is a deliberate choice to claim less than the
document it succeeds: `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4 records that the previous ratified rule
(`02.6-SCREENING-RULE.md`) constrained no published conclusion, because the axis it gated was
changed after an unfavourable result. This document does not read as that one renamed — it
ranks candidates and stops there.

Ratified at plan `02.6-07`'s blocking `checkpoint:decision`, 2026-08-10. The user reviewed all
five option groups individually and selected the recommended option in every group: **P1, Q1,
R1, S1, T1** — declining `P2`, `P3`, `Q2`, `Q3`, `R2`, `R3`, `S2`, `S3`, `T2`. Every disposition
is recorded explicitly in the table below; nothing is silently inherited.

## The criterion (D-01)

The ranking quantity is **persistent-homology agreement** — the distance between a candidate's
persistence diagram and a reference diagram. It was chosen over isometry distortion against
exact geodesics and over k-NN neighbourhood overlap. **D-01 is one-way**: `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4
already records one criterion changed after seeing an unfavourable result; a second change to
the ranking axis for the same question would be the third criterion tried, and the record cannot
absorb it — no later reader could distinguish "we learned something" from "we kept going until a
candidate won." Whatever this criterion measures is what the phase reports.

## The read-out matrix — exact cell definitions (D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06)

Every cell is named by the canonical label `{space}|{reference}|H{k}|{distance}_norm`, over:

- `space in {latent, decoder_image}`
- `reference in {intrinsic, ambient}`
- `k in {0, 1}`
- `distance in {bottleneck, wasserstein}`

That is **16 numbers per seed per candidate**, 64 across the four seeds (D-11), 192 across the
three candidates (D-20) — Swiss-roll side only. The full sixteen:

`latent|intrinsic|H0|bottleneck_norm`, `latent|intrinsic|H0|wasserstein_norm`,
`latent|intrinsic|H1|bottleneck_norm`, `latent|intrinsic|H1|wasserstein_norm`,
`latent|ambient|H0|bottleneck_norm`, `latent|ambient|H0|wasserstein_norm`,
`latent|ambient|H1|bottleneck_norm`, `latent|ambient|H1|wasserstein_norm`,
`decoder_image|intrinsic|H0|bottleneck_norm`, `decoder_image|intrinsic|H0|wasserstein_norm`,
`decoder_image|intrinsic|H1|bottleneck_norm`, `decoder_image|intrinsic|H1|wasserstein_norm`,
`decoder_image|ambient|H0|bottleneck_norm`, `decoder_image|ambient|H0|wasserstein_norm`,
`decoder_image|ambient|H1|bottleneck_norm`, `decoder_image|ambient|H1|wasserstein_norm`.

All 16 are **reported separately, never weighted, summed, averaged, or ranked into one score**.
No composite figure combining any subset of these cells exists anywhere in this phase.

**H2 is excluded.** ROADMAP.md records no power analysis for `beta_2`, so an H2 read-out could
neither support nor refute a candidate.

**The two references (D-03):**
- **(a) intrinsic** — the true 2-manifold in exact intrinsic coordinates `(arc-length s(t), y)`.
- **(b) ambient** — the ambient 3-D Swiss-roll point cloud.

A correct unroll **matches (a) and diverges from (b)**. Divergence from the ambient reference is
therefore not failure — it is what correct unrolling looks like, per the first "must not be
inferred" item in Section 10 below.

## Provenance columns required beside every cell (Task 2 requirement 4)

Every normalized cell is reported with three companions, never on its own:

1. the **raw (unnormalized) distance**,
2. the **reference diagram's own max persistence**, used as the normalization denominator,
3. a **saturation flag** for every bottleneck cell (per the travelling caveat below).

Per **Q1**, every cloud — model and reference alike — is pre-scaled by
`topoae.latent_unit_scale` before diagram construction, symmetrically, and the **applied scalar
is printed per cloud** alongside its diagram's provenance columns.

## Bars (D-07, D-22)

**Absolute on the Swiss roll, comparative on PU.** An absolute bar is possible on the roll
precisely because its answer is exact; `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §2 establishes that an
absolute bar is not computable on PU, so the PU side stays relative to a matched plain-AE
baseline and a null.

**Stage A admission is descriptive (D-22).** Failing Stage A bars nothing from Stage B: a hard
gate inside a phase whose criterion is ratified non-gating (D-09) would be a contradiction. No
candidate is excluded from the PU-side screen by anything measured on the Swiss-roll side.

## Seed protocol (D-11)

Torch/split seeds **`0, 1, 2, 3`**. `FIXTURE_SEED = 20260807` and `N_POINTS = 3000` are held
fixed across all four seeds; only the torch/split seed varies.

`SPREAD = max(values) − min(values)` is reported alongside the mean and median, per cell, over
the four seeds.

**Disqualifying condition: NONE.** The `SPREAD > mean − floor` rule is **DROPPED**. This is its
**second independent rejection**: it was declined as `C1`/`C2` at the old plan `02.6-02` (see
`02.6-SCREENING-RULE.md`'s disposition table), and it is dropped again here by D-11. The reason
is structural, not a repeat of the same argument: with a non-gating criterion there is nothing
for a disqualifier to disqualify — a disqualifying rule presupposes a promotion decision this
document does not make.

**A-03 is recorded as a live caveat.** PH seed variance is unmeasured in this project. If the
observed `SPREAD` swamps the between-candidate differences, that is a finding about the
criterion itself, and it is reported as one rather than absorbed into silence.

## The two travelling caveats — canonical wording, stated once

Following `02.6-SCREENING-RULE.md`'s own travelling-caveat convention: each is stated once here
and cited everywhere it applies, never restated with different wording.

**Thin `(H1, intrinsic)` denominator.** The intrinsic-plane reference is a flat, simply-connected
rectangle and carries only sampling-noise H1 — no genuine loop exists in it by construction.
Measured at planning time on the standard fixture (`make_swiss_roll_fixture(n=3000,
seed=20260807)`, 600-point subsample via `np.random.default_rng(20260807).permutation(3000)[:600]`,
`ripser 0.6.15`, `maxdim=1`): the intrinsic reference's top H1 life is **`0.3348`** at diameter
**`11.2886`** (life fraction `0.0297`), against the ambient reference's top H1 life of **`0.7198`**
at diameter `4.0360` (life fraction `0.1783`) — a 6.0x gap in normalized terms. `02.6-RESEARCH.md`
Pitfall 2 reports `0.420` for the same cell measured during that session's own run; that number is
subsample-procedure-dependent (a different permutation of the same fixture reproduces a different
raw value) and it is **not pinned as a constant anywhere in this document or downstream** — the
`0.3348` measured for this ratification supersedes it as this document's own record, and neither
value is written into any bar. Per **P1**, the runner reports the `(latent|intrinsic|H1|*)` and
`(decoder_image|intrinsic|H1|*)` cells' raw distance, denominator, and normalized quotient
together, with no floor applied to the denominator.

**Bottleneck saturation.** Bottleneck is a max over a matching: once a model diagram has nothing
close enough to match the reference's longest bar, that bar is sent to the diagonal at exactly
half its own life, and the distance stops responding to further degradation. Measured at
planning time: `persim.bottleneck(ambient_H1_reference, intrinsic_H1_reference) = 0.35988`,
which is **exactly half** the ambient reference's own top H1 life (`0.7198 / 2 = 0.3599`) and
**identical** to `persim.bottleneck(empty_diagram, ambient_H1_reference)`. The two references are
therefore already at the ambient reference's own bottleneck-saturation value with respect to each
other, before any model diagram enters the comparison. The `(H1, ambient)` bottleneck cell —
i.e. `latent|ambient|H1|bottleneck_norm` and `decoder_image|ambient|H1|bottleneck_norm` — is
**expected to be saturated for any candidate whose latent has correctly unrolled the roll**. A
saturated cell is reported with its flag and is **never read as a measurement** — it is not
evidence that the candidate's ambient-H1 divergence is any particular size, only that it is at
least large enough to saturate.

## The inferences that must not be drawn

Transcribed in substance from `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §4, verbatim in intent:

1. **Swiss-roll topology does not transfer.** The roll is contractible as a manifold, yet its
   ambient Vietoris-Rips diagram carries a genuine `beta_1 = 1` from near-touching spiral arms.
   Scoring a `d=2` latent on "preserve the ambient H1" penalises correct unrolling — this is why
   D-03 reports the ambient-reference cells separately rather than folding them into a single
   "topology preserved" verdict.
2. **Reconstruction quality validates nothing about curvature.** A decoder learning
   `y = 0.7 a x^2` where the truth is `y = a x^2` has tiny reconstruction error and a second
   derivative 30% wrong. The CAE reconstructs the roll 2.96x better than a matched plain AE and
   still produced structureless curvature. A clean PH-agreement number carries the same risk and
   must not be read as a curvature claim.
3. **H0 preservation is not curvature fidelity.** They are different properties measured by
   different instruments, and nothing yet measured connects them.

Carried forward from `02.6-CONTEXT.md` in substance, as this document's own record:

- **A-01 (the bridge tests numerical consistency, not correctness).** Autodiff-versus-
  finite-difference agreement (D-16) establishes that the derivative *computation* is stable. A
  net that learned a smooth but *wrong* surface passes it cleanly. As composed, nothing in this
  phase demonstrates that a winner's derivatives are *right* — only that they are *computable*.
- **A-02 (a PH-ranked winner is not thereby a curvature-capable winner).**
  `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §4's first objection — H0 preservation is not curvature
  fidelity — stands unretired by this document's D-03/D-04 machinery, which addresses only the
  ambient-H1 objection. This document's ranking says nothing about curvature capability on its
  own.
