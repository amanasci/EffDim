<objective>
Close the phase: re-check the ancestry proof, assemble the findings, and state what the front
end can and cannot claim.

Purpose: the ancestry re-check at close is not ceremonial. Plan `02.7-08` asserted the rule
commit was an ancestor of `HEAD` **at ratification time**, when nothing had been scored yet —
which is trivially true and proves little on its own. The load-bearing check is this one:
that the rule commit precedes every commit that actually scored a cloud. That is the `02.6-15`
precedent, and it is the difference between a claim of blind ratification and a proof of it.

The findings document is what the deferred topological-VAE phase reads to decide whether it
runs at all. It must therefore be exact about what abstained and why, and honest about the
bounds — a front end whose accuracy is unknown is worse than useless to a phase that would
constrain a VAE's latent space on its output.

Output: `02.7-FINDINGS.md`, and the ROADMAP/STATE updates that record the phase's outcome.

(visible skip).*
