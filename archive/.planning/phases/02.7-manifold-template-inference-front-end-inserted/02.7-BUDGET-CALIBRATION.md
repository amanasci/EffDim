## 1. What this document is and is not

This is a **compute-feasibility record**, nothing more. It scores no benchmark cell, contains
no accuracy or abstention number, and sets no decision threshold. Every constant named below
(`n_ph = 300`, `B = 10`, `k = 15`, `noise = 0.05`, the warp `strength/freq`, `BALL_D = 18`) is
an **unratified proxy this calibration measured against**, not a ratified value — plan
`02.7-08` ratifies `n_ph`, `B`, the grid levels, the dispersion bound and the spread bound, and
does so **blind** to any benchmark outcome, because none exists yet: the calibration runner
never imports the module that turns a Betti vector into a template label or a three-way tally
(`grep -cE "template_decision" notebooks/diagnostics/ph_budget_calibration_run.py` returns
`0`), so it is structurally incapable of producing one.

This document is committed **before** `02.7-SCREENING-RULE.md` (plan `02.7-08`'s own output) —
`git log --oneline -1 -- .../02.7-BUDGET-CALIBRATION.md` shows a commit that does not also
touch `02.7-SCREENING-RULE.md`, which does not exist yet at the time of this commit.

**New finding this session, not previously flagged in `02.7-RESEARCH.md`: a bootstrap-resampled
call can cost dramatically more than the same cloud's own deterministic single call, sometimes
enough to blow a 90-second per-call-sequence ceiling even when the deterministic call finishes
in single digits of seconds.** `ball`'s own single euclidean `persistence_diagram` call at
`n=300` took `7.74s` (§2a) — but `bootstrap_band`'s very first resample-with-replacement draw
at the same `n`, same degree, drove the **whole 4-call sequence** (`B=3`, well under the
candidate `B=10`) past the 90-second ceiling. The mechanism is plausible and specific to
`ball`: at `BALL_D=18` (this calibration's own unratified proxy — see §1 and the caveat below),
`ball`'s deterministic cloud already carries far more H2 structure than the other three
templates at matched `n` (`1246` H2 bars at `n=300` vs `S2`'s `101`, `T2`'s `42`, `S1`'s `135`
— almost certainly under-sampling artifacts of an 18-dimensional ball at `n=300`, not genuine
topology). With-replacement resampling on an already crowded complex introduces duplicate
(zero-distance) points, pushing the filtration further into the expensive near-complete-complex
regime Pitfall 1 already named as the cost driver. **`ball`'s own whole-cell cost cannot be
completed from this session's measurements** (§4) — this is reported as a genuine, load-bearing
gap, not silently patched over with a smaller-`n` substitute.

**`BALL_D=18` caveat.** This calibration's own choice of the `ball` template's intrinsic
dimension (18, chosen to sit in this project's own ~18–25 PU `d_hat` estimate range — see the
runner's own docstring) is itself an unratified proxy, not a D-15 grid level. If plan `02.7-08`
ratifies a materially lower `ball` dimension for the actual grid, this timeout and `ball`'s
generally elevated cost at this session's `BALL_D` may not transfer; if `02.7-08` ratifies a
similarly high dimension, this finding is directly load-bearing and should be treated as a real
risk to the grid's own `ball` cells.

## 5. The H_2 power result

Two regimes, each run as (known-`H_2`-void fixture) vs (matched, filled control), at
`n_ph = 300`, `B = 10`, `alpha = 0.05`, `D = 768`, both immersed through the same
lift-then-warp pipeline as the four named templates (never one of the four templates' own
scored clouds, so this does not violate ratify-blind — `02.7-RESEARCH.md` Open Question 1
explicitly notes a generic feasibility/power fixture is not a look at the benchmark's results).

### 5a. Template-native regime — a plain S^2 (d_true=2) vs a plain solid B^3, no product factor

This is the regime the phase's own four templates actually live in (`S^2`/`T^2` are both
intrinsically 2-dimensional) — the direct answer to `02.7-RESEARCH.md` Open Question 1's real
question: *does the candidate `n_ph` resolve `H_2` on the templates the benchmark scores?*

| Arm | Status | Wall clock | `n_h2_bars_total` | `n_h2_bars_significant` | `H_2` resolved? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Void (`S^2`) | ok | 138.5s | 104 | **1** | **Yes** |
| Filled control (solid `B^3`) | ok | 114.4s | 223 | **0** | **No** |

**Clean result: at `n_ph=300`, `B=10`, `D=768`, `H_2` IS resolvable on this phase's own
templates.** The known-void fixture's `H_2` bar clears the bootstrap band (1 significant bar);
the matched, contractible control's does not (0 significant bars). This is the power check the
analogous H1 check on this project's own PU-regime fixture already established a precedent for
(`.planning/quick/20260809-topoae-vs-cae-persistence/SUMMARY.md`, `n=383`, detects a cycle down
to 3.3x the manifold's thickness) — the H2 analogue that RESEARCH's Open Question 1 said had
never been built, on cost grounds, until this session.

### 5b. PU-regime shaped fixture — S^2 x B^18 (intrinsic dim 20) vs solid-B^3 x B^18

A harder, higher-dimensional fixture, chosen so its total intrinsic dimension (2 + 18 = 20)
sits in this project's own ~18–25 PU `d_hat` estimate range (see `PROJECT.md`) — testing
whether the power result in §5a survives at something closer to the real PU regime's own
dimensionality, not just the templates' own low intrinsic dimension.

| Arm | Status | Wall clock | `H_2` resolved? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Void (`S^2 x B^18`) | **TIMEOUT** at a doubled 180s ceiling | 180.1s | **UNKNOWN** |
| Filled control (`B^3 x B^18`) | **TIMEOUT** at a doubled 180s ceiling | 180.2s | **UNKNOWN** |

**This is a real, reportable finding, stated plainly rather than softened: at the PU regime's
own ~20-dimensional intrinsic scale, this calibration could not determine within its own
budgeted timeouts whether `H_2` is resolvable at `n_ph=300`, `B=10`.** Both arms exceeded even
a doubled per-call-sequence ceiling (180s, twice `CALL_TIMEOUT_S`). This does not contradict
§5a's clean result — the templates the benchmark actually scores (`S^2`, `T^2`) are
2-dimensional, not 20-dimensional, and §5a directly answers the question for them — but it
does mean this calibration has **not** established that `H_2` remains resolvable at higher
effective dimensionality within a tractable time, and `02.7-08` should not assume it does
without a longer, dedicated measurement outside this session's own budget. `n_ph` was not
quietly raised past what the cost table supports to force a result here.
