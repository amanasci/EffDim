<objective>
Complete the decision rule: D-04's joint `(Betti vector, d_hat)` lookup, D-03's four **named**
abstain conditions with the fired condition recorded, and D-13's three-way tally.

Purpose: the four abstain conditions carry **opposite** downstream instructions — (a) widen the
library, (b) terminate the follow-on arm, (c) and (d) retry with more data or a better metric.
One undifferentiated abstention rate hides which applies, and the deferred topological-VAE
phase reads this output to decide whether it runs at all. Underneath everything is the
asymmetry the whole design is built around: **abstention is the cheap failure.** A wrong
template sends that phase to constrain a VAE to the wrong `Z` and derive curvature through it —
a confident wrong number with no detection path.

D-13 keeps that asymmetry visible as data rather than encoding a preference in a formula. Under
the selective-prediction convention a front end that abstains on 95% of cells and is right on
the rest scores 100% accuracy — exactly the failure SC-4's "a selector that never abstains is
not thereby better" warning names, running in the other direction.

Output: `template_decision.py`'s full surface and its three verbatim-named tests.
