<objective>
Run D-15's benchmark and report D-13's three-way tally. This is the plan that answers the
phase's actual question: **can a latent manifold template be inferred from an unlabelled point
cloud at all, and with what accuracy and abstention rate.**

Purpose: selection accuracy is only measurable where the template is known by construction,
which is why this phase is synthetic-first. D-15's grid is a full factorial on `(D, noise)`
because the phase's entire premise is a `D`-by-noise **interaction** — Euclidean PH is
documented to lose a *noisy* circle in *high* ambient dimension, and neither factor alone
reproduces it. Decomposing them into separate one-factor arms would design the effect out of
the benchmark: both metrics could agree everywhere simply because the cell where they diverge
was never visited. `n` and sampling density are the OFAT arms because a full four-axis factorial
is 324 cells before replicate draws, which this phase is not sized for.

`D = 768` is a **required** level, not optional. Phase 02.6 produced three defects that passed
every toy-scale check and failed at real scale — `torch.quantile`'s undocumented `2**24` cap, a
training-budget asymmetry that silently made a comparison unfair, and a safety guard bypassed
by passing a bound method. `02.6-FINDINGS-02.md` §12 records the pattern.

Output: `template_benchmark_run.py` and `02.7-BENCHMARK-RESULTS.md`.

*Planning note: spec-less probe fallback skipped: phase has no requirement IDs to probe
(visible skip).*
</objective>
