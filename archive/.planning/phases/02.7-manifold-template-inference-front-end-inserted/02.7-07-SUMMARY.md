## Accomplishments

- **`notebooks/diagnostics/ph_budget_calibration_run.py`** measures and prints only — it never imports `template_decision` (`grep -cE "template_decision"` returns `0`), so it is structurally incapable of scoring a benchmark cell. Every `ripser`-touching call runs in a forked, hard-timeout-wrapped subprocess (`CALL_TIMEOUT_S = 90.0`) so peak resident memory is isolated per call and a non-finishing cell is recorded as a timeout at a known ceiling rather than hanging silently.
- **Assumption A2 (D=768-plus-warp interaction unverified) did not hold cleanly.** The real immersion is measurably **30–90% more expensive** than `02.7-RESEARCH.md`'s D=3, flat-fixture numbers at every matched `n`, for both `S^2` and `T^2`. `S^2` remains the more expensive template in absolute terms at every `n`, so RESEARCH's sizing advice ("size against `S^2`, not an average") still holds and matters more now that the absolute numbers are higher than anyone reading the D=3 table would expect. Peak resident memory never exceeded ~1.6 GB at any single cost-grid cell — a large margin under the `>7 GB` an earlier, uncontrolled measurement grew past.
- **One complete grid cell composed as a real number** for S1 (241.5s), S2 (341.0s, worst of the three complete templates), and T2 (211.2s, with borrowed H0/H1 legs stated explicitly). `ball`'s own whole-cell cost is reported as an **unresolved gap** — its euclidean H2 bootstrap call timed out even at the small measurement (B=3), so no real per-call number exists to compose from; a rough stand-in extrapolation suggests `ball` could be the true worst-case template, not `S2`.
- **The headline correction plan `02.7-08` must read against:** RESEARCH's own worked arithmetic (`60 combinations x 66 calls x ~7.5s blended H2 rate ≈ 8.25h`) used a single blended per-call guess taken from D=3 numbers. This session's real, degree-specific D=768 measurements give a **higher** worst-template total — `S2`'s own euclidean H2 per-call rate (13.44s/call) alone sits above the top of RESEARCH's blended range. The 180-cell worst-measured-template projection (**17.05h**) is **roughly double** RESEARCH's H2-only estimate, and `ball`'s true cost (unmeasured) could push the real worst case higher still.
- **H_2 power check, two regimes, reported separately, no verdict applied.** (§5a, template-native, `S^2` d=2 vs solid `B^3`): clean — void arm's `H_2` bar clears the band (1 significant bar), filled control does not (0 significant bars). This is the direct answer to RESEARCH's Open Question 1 for the templates the benchmark actually scores. (§5b, PU-regime-shaped, `S^2 x B^18` d=20 vs `B^3 x B^18`): **both arms timed out** even at a doubled 180s ceiling — reported as a genuine, unresolved finding, not softened and not forced by quietly raising `n_ph`.
- **Four options for plan `02.7-08` (A/B/C/D), no option selected here** — see the table transcribed below, each with its measured or stated-extrapolation total wall clock.
- **Every number in the document is labelled measured or explicitly-labelled extrapolation** (fitted power-law for unmeasured `n`; linear-in-B for band cost) — kept visibly separate everywhere, never merged into one figure.
