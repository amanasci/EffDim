## Correction: the "paper's own minimum searched lambda" justification is withdrawn

During plan 02.4-04, the orchestrator supplied a reframing (recorded in `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 10 and Known Limitation 2) stating that `LAMBDA_TOPO=0.1` is "the smallest lambda the paper's own authors considered," because the lambda sweep's grid endpoints `(0.1, 3.0)` exactly match the paper's own log-uniform `[0.1, 3]` search range. **This claim is wrong**, and the error is the orchestrator's, not this plan's or any prior plan's own measurement.

A fifth fidelity gap was found on re-audit (not by this plan, and not acted on here): the reference implementation's reconstruction term is `nn.MSELoss()` -- mean over both batch **and** ambient features. `topoae.py:375`'s reconstruction term is `((xb - decode) ** 2).sum(-1).mean()` -- **sum** over features, mean over batch only. EffDim's reconstruction loss is therefore `D` times the reference's (`D` = ambient dimension), which reparameterizes `lambda_topo` by a factor of `D` relative to the paper's own convention: EffDim's `LAMBDA_TOPO=0.1` corresponds to a paper-convention lambda of roughly `0.1 / D` -- about `0.033` on the 3-dimensional Swiss roll fixture, and about `1.3e-4` on the 768-dimensional PU embedding -- both far below the paper's searched `[0.1, 3]` range, not at its floor.

**Both halves of this correction stand together, deliberately:**
- The "paper's own minimum searched value" justification for `LAMBDA_TOPO=0.1` in `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` is withdrawn.
- This does **not** mean the topological term was inactive or negligible. The topo-to-recon *ratio* (the quantity `_transfer_ratio_from_history` measures, invariant under this feature-sum-vs-mean reparameterization since it is a ratio of the two terms as actually computed, not a comparison to an external scale) was measured at `0.227701`-`0.313072` across the eight PU rungs and `0.422840` on the Swiss roll (`02.4-05-SUMMARY.md`, `02.4-03-SUMMARY.md`) -- the topological term carried real, comparable weight in both settings, never an order-of-magnitude gap.
- Gap #5 therefore does **not** explain T1's near-parity with (in fact, its measured loss to) the baseline in this plan's evaluation -- the topological term was genuinely active during training; whatever produced T1's FAIL is a separate question from whether `lambda` was effectively too small to matter.

**No pre-registered constant is changed by this correction.** `LAMBDA_TOPO` stays `0.1` (the value actually fit under, sixteen times, and the value this plan's verdict is computed against). No fit is re-run. The measured `TOPOAE_VERDICT=FAIL` and its three gate values are unaffected -- this is a correction to a stated *justification* in `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md`'s prose, not to any measured quantity this plan or any prior plan produced. `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` itself is corrected by plan 02.4-08, not by this plan (this plan does not modify it -- confirmed by `git diff --quiet` throughout).

Grep-confirmed the withdrawn "minimum searched"/"log-uniform"/"paper's own bound" phrasing was never repeated in this plan's own code or printed output (`notebooks/diagnostics/topoae_evaluate_run.py`), so no in-plan propagation needed correcting here.
