- **Found during:** post-completion orchestrator verification of `744c1c1` against `notebooks/pu_manifold/topoae.py`'s actual training loop
- **Issue:** Section 1's `AMBIENT_DIST_NORM = "none"` prose read "identically at training time and at the T1 gate." That is false: `train_topoae` normalizes ambient distances by their own per-batch max (`d_x_norm = d_x / d_x.max()`) and latent distances by the jointly-trained `latent_norm` (`d_z_norm = d_z / latent_norm`) -- the paper's own convention, closed as fidelity gap #2 in plan 02.4-03's audit (commit `4b9b6c9`). Only the T1 gate uses raw, unnormalized `d_x`. Section 8's Limitation 3 already stated the correct, different picture; Section 1 contradicted it. Left standing, a runner reading Section 1 in isolation could train without `d_x / d_x.max()`, silently reintroducing the closed fidelity gap across ten hours of PU fits.
- **Fix:** Corrected Section 1 to scope `AMBIENT_DIST_NORM = "none"` explicitly to the T1 gate, name the training-side normalization as the separate, deliberate thing it is, and state why the two differ on purpose (a per-batch maximum has no single well-defined value over a whole held-out set; a per-model denominator at gate time would destroy the cross-model comparability the baseline-relative margins depend on). Added a matching clarification to Section 4's constants-block prose. Added Section 11, a dated erratum record naming the original text, the code that settles it, how it was found, and that no pre-registered constant or threshold moved.
