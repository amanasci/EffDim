<objective>
Compute the full classical-MDS eigenspectrum of the frozen Isomap fit by hand, and turn it into the
two pre-registered gate statistics.

Purpose: this is the phase's load-bearing claim and its one genuine architectural unknown —
everything downstream (residual curves, frozen `d`, verdict artifact, whether Phase 3 runs at all)
is arithmetic on the 10,000 eigenvalues of the double-centred geodesic matrix. The tracer proves the
memory-mapped load, in-place centring, and split eigensolve work at this scale before any expansion
touches it. `Isomap.kernel_pca_.eigenvalues_` is truncated to `n_components` by construction, so
reading it and reporting "no negative eigenvalues" is a false negative *by construction*
(PITFALLS Pitfall 3) — the only defence is computing the whole spectrum independently and asserting
its length is exactly 10,000.

Output: sections 6.0-6.4 of `notebooks/01_manifold_and_gate.ipynb`, cached
`mds_eigenspectrum_{fit_key}.npz`, bound statistics `R_STAT`/`M_STAT`, classifier `_gate_classify`,
and the two figures making the spectrum's shape visible rather than compressed into two numbers.
</objective>

- **d-axis sweep ceiling / K eigenvector ceiling:** `D_SWEEP_MAX = 40` — smallest round value more
  than twice `D_PROVISIONAL=18`, so the elbow candidate sits mid-range not at a boundary; top-40
  eigenvector block is ~3.2 MiB, well under D-11's <10MB budget; `eigh(subset_by_index=...)`
  computes only that band.
- **Equivalence-guard tolerance/size:** `EQUIV_N=50`, `EQUIV_TOL=1e-12` (both rtol/atol) on two
  different 50x50 inputs. Rounding error from the literal `J`-form's two matrix products is ~`n*eps`
  = `50*2.2e-16 = 1.1e-14`; `1e-12` leaves two orders of margin while staying far tighter than any
  genuine algebraic discrepancy (order 1). Two inputs (a real squared-Euclidean distance matrix from
  50 random R^5 points, and a symmetrised non-metric random matrix) because the identity
  `B = -0.5*J D2 J` is purely algebraic and must hold for any symmetric input.
- **SPEC-02's steep dropoff, distinct from SPEC-04's elbow:** dropoff index = `argmax` over `j` in
  `1..D_SWEEP_MAX-1` of `log(lambda_j) - log(lambda_{j+1})` — the largest single multiplicative gap
  — reported with that ratio on a log-scale scree plot. Deliberately different from the SPEC-04
  elbow (which reads a cumulative residual-variance curve): they can disagree by several dimensions
  on a smoothly decaying spectrum, and that disagreement is information, not a discrepancy to
  reconcile. Ties break to the lower index, consistent with Phase 1's `PLATEAU_TIE_BREAK="lower"`.
- **Section 6 sub-layout**, fixed so all three plans append without collision: `§6.0`-`§6.4` this
  plan (constants+ordering, D-03/05; release/map/centre, SPEC-01; eigensolve, SPEC-01; negativity
  stats+verdict, SPEC-03; leading spectrum+dropoff, SPEC-02); `§6.5`-`§6.6` plan 02-02 (residual
  curves+kneedle, freeze `d`, SPEC-04/05); `§6.7`-`§6.9` plan 02-03 (`gate_verdict.json`,

<assumption_delta_decision>

The detector fired one `chosen` signal: *"The **chosen** embedding dimension `d` is frozen and
recorded before any decoder is trained."* — a derived value is becoming a recorded constant.

**Decision: promote.** Phase 1's `n_components=18` (from the `compute_dim` geometric median,
baked into `fit_key`) is demoted to a fit-capacity detail — it only bounds how wide a slice can be
taken. From this phase onward, the dimension every downstream consumer means is `d_frozen` (recorded
in `gate_verdict_{fit_key}.json`), which follows from the SPEC-04 elbow. Phase 3's decoder input
width, the curvature field's coordinate space, CURV-08's support region, and Phase 4's region
partition all inherit `d_frozen`, not `n_components` — adding the new dimension alongside the old
(leaving downstream code reading `n_components`) is exactly the failure this checkpoint exists to
catch, since the two are equal in the common case and diverge silently in the case that matters.

**Invariant** (encoded in plan 02-02's §6.6 and plan 02-03's artifact contract): `d_frozen <=
n_components` always holds; every downstream consumer reads `d_frozen` from the verdict artifact.
An elbow above `n_components` cannot be satisfied by a slice — the notebook halts rather than
quietly capping `d_frozen` at 18. Advisory, does not block the phase.

</assumption_delta_decision>
