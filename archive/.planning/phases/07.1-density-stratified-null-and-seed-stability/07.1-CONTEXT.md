- **D7.1-01 — a density-stratified null for `partial_rho_density_controlled`.** Permute within

- **D7.1-02 — seed stability at `d=25`.** `d=25` (-0.06583, ~3.2x the edge) is the only cell that
  clears with room. Three fits at `d=25` alone (~2h) test whether it holds, replacing Phase 7's
  `SEED_HANDLING_RULE = "single_seed_across_d_sweep"` accepted limitation at that one cell.

**In scope:** the stratified null and its positive control; three `d=25` fits; the frozen

**Out of scope:** reinterpreting any sealed verdict from Phases 2, 02.x, 3, 03.1, 4, 5, 6 or 7;

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

Decision IDs are `D-NN`, local to this phase. They are distinct from the ROADMAP requirement IDs
`D7.1-01` / `D7.1-02`, and from Phase 7's `D7-NN` context decisions; each decision below names the
requirement it serves.

### Density stratum design (serves D7.1-01)

- **D-01: Strata are equal-count quantile bins on density RANK.** Not equal-width bins in
  log-density. Density spans p05=6.07e4 / p50=2.29e9 / p95=3.63e12 — a 7.8-order range that
  `07-FINDINGS.md` Sec 1.4 already flags as a diagnostic oddity — so rank bins give every stratum
  identical permutation entropy and keep the null on the same rank scale
  `cross_split_curvature.partial_spearman` already works in.

- **D-02: `S = 20` strata (500 points each) is the headline, gating stratum count.**
  Deliberately the middle of the defensible range. Finer strata make the test MORE sensitive, not
  more conservative — within-stratum permutation constrains how far a value can move, so the
  permuted partial varies less and the band narrows. `S=10` risks calling a real residual noise;
  `S=50` risks clearing a residual on stratification tightness alone. 500 points is far above the
  3-point floor `partial_spearman` enforces.

- **D-03: A non-gating threshold grid at `S in {10, 20, 50}` is reported alongside.** This is a
  grid of **thresholds, not of point estimates** — unlike `MKNN_K_GRID`, the observed
  `partial_rho_density_controlled` is computed with `controls=density` and never touches strata,
  so `S` moves only the null. Its purpose is to expose the D-02 artifact directly: a residual that
  clears at `S=50` but not at `S=10` cleared on tightness, not on curvature. Mirrors Phase 7's
  `SENSITIVITY_GRID_RULE` — the grid may qualify a reading, never overturn or escalate it. Costs
  3x the permutations.

- **D-04: The stratified null gets its own positive control.** No "not real" reading is licensed
  without it. Extend `crossmodal_curvature.plant_positive_control`'s bisection to target
  `partial_rho_density_controlled` and show the stratified test recovers a planted residual. D7-02
  validated the machinery of the **raw** statistic; the partial residualizes both arrays against
  density first, so an inherited detection floor would be an assumption, not a measurement.
  Without this, "d=20 does not clear" is uninterpretable in exactly the way Phase 7's D7-02 note
  warns about. — **Reversibility:** costly — dropping it later strips the license from every
  non-clearing reading in the phase, so the verdict text, FINDINGS and notebook all need rework.

### What the null permutes (serves D7.1-01)

- **D-05: `h` and `m` are each permuted independently within strata.** This mirrors how
  `scipy.stats.permutation_test(permutation_type="pairings")` builds Phase 7's raw null, so the two
  phases' nulls are constructed the same way. **Recorded trade-off, to be stated in FINDINGS:**
  because `h`'s residualization also varies per permutation, the band mixes both residualization
  sides and is NOT attributable to the MKNN link alone. The considered alternative — shuffling `m`
  only, holding `h`'s joint distribution with density exactly fixed — was not chosen.

- **D-06: A new stratified permutation routine is required.** `curvature_probe.permutation_null`
  cannot be reused: it delegates to `scipy.stats.permutation_test` with
  `permutation_type="pairings"`, which re-pairs globally and takes no stratum argument. The new
  routine lives in 07.1's own module. `mknn.permutation_null`'s hand-rolled loop is read for naming
  convention only, never copied — the same restraint Phase 7 exercised.

- **D-07: The observed partial is recomputed, then asserted equal to Phase 7's record.**
  Recompute from the frozen npz and assert against `notebooks/.cache/07_crossmodal_curvature.jsonl` at tight tolerance
  (-0.02419 at d=20, -0.06583 at d=25, -0.02172 at d=32). This proves the frozen field and the
  reload path are intact before any new number is trusted; a mismatch is a real finding, not a
  nuisance to paper over.

- **D-08: Phase 7's permutation constants are re-declared, not imported.**
  `N_PERMUTATIONS = 1000`, `NULL_QUANTILE_PER_TAIL = 0.975`, two-tailed — the same values, declared
  fresh in 07.1's own module. D7-05 seals
  `crossmodal_curvature.py`, and a gating constant imported across a freeze boundary is not
  covered by 07.1's own `assert_preregistered()` and git-ancestry proof. This is the D7-06
  freeze-before-any-number discipline applied to 07.1's own constants.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — the pre-registration contract is that gating constants are
  committed in source before the runner can produce a number, with git ancestry as the proof.
  Changing any of them after the first number exists breaks that contract and voids the verdict;
  the only remedy is a fresh freeze and a fresh run.

### Seed axis at d=25 (serves D7.1-02)

- **D-09: `TORCH_INIT_SEED` varies alone; `SPLIT_SEED = 20260813` is held fixed.** Line 157 of
  `07_crossmodal_curvature_run.py` calls `torch.manual_seed(cc.TORCH_INIT_SEED)`, which drives
  weight init and minibatch order. Holding the split fixed keeps training data identical across
  the three runs and isolates the fitted decoder curvature field — precisely what Phase 5 measured
  as unstable and what D7.1-02 exists to test.

- **D-10: Across the three runs, ONLY `h_norm` changes.** MKNN is computed between the `hsc` and
  `legacysurvey` ambient embeddings (`PU_COLUMN_A` / `PU_COLUMN_B`) and density on
  `DENSITY_INPUT = "legacysurvey_ambient_768"` — neither passes through the autoencoder, and
  density is also `d`-independent. So one stratification serves all three `d` and all three seeds.
  There is no per-seed edge array here: **Phase 5's `BUCKET_EDGES` -> `BUCKET_EDGES_PER_SEED`
  landmine does not recur in this phase**, and the plan should not invent machinery for it.

- **D-11: Combination rule is unanimity — all three seeds must clear.** Two-of-three is
  `SPLIT ACROSS SEEDS`, a terminal non-supportive outcome, not a claim. Directly inherits Phase
  5's ratified don't-pool decision (`05-03-DECISION.md`) and its `SPLIT ACROSS SEEDS` framing. On
  Phase 5's measured evidence a split is a live possibility, so this is the consequential call of
  the phase and it is made in the direction that refuses to overclaim.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — this is the same ratification Phase 5 escalated to a blocking
  developer checkpoint. Relaxing it after seeing the three numbers is post-hoc rule-fitting, and
  the only clean remedy would be re-running the seeds under a rule frozen in advance.

- **D-12: Each seed is gated on D7.1-01's stratified partial.** The raw statistic is reported
  alongside, non-gating. `d=25` was selected precisely because its density-controlled residual
  survives, so the partial is the load-bearing quantity. **Planning consequence: D7.1-02 depends on
  D7.1-01's null existing — they are separate waves, not parallel work.**
  — **Reversibility:** costly — undoing it re-orders the phase's waves and changes which
  deliverable can start first.

- **D-13: `PERMUTATION_SEED` is fixed at 20260825 across all three runs.** All three nulls draw
  identical permutation index sets, so any difference between the three thresholds comes from the
  curvature field alone — the one thing D7.1-02 measures. Varying it would make field instability
  and null-sampling noise inseparable in a 3-point comparison.

### Verdict rules (serve both requirements)

- **D-14: Two independent verdicts, one per deliverable.** D7.1-01 gets a verdict over
  `d in {20, 25, 32}` on the stratified partial; D7.1-02 gets a separate verdict over the three
  seeds at `d=25`. Each deliverable answers its own question and can succeed or fail alone, which
  matters because D7.1-02 is gated on D7.1-01 (D-12) and a null there must not silently void the
  seed work. Phase 7's `apply_verdict` cannot be reused for either: it raises unless
  `per_d_results` keys are exactly `set(D_SWEEP)`, and its two-parameter signature structurally
  cannot accept a density number.

- **D-15: D7.1-01 mints a distinct verdict value for subset survival.** The per-`d` clearance
  table is always reported. The phase's most likely outcome — `d=25` clears, `d=20` and `d=32` do not — is
  a genuine finding (most of Phase 7's association was density everywhere except `d=25`), and
  Phase 7's `SPLIT ACROSS d` vocabulary would label it identically to a failure. Name it
  separately, e.g. `SURVIVES AT SUBSET OF d`. The per-`d` table is reported unconditionally
  regardless of which verdict fires, because the goal names individual residuals to adjudicate.

- **D-16: `DIAGNOSTICS_ARE_NON_GATING` stays structural, not promissory.** Whatever verdict
  functions 07.1 mints, keep Phase 7's property that the function's signature cannot accept a
  quantity it is not allowed to gate on.

### Phase 7 review debt

- **D-17: WR-01, WR-02 and WR-04 are all folded into this phase.** WR-03 is already closed by

  - **WR-01** — `crossmodal_curvature.density_diagnostics` (lines 649-733) has no finite/constant

  - **WR-04** — all 157 tests in `test_crossmodal_curvature.py` exercise pure compute functions;
    none imports the runner, which is exactly where CR-01..CR-03 all lived. 07.1 adds a second
    runner, compounding the gap. Add a runner test module covering `resolve_record_path`,
    `is_scratch` detection, and the argv parsing.

- **D-18: WR-01 and WR-02 are amended in place, under a no-op proof.** The plan must DEMONSTRATE
  each amendment is a no-op on the realized record, not assert it. Both fixes are behaviour-
  preserving on Phase 7's actual path — WR-01's guards fire only on non-finite or constant input
  and Phase 7's run produced finite numbers; WR-02's two implementations currently agree
  numerically — but the phase proves this rather than asserting it: re-run `--mode smoke` and
  assert the distinct count is unchanged, and unit-test that the new guards do not fire on Phase
  7's actual arrays. Precedent for post-seal amendment: `c92260f` hardened CR-01..CR-03 in-phase,
  and Phase 6 amended its own record serializer.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — D7-05 published these files as sealed ("import, never edit"), and
  editing them after the record was produced means the committed record was not generated by the
  current source. The no-op proof is what keeps that defensible; without it the change cannot
  simply be reverted out of the record's provenance story.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The `S` grid is a grid of **thresholds**, not point estimates — the observed partial is invariant
  to `S`. This is the one place 07.1's sensitivity reporting differs in kind from Phase 7's
  `MKNN_K_GRID`, and the write-up should say so rather than letting a reader assume the analogy.
- `rankdata` is equivariant under permutation, so `rank(m_perm) == rank(m)[perm]`. The rank vectors
  of `h`, `m` and `density` can each be computed once and indexed per permutation instead of
  recomputed — worth taking, given 1000 permutations x 3 `d` x 3 grid points.
- The `n=800` Betti draw-2 detail from `07-CONTEXT.md` §5 (max life exactly equal to its band,
  failing strict `>`) is the house style for how a marginal number gets reported: name it, do not
  round it away. The `d=20` and `d=32` residuals deserve the same treatment whichever way they land.

</specifics>
