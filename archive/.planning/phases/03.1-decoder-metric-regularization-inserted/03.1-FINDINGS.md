# Phase 03.1 Findings — decoder metric regularization

**Date:** 2026-08-21. **Milestone:** v1.1 PU Manifold Curvature. **Phase:**
03.1-decoder-metric-regularization-inserted.

**One-line outcome. The metric was fixed and the curvature estimate was not.** `scale` demonstrably
drove its own target quantity to its optimum — `log10_det_g` from `-83.9` to `+0.037`, i.e.
geometric-mean metric eigenvalue `~1.004` — at *negative* reconstruction cost. The estimand moved
from `rho -0.122` to `+0.116` and stopped. **This is not a null; it is necessary but not
sufficient.** Regularizing the decoder's parameterization repairs the metric completely and does
not recover ordering at `d=20`. That distinction exists only because Tier 1 (mechanism) and Tier 2
(estimand) were scored separately, per D-08, and it is this phase's main result. **Phase 4 stays
blocked, with no proposed route out.**

---

## 1. What this phase measured, and what it did not

- **The `d=20` saddle only** (D-01). It is the sole synthetic control that scores all four Tier-2
  axes — its analytic `H` is varying and mixed-sign, so `rho` and `R²` are defined, where the
  sphere's constant `H` leaves both undefined by construction (`03-FINDINGS.md` §6). The sphere
  was **deliberately not run**; it is the first candidate extension if the saddle moves, since a
  constant analytic `H` would make flattening bias unusually readable.
- **No PU fit.** The roadmap states "PU comes after, if and only if a control clears"
  (`.planning/ROADMAP.md` § Phase 03.1). The saddle control did not clear on the estimand (§5, §10
  below), so no PU fit was run and none was in scope regardless.
- **No sealed verdict reopened.** The phase-2 stage stays on hold; no Phase 2/02.1/02.2/02.4/02.5
  number is touched, softened, or reinterpreted.
- **`cae.py` not edited** — verified directly: `git diff --quiet notebooks/pu_manifold/cae.py`
  exits 0. Also unmodified: `notebooks/pu_manifold/decoder_priors.py`,
  `notebooks/pu_manifold/chart_curvature.py` (edited only for D-15/CURV-04's additive fields, not
  for this plan), `notebooks/diagnostics/synthetic_control_run.py`,
  `notebooks/diagnostics/swiss_roll_isometry_prior_sweep_run.py`.
- **Two priors measured, separately and in one post-hoc combination — never collapsed into one
  weight (D-02, D-12).** `scale` (`(log det g / d)²`, diverges under collapse) targets the
  uniform-collapse pathology seeds 20260814/20260815 exhibited in `03-FINDINGS.md` §5; `christoffel`
  (penalizes the tangential part of `D²F`, exactly invariant under uniform rescaling) targets the
  anisotropy seed 20260813 exhibited (`λ_min = 1.017e-07` at a healthy `λ_max = 3.352`).
- **Context this phase inherits, not re-measures:** at `chart_dim = 4` the identical fixture and
  pipeline recover `rho = 0.989`, `R² = 0.980` (`03-FINDINGS.md` §6). The `d=20` gap this phase
  works against is the fit's, not the estimand's — nothing here revisits that finding, it is the
  reason the saddle was chosen as the test bed.

## 9. Carried limitations, stated rather than hidden

1. **The 20-vs-300 epoch gap threatens the headline directly.** This phase's `weight=0` baselines
   (20 epochs) measure `log10_det_g` median `≈ -83.9`; the sealed 300-epoch fit measured `-22.78`
   (§2). The 20-epoch baseline is far more collapsed than the fit Phase 3 actually diagnosed, so a
   meaningful share of what `scale` appears to repair is undertraining rather than the Phase 3
   pathology at its true severity. This is the single biggest threat to the headline, first-class,
   not a footnote.
2. **F1's relief valve cost `christoffel` its own answer.** Dropping the middle rung removed
   exactly the resolution needed to locate where `christoffel`'s `cond(g)` bottoms out: the weak
   rung alone (`cond=1.047e+04`) would have passed a plain top-rung-vs-baseline rule outright, but
   the declared rule reads the *top* rung, whose `cond(g)` rose back to `1.380e+05`. `christoffel`
   has a useful weight somewhere in this ladder and F1 made it unreadable from the top-rung rule
   alone — the combination cell's `cond(g)` halving (§6) is independent evidence of this.
3. **`christoffel` cost 21–22% held-out reconstruction** (§5); under D-09's stated rule this is
   independent of the Tier-1 verdict and disqualifies it as an improvement on its own.
4. **`eval_n = 1000` (D-16 + F2), not the sealed row's full 10,000-row cloud.** Ladder and
   combination cells are **not cell-for-cell comparable** to the sealed `d=20` full-cloud row on
   evaluation-set size; `eval_sample_size` is recorded on every cell so a full-cloud
   re-evaluation is well defined.
5. **Three seeds cannot separate a small real effect from seed noise (D-03).** `scale`'s direction
   cosine and calibration slope/`R²` movements are `NOT SEED-CONSISTENT` and are not claimed as
   effects even though their medians moved toward target; the combination cell's tight variance
   (§6) is an observation, explicitly not a finding, for the same reason.
6. **The fixture has anisotropy, not uniform collapse** (`λ_max = 174` healthy beside
   `λ_min = 9.4e-07`, §2). `scale` nonetheless produced the mechanism win — the opposite of what
   an arm-to-pathology mapping keyed on uniform collapse would have predicted. Recorded as a
   finding about the reading rule, not a contradiction: `scale`'s penalty diverges on the
   determinant regardless of which eigenvalue is small, so it can repair an anisotropic collapse
