<the_selection_rule_declared_before_the_ladder_ran>

From `03.1-01-PLAN.md` §F, encoded in source and printed by `--dry-run` before any cell ran.
**Not one word of it may change now that the ladder's numbers exist.**

- `best_scale` = the rung whose median `log10_det_g` is closest to `0.0` — the quantity `scale`'s
  own penalty `(log det g / d)²` minimizes.
- `best_christoffel` = the rung with the lowest median `cond(g)` among rungs whose median
  `λ_max` did not fall by at least one decade from the `weight=0` cell — the same `COLLAPSE`
  guard as Tier 1.
- If a mode's Tier 1 is `MECHANISM NOT DEMONSTRATED` at every rung, its best is the largest
  weight rung and the combination cell carries the extra label `no mechanism in the {mode} arm`.
- The bias guard is a reading rule, never a selector: **no rule above selects on held-out
  reconstruction** (D-09).

**Every table carrying the combination cell labels it**
`POST-HOC (weights selected after seeing the ladders; not an independent measurement)`.
