- `scale`'s Tier-1 verdict: **MECHANISM DEMONSTRATED**. - Bias guard (D-09), read but never a selector: `scale`'s held-out reconstruction *improved* against `weight=0` (`0.9653`, `0.9809` ratio at the two rungs); `christoffel`'s got materially *worse* (`1.2141`, `1.2217` — 22% worse at its own best-cond rung).
- Tier 2 for `scale` (read as evidence, since Tier 1 fired): magnitude ratio moved from `77464` toward `11.28`; rank rho moved from `-0.1216` toward `+0.1164`; direction cosine and calibration R2 both moved toward their targets but were `NOT SEED-CONSISTENT`. Christoffel's Tier 2 is printed in full per D-10 but is explicitly labelled `not read as evidence about the estimand`.

## Carried Limitations (surfaced by this plan's own numbers, read into `03.1-05`)

1. **The 20-vs-300 epoch gap threatens the headline directly.** This phase's `weight=0` baselines (`max_epochs=20`) measure `log10_det_g` median `≈ -83.9`; the sealed 300-epoch fit `control_saddle_nc4_d20_ep300` measured `-22.78` (`03.1-03`'s anchor cell). The 20-epoch baseline is **far more collapsed** than the fit Phase 3 actually diagnosed. A meaningful share of what `scale` appears to repair is undertraining, not necessarily the Phase 3 pathology at its real severity. This is the single biggest threat to reading `scale`'s Tier-1 win as evidence about the sealed fit's own collapse.
2. **F1's relief valve cost christoffel its own answer.** Dropping the middle rung (3 rungs -> 2, keeping only the extremes `0.01` and `1.0` of the declared fractions) removed exactly the resolution needed to locate where `christoffel`'s `cond(g)` peaks: the weak rung alone (`cond=1.047e+04`) would have passed the declared rule outright, but the rule reads the *top* rung, and the top rung's `cond` rose back to `1.380e+05`. `christoffel` has a useful weight somewhere in this ladder and F1 made it unreadable from the top-rung rule alone.
3. **`christoffel` bought its Tier-2 movement with materially worse reconstruction** — held-out `mse_per_dim` ratio `1.2141`/`1.2217` against `weight=0`, a 21-22% cost. Under D-09's stated rule ("an improvement bought with materially worse reconstruction is not an improvement") this is independent of the Tier-1 verdict and stands on its own.
4. **`eval_n=1000` (D-16 + F2), not the sealed row's full 10,000-row cloud.** Ladder cells are not cell-for-cell comparable to the sealed `d=20` full-cloud row on evaluation-set size. `eval_sample_size` is recorded on every cell so a full-cloud re-evaluation is well defined.
5. **Three seeds cannot separate a small real effect from seed noise (D-03).** `scale`'s direction-cosine and calibration-R2 movements are both labelled `NOT SEED-CONSISTENT` in the Tier-2 read-out and must not be claimed as effects, even though the medians moved toward target.
6. **The fixture has anisotropy, not uniform collapse** — the anchor's own healthy `lambda_max=174` sits beside a collapsed `lambda_min≈9.4e-07` in the sealed fit's absolute-scale reading. `scale` nonetheless produced the mechanism win on this fixture, the opposite of what an arm-to-pathology mapping keyed on uniform collapse would have predicted — worth carrying forward as a reading note, not a contradiction.
7. **The probe-truncation defect** found and fixed in `03.1-03` is confirmed, not refuted, by this plan's clean run: no cell in this record shows the truncated-eval-size symptom, and A2 (evaluation cost linear in row count) held.
8. **The mixed-source multiplier caveat** from the second probe run (`03.1-03`) still applies to any wall-clock comparison against the probe's own per-mode multipliers; this plan's own measured 5.4h total is the authoritative number for this run, not a re-derivation from the probe's multipliers.
9. **15 cells, not the dry-run's originally stated 18** — the shared-baseline cost-model correction above.
10. **D-07's fence held.** No cell in `notebooks/.cache/03.1_decoder_prior_ladder.jsonl` carries the F4 override label; every cell has `epochs_run == 20 == max_epochs` and `early_stopped == False`. Zero diverged cells (`nonfinite_guard.tripped` is `False` on all 15).
