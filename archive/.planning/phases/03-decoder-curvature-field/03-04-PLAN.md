<objective>
Build the three known-geometry synthetic control fixtures — flat plane, sphere, saddle — at PU's
actual working scale (`d = 20`, `D = 768`), as a new phase-scoped module that imports the sealed
02.5 curvature code and never edits it.

These fixtures are step 4's ground truth. Two of them (flat, sphere) are direct parameter
generalizations of constructions already tested at small scale. The third (saddle) is genuinely new
and is flagged `[ASSUMED]` by `03-RESEARCH.md` — it has no existing regression test anywhere, so it
gets a finite-difference cross-check before it is trusted as ground truth.

The saddle earns its place by testing the one case neither of the others reaches: a point where
`||H||` is small because positive and negative principal curvatures cancel in the trace, as opposed
to a point where `||H||` is small because the differential is nearly rank-deficient. Those produce
the same symptom for opposite reasons, and only `cond(g)` reported alongside `||H||` tells them apart.

**What these controls cannot do, stated here and carried into every artifact that quotes them.** A
synthetic manifold sampled cleanly and fitted under this protocol trains to a clean, unfragmented
atlas. It has none of the pathology the phase's gate override is worried about — `02.5-09`'s
chart-count-driven fragmentation, `max cond(g)` climbing from `3.26` to `122.22`, second derivatives
banding along artificial chart seams. A control that passes therefore establishes only that the
decoder-curvature *pipeline* is correct on a manifold that is easy to fit. It is necessary, not
sufficient, and it cannot rule out parameterization damage on PU.

Purpose: give step 4 a ground truth that is verified rather than assumed.
Output: `synthetic_controls.py` plus its test file, closing the last Wave 0 test gap.
</objective>
