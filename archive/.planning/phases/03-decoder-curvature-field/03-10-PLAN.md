<objective>
Fit the same architecture, under the same protocol, to three manifolds whose curvature is known in
closed form, and state what the PU numbers can and cannot mean.

This is ROADMAP step 4, and the ROADMAP requires it complete and reported **before** Phase 4 starts.
Its honest scope is narrow and must be stated where the numbers are, not in a footnote: a synthetic
manifold sampled cleanly and fitted under this protocol trains to a clean, unfragmented atlas. It has
none of the pathology the gate override is worried about — `02.5-09`'s chart-count-driven
fragmentation, `max cond(g)` climbing from `3.26` to `122.22`, second derivatives banding along
artificial chart seams. So a control that passes establishes that the decoder-curvature *pipeline* is
correct on an easy manifold. That is necessary and not sufficient, and it cannot rule out
parameterization damage on PU.

What the controls can do is real. The flat plane's analytic `||H||` is exactly zero, so any apparent
curvature there is entirely artifact and its magnitude is a direct read on the pipeline's noise
floor. The sphere's is exactly `d / R`, large and unambiguous. The saddle's varies in space with
positive and negative principal curvatures mixing, which is the only fixture that exercises the
near-cancellation case `cond(g)` exists to disambiguate.

Purpose: bound the interpretation of the PU field, and say plainly where that bound stops.
Output: three matched fits, one four-axis comparison table per fixture, one printed caveat.
</objective>
