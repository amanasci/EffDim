<objective>
Deliver the Swiss roll sanity notebook CLAUDE.md mandates for every new manifold model.

This phase ships a new differentiation path through the chart-decoder curvature field, so the
standing rule applies: the model is not done until its Swiss roll notebook exists and passes. Its
purpose is diagnostic separation — a FAIL on real data has two possible causes, the data having no
structure or the implementation being broken, and only a manifold whose answer is known in advance
tells them apart.

**This is a distinct deliverable from the phase's Step-1 gate, and the two must not merge.** The gate
is a 4-config by 5-seed sweep living in `notebooks/diagnostics/swiss_roll_curvature_sweep_run.py`
(plans 03-01 and 03-02). This notebook is single-seed, `chart_dim = 2`, at most about fifteen cells,
under two minutes on CPU, with no gate machinery of any kind. A notebook that grows past that has
drifted from its purpose. Conversely the gate does not excuse the notebook: CLAUDE.md's rule is
unconditional per new model.

Purpose: a cheap, readable, re-runnable check that the shipped code recovers a known answer.
Output: one committed, executed notebook.
