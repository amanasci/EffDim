<objective>
Build the PU sweep runner: three `n_charts` values by three seeds, nine fresh CAE fits at
`chart_dim = 20`, `D = 768`, each carrying the four model-side diagnostics D-07 selects on.

Nothing here needs ground truth. PU has no analytic `H`, so there is no gate on this side — only a
diagnostics table to read. This plan builds and proves the instrument; plan 03-08 runs the grid.

Two things are settled in this plan's source before any PU number exists. **The working dimension:**
`chart_dim = 20`, justified in the runner's own docstring rather than inherited, against TwoNN 19.5,
local-PCA median 25.0 (mean 24.5, std 2.0, 5-95% range 21-28, no neighbourhood above 29), and a
median of 18 across Phase 1's eight geometric estimators. `d_frozen = 5` is rejected with its reason
recorded: at 41% negative eigenvalue mass the Tenenbaum residual curve saturates early, so the elbow
measured the flatness failure, not the geometry. **The selection rule:** a printed table plus a
stated lexicographic order, written down before the grid runs, for the same reason the roll floor was.

Costs are unmeasured at this scale. 02.2's timing probe recorded 1,941.2 s per PU fit for training
alone, and curvature at `d = 20, D = 768` has never been timed anywhere in this milestone. The runner
measures both before committing the grid.

Purpose: an instrument whose choices are on the record before its numbers are.
Output: `notebooks/diagnostics/curvature_field_pu_run.py`, verified by `--dry-run`, `--smoke` and a
timing probe.
</objective>
