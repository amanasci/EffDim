# Phase 02.1 Plan 4: Recommendation, Working Dimension, and Phase 3 Handoff

**GEOM-04 = Ollivier-Ricci discrete curvature on the frozen k\*=15 geodesic graph.** Retention
stance: **retain**. Working dimension: **none — inapplicable on this branch**; `D_FROZEN = 5`
**discarded as inapplicable**, not declared wrong.

## The representation and why

Per-edge `κ(x,y) = 1 − W₁(m_x, m_y)/d(x,y)` on the validated k\*=15 graph, with Forman-Ricci as a
cheap combinatorial cross-check. No embedding, no coordinates, no flat target — which is the point:
four structurally unrelated algorithm families wall at ~0.0796 distortion, and metric SMACOF
reaching that floor with no eigendecomposition and no PSD constraint shows the binding constraint is
flatness of the target space, not the algorithm.

Retention survives the branch switch unchanged and is honoured more strictly than any coordinate
representation could: the graph-native branch never forms `B`, never eigendecomposes it, and never
asks the metric to be PSD.

## Working dimension and the D_FROZEN disposition

Ollivier-Ricci assigns a scalar to each of 150,000 edges. There is no coordinate vector and no
target space, so the GEOM-05 question dissolves rather than receiving a different number. The
pre-registered prohibition forbids *declaring* `D_FROZEN = 5` wrong or *inheriting* it silently;
neither happens.

Three scale parameters replace dimension: the frozen graph (`k* = 15`, 150,000 edges, mandatory
reuse), the random-walk laziness α (must be pre-registered before any curvature number), and the
Sinkhorn regularisation ε (with a bias sweep at ≥3 values).

The coordinate branch's re-derivation is preserved — `(p,q) = (8,0)` under the verbatim kneedle
criterion, `classical_p = 8`, corroborated by Phase 2's own eigenvalue-based elbow of 8 — so
rejecting Amendment 02 costs no re-derivation.

## Alternatives rejected, one line each

- **Pseudo-Euclidean / Krein retention** — won the pre-registered criterion (0.065190, 18.4% below
  the q=0 best) and is still the only measured arm below the wall; rejected by user directive and
  because the advantage is signature-carried and worth only +1.44%/+0.10% at an actual decoder.
- **Metric SMACOF** — 0.079605, the best decoder-consumable arm, rejected because it *is* the wall:
  it reproduces by a different route the classical result Phase 2 gated as FAIL.
- **Hyperbolic (Poincaré/Lorentz)** — `delta_rel_max = 0.383921` sits past the flat anchor 0.360433
  against a 0.036043 threshold; less tree-like than flat space, so constant negative curvature is
  contradicted.
- **Mixed-curvature product** — same delta evidence per factor, plus unresolved factor/signature
  model selection; never shortlisted.
- **Ambient Riemannian via geomstats** — 0.769597 / 0.780607, an order of magnitude off; both arms
  measure ambient sphere geometry, ignoring the ~20–25-d manifold inside S^767. Carries a numpy-2
  ImportError defect as a dependency.
- **Laplacian eigenmaps / LLE / non-metric MDS** — 0.279918 / 0.420736 / 0.488891 even after
  optimal per-arm rescaling; structural properties of the objectives.
- **Diffusion maps** — never measured, so recommending it would violate the evidence-strength
  prohibition; most plausible coordinate-producing fallback, but a flat-target method that SMACOF
  predicts hits the same wall.

**Forman-Ricci is not rejected** — retained as the cross-check on the recommended branch.

## Falsifier status

**Fired.** Condition (a) trips wide (`delta_rel_max = 0.383921` past a 0.360433 flat anchor,
threshold 0.036043). Condition (b) trips under `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md`'s amended reading — requiring
the ladder's drop be realisable in a decoder-consumable form — on three legs: the direct decoder
measurement (+1.44%/+0.10% of a promised +18.37%), the matched-width signature control (−2.07%/
−3.61%, negative in both preprocessings), and the absence of any consumable rung below the ~0.0796
wall.

Both trip → the coordinate-producing verdict is overturned toward graph-native, which is the
pre-registration's own stated consequence and the path `02.1-FORK.md` named in advance.

Under the *un-amended* reading (b) does not trip and the coordinate verdict stands with Krein as its
winner. That reading is fully recoverable; `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` §6 states how to discount the
amendment.

## Deviations

- **Working dimension is not a `(p,q)` pair.** The plan expected one; the branch has no embedding
  dimension. Disposition made explicit and the coordinate-branch pair preserved.
- **The handoff gained a fourth `dropped` requirements bucket.** Nine of thirteen DEC/CURV
  requirements are inapplicable rather than modified; forcing them into `rewrite` would misreport
  nine dropped requirements as nine rewritten ones. `working_dimension.applicable = false` carries
  the branch's real scale parameters.
- **`geometry_handoff.py` had to be restored before it could be rewritten** — deleted by quick task
  `260801-ovf` (`8958488`) during the phase's open checkpoint.
- **The superseded Krein handoff was archived, not overwritten.** `json_cache`'s manifest guard
  refused the silent overwrite exactly as T-02.1-12 intended; the Krein artifact is kept at
  `*.superseded-krein.json`.
- **Two extra artifacts beyond plan scope**, both user-directed: the Swiss roll notebook and the
  decoder check with its pre-registration.
- **A bug in the decoder runner's first pass**, found and fixed before any result was recorded:
  `geo_pairs_r2` holds geodesic distances, not squared distances, and passing them unsquared
  inflated every distortion number ~4×. The script now reproduces all three published ladder rungs
  and halts if it cannot. MSE never touched the pair sample and was unaffected.

## Flagged for re-opening

**The evaluation criterion itself.** The decoder check surfaced, as an unplanned observation, that
held-out reconstruction was nearly decoupled from the distance-distortion statistic this phase
ranked representations by: classical (40,0), worst distortion of the three ladder rungs at 0.179641,
reconstructed *best* of all four arms on both preprocessings. Distortion spanned 2.75×; MSE spanned
~6%.

One seed was run, so few-percent gaps cannot be separated from initialisation noise and decoder
capacity saturation is not excluded — an observation, not a verdict. But if a distance statistic
does not predict what a decoder does with a representation, then ranking by it is weaker evidence
than this phase treated it as, in **both** directions. `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` §6.4 records this as
the strongest reason to doubt the amendment, and §6.5 names the seed-sensitivity study that would
settle it. **Not run.** Any phase inheriting this recommendation should know the criterion that
selected its predecessor may not have measured the right thing.
