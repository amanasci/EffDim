unchanged on a Swiss roll — developable, so its geodesic metric genuinely *is* Euclidean-embeddable
and the answer is known in advance — under Phase 2's own pre-registered bounds:

matches sklearn's Isomap to 1.8e-13; the roll unrolls with `|corr(t, coord₀)| = 0.992`. The 41%
negative mass is a property of the embedding data, not an artifact of the probe machinery.

promised, with the matched-width signature control negative in both preprocessings.

## Caveats carried forward

  unplanned observation, that held-out reconstruction was nearly decoupled from the
  distance-distortion statistic this phase ranked by: classical `(40,0)`, worst distortion of the
  three ladder rungs, reconstructed best of all four arms on both preprocessings. One seed, so
  few-percent gaps are not separable from initialisation noise and capacity saturation is not
  excluded — an observation, not a verdict. `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` §6.4 records it as the strongest
  reason to doubt the amendment; §6.5 names the seed-sensitivity study that would settle it.
  **Not run.**
- **Amendment 02 changed a falsifier after the deciding evidence existed.** §6 states how to
  discount it; the un-amended reading and the original argument are both fully recoverable
  (`59742af`, `11de2f6`).

  confirmation. Only the part both readings agree on is used.

  sweep is not run.
- **The continuum-limit claim (arXiv:2307.02378) is [CITED]-strength, unread.** The recommendation
  does not lean on it; any plan that does must read the paper first.
- **`02.1-SECURITY.md` not produced.** The threat register (T-02.1-SC, T-02.1-02, -04, -07, -10, -11,
