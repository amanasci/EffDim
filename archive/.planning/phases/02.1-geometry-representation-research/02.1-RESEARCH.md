# Phase 02.1: Geometry Representation Research - Research

**Researched:** 2026-07-31 · **Domain:** non-Euclidean representation + curvature estimation for a
~20-25-dim manifold with 41% negative classical-MDS eigenvalue mass · **Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Summary

Phase 2 established (m=0.412071, robust to k, column, resample, normalization) that classical MDS
cannot describe this geodesic geometry. Well-studied failure mode: corrections branch (Cailliez
1983, Lingoes 1971), representation branch (hyperbolic 2017-19, mixed-curvature 2019, Neuc-MDS
NeurIPS 2024).

**Finding 1 (highest-leverage, § Entanglement):** the terrain splits on whether a candidate
produces coordinates Phase 3 can decode from. Coordinate-producing (hyperbolic/Lorentz,
mixed-curvature product, pseudo-Euclidean/Krein, diffusion maps) keep the DEC/CURV requirement
shape. Graph-native (Ollivier-Ricci, Forman-Ricci) produce no coordinates, give Phase 3 nothing to
differentiate, and compute *intrinsic* Ricci rather than the *extrinsic* mean-curvature vector
CURV-01..03 ask for — choosing them re-opens Phase 3's requirements.

**Finding 2:** flat-target methods do not share Isomap's diagnostic. LLE/Hessian-LLE/LTSA/
Laplacian-eigenmaps/MVU are PSD-constrained by construction; t-SNE/UMAP are probability-based —
none can produce a negative-eigenvalue statistic. A clean UMAP/t-SNE plot = the failure made
invisible, not absent.

**Finding 3 (bears on GEOM-05):** Cailliez/Lingoes/clipping succeed on *any* input —
diagnostic-blind by construction. Neuc-MDS (2024) is the literature's articulated alternative:
keep both signs, work in a pseudo-Euclidean/Krein bilinear-form space.

**Scoping recommendation (not a GEOM-04 foreclosure):** compare at most three candidates — one
mixed-curvature/product embedding (pure hyperbolic's constant-curvature assumption is likely wrong
for heterogeneous morphology), pseudo-Euclidean/Krein retention (split already computed), one
graph-native cross-check (Ollivier-Ricci on the k*=15 graph, Sinkhorn) — against one pre-registered
criterion.

## Architecture Patterns

**The fork:** frozen dist_matrix_/graph/spectrum → DECISION coordinate-producing vs graph-native.
Coordinate path: fit embedding from dist_matrix_ → native diagnostic (stress/distortion/mAP/delta)
→ coordinates + working dimension → Phase 3 C2 decoder → torch.func → mean-curvature VECTOR →
Phase 4. Graph-native path: per-node/edge Ricci on the k*=15 graph, no embedding → incompatible
with CURV-01..03 as specified → re-opens DEC/CURV. The evaluation criterion must be chosen before
the fork's candidates are compared.

**Pattern 1 — diagnostic-blind corrections vs signal-retaining representations:**
Cailliez/Lingoes/clipping force PSD on any input; flipping and Krein retention keep the negatives
as information. Corrections are legitimate only as a labelled contrast baseline. (No canonical
Python implementation; R's `ape::pcoa`/`vegan::wcmdscale` implement it. Cailliez's constant needs
a 2n×2n eigenproblem.)

**Anti-patterns:** clean UMAP/t-SNE as resolution; deriving "the" dimension once globally before
picking a representation; graph-native curvature on a freshly built graph instead of k*=15;
choosing the criterion after seeing which candidate looks best (Phase 2's pre-registration
discipline applies).

## Don't Hand-Roll

| Problem | Don't build | Use | Why |
|---------|-------------|-----|-----|
| Ollivier/Forman-Ricci at n=10,000 | per-edge OT loop | GraphRicciCurvature (Sinkhorn) | exact OT is an LP with d_x·d_y variables per edge; intractable at 150,000 edges without entropic regularization, which is easy to get wrong |
| Riemannian optimization | custom Poincare exp/log + RSGD | geoopt | numerically delicate near the ball boundary; silent NaN bugs |
| Diffusion-map bandwidth + stabilisation | naive kernel + eigh | datafold | wrong bandwidth silently changes what diffusion distance means |
| Gromov delta at scale | exhaustive quadruples (C(10000,4)) | **hand-rolled sampled estimator** | the one item where hand-rolling is right — but sampling is the part with a documented wrong way (literature uses hundreds-to-thousands of quadruples) |

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Method choice masquerading as a class conclusion** — establish which other tools share the
   assumption and would fail invisibly; "doesn't show negative eigenvalues" ≠ "doesn't have them".
2. **Correction-as-validation** — a 100% pass rate across corrections is what corrections
   guarantee, not evidence about the data.
3. **Single constant curvature on heterogeneous data** — pure Poincare (Nickel & Kiela 2017/18) is
   the heavily-tooled default; prefer product/mixed-curvature (Gu et al., ICLR 2019) or check
   whether the negative mass pattern is consistent with one constant curvature.
4. **Graph-native curvature on the wrong graph/k** — export the k*=15 graph from the cached fit;
   rebuilding at another k re-opens the closed k-sensitivity question. Warning sign: curvature
   stats that don't reproduce ISO-01's connectivity facts.

## Open Questions

1. **Does Ollivier-Ricci continuum-limit convergence hold here?** Conditions unclear for a
   ~20-25-dim manifold at n=10,000 (~400-500 points per intrinsic dimension). Read
   arXiv:2307.02378 directly; [CITED]-strength at best.
2. **Is one evaluation criterion fair across the fork?** Distortion is undefined for graph-native
   candidates (they don't attempt distance preservation). Decide explicitly and pre-register.
3. **The model was never varied** (DINOv3 ViT-B/16 only). The 35-model sweep could change how
   general the recommendation should claim to be — GEOM-04 must state architecture-specific vs
   expected-to-generalise, matching FINDINGS §8.

## Entanglement

1. **Representation ↔ Phase 3 viability** (largest): coordinate-producing re-specifies the input;
   graph-native re-opens the requirements. Decide the fork first — it gates everything.
2. **Representation ↔ working dimension:** curved targets can need fewer dims (striking datum:
   hyperbolic MAP=0.989 in 2 dims where 200-dim Euclidean did worse). Per-candidate curves;
   representation + dimension + criterion are one joint decision.
3. **Criterion ↔ representation:** metrics not natively comparable across families; fix the
   criterion before shortlisting or narrow the shortlist to families sharing one.
4. **GEOM-03 ↔ GEOM-04:** recommending Krein IS choosing retention — the two answer each other.
5. **Compute ↔ graph k:** Ollivier-Ricci cost scales with edges; reuse k*=15 (150,000 edges) —
   cheaper and avoids re-litigating k-sensitivity.
