## The Finding

**The chart decoder recovers curvature's direction and loses its amplitude entirely.** Median cosine similarity 0.9706 says `H_chart` points very nearly the right way. Median magnitude ratio 0.3356 at CV 0.7888 says its size is roughly a third of the truth and scattered; calibration slope **-0.1856** with R² 0.0533 says its size carries essentially *no* information about the true size — a rank statistic cannot see this, and here the rank statistic collapsed too.

**This is not a non-immersion artifact.** The maximum metric condition number over all 3,000 points is 63.19 (median 1.79). The learned map is a genuine immersion everywhere, so its curvature is meaningful; the learned manifold simply is not curved the way the roll is.

**The mechanism is visible in the plots.** The reconstruction, though numerically excellent, is fragmented: the x-z panel shows the spiral broken into chart-sized pieces joined by near-straight chords, with visible gaps. `||H_chart||` then bands along those chart pieces rather than along the spiral, and is brightest at the *outer* arc where the analytic field is darkest. The identity scatter shows smooth festoon-shaped arcs — within a chart the field varies smoothly, but oscillates up and down as the true value increases, and collapses to ~0.2 wherever the analytic `||H||` exceeds 1.25.

**The same fragmentation is present in the sealed 02.2 reference notebook's reconstruction figure**, which passed at 4.8% relative error and is this milestone's one clean CAE success. So this run is not an anomalously broken setup — it reproduces the reference model's known behaviour at a different fixture seed (5.2% vs 4.8%). What the finding says is that the MSE criterion 02.2 passed on was never sensitive to the piecewise-flat-with-joins geometry that destroys second derivatives.

(raw-point baseline `rho = 0.6712`, seed-independent.)

Three things follow, and all three are material to plan 02.5-10:

1. **The FAIL is a seed lottery, not a proof the method cannot work.** At seed 2 the chart arm *beats* the raw-point baseline on every axis at once — Spearman 0.8665 vs 0.6712, median ratio 0.9844 vs 0.8934, CV **0.158 vs 0.293**, slope 0.868 vs 0.706. That is the only place in this phase where the noise-averaging argument has actually shown something.
2. **The driver is atlas fragmentation.** `rho_chart` is monotone in the number of charts the model ends up using (3 → 0.8665, 5 → 0.4250, 8 → -0.0604 and -0.1444), and `max cond` tracks it the same way (3.26, 7.64, 63.19, 122.22). More charts means more artificial joins in the learned surface, and second derivatives see every one of them.
3. **The D-09 point survives the sweep and is sharpened by it.** Seeds 0 and 3 have essentially the same reconstruction quality (5.21% vs 5.50%) and Spearman differing by 0.49. Reconstruction quality does not predict curvature agreement even across models of the same architecture on the same data.

The notebook was **not** re-seeded to a luckier draw. Seed 0 is 02.2's own seed and it stands as measured.
