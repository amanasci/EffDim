# Plan 08-07 — Post-freeze diagnostics and the reporting correction

## Why this plan exists

Five questions are open against Phase 8's result, four of them raised after the freeze and none
of them answerable by a number already on the record. All five are **non-gating**: nothing here
can change a sealed verdict, and nothing here may touch a frozen constant. What they change is
what the write-up is allowed to claim, which is why they land before `08-06` rather than after it.

1. **Density control.** The density field is a k-NN estimate in the ambient 768-D LegacySurvey
   space, and the milestone controls on it three different ways. A curved manifold's ambient chord
   runs shorter than its geodesic, more so where curvature is higher, so the density estimate
   could be curvature-contaminated and the control could be removing real signal. Measured
   informally on 2026-08-30 and the hypothesis did **not** hold; this plan promotes that
   measurement to a committed runner so the record shows the control was interrogated rather than
   assumed.
2. **Radial curvature.** `subsample.l2_normalize` puts every row on the unit sphere — verified,
   `norm min/med/max = 1.000000` for both modalities. A `d`-dimensional submanifold of the unit
   sphere carries a radial mean-curvature component of exactly `-d` under the `tr_g(II)` convention
   this milestone uses. The frozen `||H||` medians are 37.19 / 41.41 / 47.03 at `d` = 20 / 25 / 32,
   and removing a radial term of magnitude `d` in quadrature leaves 31.36 / 33.02 / 34.46 — a
   spread of 10% where the raw field's is 26%. That arithmetic is suggestive, not conclusive, and
   it bears directly on the milestone's thinnest number: PU's `||H||` p95/p05 spread of ~1.5, and
   Phase 8's `realized_h_contrast` of 1.16.
3. **Per-`d` instrument fidelity.** `INSTRUMENT_FIDELITY_RANGE = (0.53, 0.99)` was measured on
   analytic fixtures at `d=20` only. Both Phase 7 (after density control) and Phase 8 lose the
   signal at `d=32`. A dying instrument and a vanishing effect are not currently distinguishable
   there, and that is the single largest hole in the `d=32` reading.
4. **The invalid positive control.** `08-05`'s positive control returned `POSITIVE CONTROL INVALID`
   — the `magnitude=0.0` no-injection anchor cleared its null at all six `(S, kernel)` cells, so
   the detection-floor power curve D8-18 was meant to deliver does not exist. `08-06`'s
   `must_haves` predate that outcome and require only the `d=32` gap and the false-positive rate in
   the verdict sentence. Whether the invalidity joins them there is a one-way reporting decision
   and belongs to the developer, not to an executor.
5. **Missing p-values.** The frozen record carries **no p-value for any rho**, by design: the
   per-point MKNN statistic is `j/k` for integer `j` and takes at most 21 distinct values across
   10,000 points (measured 15 at `HEADLINE_K = 20`), so `spearmanr`'s asymptotic p assumes no ties
   and is invalid. The frozen significance route is threshold clearance at
   `NULL_QUANTILE_PER_TAIL = 0.975` on both tails — Bonferroni-equivalent to one two-sided 0.05
   test — and the null draws are not stored, so an exact p is not recoverable from the record and
   must be recomputed. Threshold clearance is a correct answer to a reviewer but not a sufficient
   one; every rho the milestone reports needs a number beside it.

**Scope guard.** Items 2 and 3 audit Phase 7's curvature instrument, not Phase 8's CKA statistic.
They sit here because Phase 8's write-up is the document that must state them and because Phase 7
is sealed. Neither reopens, softens or recomputes a Phase 7 verdict — `ASSOCIATION DETECTED`,
`SURVIVES AT SUBSET OF d` and `SEED STABLE AT d=25` all stand exactly as measured.

**CLAUDE.md Swiss roll gate: NOT APPLICABLE, by declaration rather than omission.** This plan
introduces no new manifold-learning or representation-learning model. Task 2 reuses
`cae.PlainAutoEncoder`, `cae.train_plain_ae` and `decoder_curvature.plain_decoder_curvature`

`notebooks/02.6_swiss_roll_plainae_curvature_check.ipynb` already tests against a known analytic
answer, and which `07-FINDINGS.md` §4 already declared satisfied for this instrument.
