`## 4` also states the three gating metrics with their exact computation and threshold:

T1 — geodesic distortion, gating. Computed on the initial encoder's global embedding
`z = E(x)` in `R^40` for all 10,000 rows, never on chart-local coordinates (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3:
two points on different charts have coordinates in unrelated parameterisations). Pairs are the
frozen cached 200,000-pair sample; the runner re-draws them with `draw_geo_pairs` under

distances (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 5). `d2_rep[i] = ||z[rows[i]] - z[cols[i]]||^2`. Because a neural
encoder carries no constraint tying its output scale to the geodesic scale, exactly one global
isotropic scale factor `s` is granted and pre-registered here:
`s = median(d2_geo / d2_rep)` taken over the subset of pairs with **both** endpoints in the
training split, then applied to all 200,000 pairs. `s` is reported in the verdict. The statistic is
`geometry_probes.distortion_stats(s * d2_rep, d2_geo)["median_abs_rel"]` — the same function 02.1
used, imported not reimplemented. PASS iff `median_abs_rel < THRESH_DISTORTION_MAX = 0.15`. State
the argument: 02.1's classical q=0 baseline at the matched dimension p=40 is 0.179641 and its best
rung at p=8 is 0.121024, so 0.15 sits materially below the matched-dimension linear baseline while

scalar is a disclosed generosity the linear baselines did not need, since their scale comes from
the eigendecomposition by construction. Also report, non-gating, the same statistic restricted to
pairs with both endpoints in the holdout split.

T2 — chart-transition cycle residual, gating. `R_cycle(x)` is the paper's eq. 8 verbatim: the sum
of the two unsquared L2 norms obtained by encoding and decoding `x` through chart alpha, then
re-encoding and decoding that result through chart beta and comparing to `x`, plus the symmetric
beta-then-alpha direction. Evaluated on holdout rows only. For each holdout row the pair
`(alpha, beta)` is the two highest chart-predictor probabilities among surviving charts, and the
row is admitted to the overlap set only if both `p_alpha >= OVERLAP_P_MIN = 0.05` and
`p_beta >= OVERLAP_P_MIN`; this cutoff is pre-registered here because without it the evaluation
set is ill-defined and could be narrowed to high-confidence single-chart points that trivially
give near-zero residual. If fewer than `OVERLAP_MIN_POINTS = 200` holdout rows qualify, T2 is
undefined and the run halts rather than reporting a value. The matched base quantity is
`E_base(x) = 2 * ||x - yhat(x)||` — twice the single-pass argmax-chart reconstruction norm, matching
eq. 8's two-term unsquared form. PASS iff
`mean(R_cycle) < THRESH_RCYCLE_RATIO_MAX * mean(E_base)` with `THRESH_RCYCLE_RATIO_MAX = 2.0`.
State the argument: no absolute reference value for `R_cycle` exists in the paper or this codebase,
so the bound is relative to the model's own reconstruction noise; round-tripping through a second
chart must not more than double the base error.

reconstruction back to the 768-d ambient space) and that classical MDS has no native inverse map,

`mse_cae < (1 - THRESH_RECON_MARGIN) * mse_mds_dec8` with `THRESH_RECON_MARGIN = 0.10`. State the

The three proposed gates, each of which must hold for PASS (every comparison strict less-than):

1. **Geodesic distortion** on the initial encoder's global `R^40` embedding, against the frozen
   200,000-pair geodesic sample, after fitting one global isotropic scale on training-split pairs:

   Reference points: 02.1's classical q=0 baseline is `0.179641` at the matched dimension p=40 and

   matched-dimension linear baseline); a stricter alternative is `< 0.121024` (beat the best
   classical rung anywhere).

2. **Chart-transition cycle residual** (paper eq. 8) on holdout points where the two top chart
   probabilities are both at least `0.05`, requiring at least 200 qualifying points:
   `mean(R_cycle) < 2.0 * mean(E_base)`, where `E_base` is twice the single-pass reconstruction
   norm. No absolute reference exists in the paper or this codebase, so the bound is relative to
   the model's own reconstruction noise.

3. **Held-out reconstruction** versus both baselines at matched capacity: CAE per-dimension MSE
   must be at least **10% below both** the plain single-chart autoencoder control at bottleneck 20
   and the fitted classical-MDS decoder at p=8. CAE-03 states that merely tying the control is not
   a success.

Two disclosures worth your attention before you ratify:

- Granting the CAE one free global scale factor for gate 1 is a generosity the linear baselines did
  not need — their scale comes from the eigendecomposition by construction. Without it the metric

- There is deliberately **no MARGINAL tier**. Phase 2's gate had one; here D-02 routes every

**Decision: `promote`.** The latent representation is intrinsically multi-chart. There is no
single global latent chart any more, and every downstream consumer (Phase 3's decoder and

globally comparable coordinate system this phase produces is the initial encoder's output
