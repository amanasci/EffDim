  <context>
`09-ALIGNMENT-PROOF.md` now carries the measured curve and the verdict against the frozen margin.
D9-08 fixed the response to a failure before any of it was measured: "If shift 0 fails but some
other shift passes, adopt that offset and proceed. Recorded as a post-hoc, data-chosen step: the
developer chose this over halt with no Physics number."

That choice was made in `/gsd-discuss-phase`, so the policy is not being re-asked. What is being
ruled on here is its application to the measured outcome, because the adoption is one-way: the
offset is baked into the label vector every downstream number is computed from, and changing it
afterwards means a third freeze and a complete re-run of the execution-host workload.

The measured branch determines which options are live:

- **Shift 0 PASSED.** Only `proceed-as-assumed` applies. The ruling records the pass and the phase
  continues on `ALIGNMENT_ASSUMED_OFFSET = 0` with no amendment and no re-freeze.
- **Shift 0 FAILED and exactly one alignment clears.** `adopt-found-offset` and `halt` are both
  live. Adoption requires: a numbered amendment document superseding the offset constant, a fresh
  freeze commit containing that single change, that commit's SHA rewired into both runners and
  both test suites, the fresh freeze pushed and re-verified from a clone, and every subsequent run
  gated on the NEW SHA. `09-FINDINGS.md` must then state which offset was used and that it was
  found rather than assumed.
- **Shift 0 FAILED and two or more alignments clear (AMBIGUOUS), or none clears.** Only `halt`
  applies. `ALIGNMENT_SEARCH_RULE` fixed this before the data: with more than one candidate there
  is no principled choice, and picking the largest would be selection on the outcome.

**Undo cost if wrong:** an adopted offset reaches every local R2, every control, every curvature
pairing and every verdict cell. Undoing it is a third freeze plus a full re-run — four autoencoder
fits at 86,471 rows, the two gates, and the conditional seed wave.
  </context>
