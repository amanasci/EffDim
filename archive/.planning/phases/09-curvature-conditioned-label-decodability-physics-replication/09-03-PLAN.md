<discretion_decisions>
- **The label table is read by direct column-projected `pyarrow` reads, not
  `datasets.load_dataset`.** `09-RESEARCH.md` measured a column-projected read of 8 columns from
  one `v2.0` shard at ~43s without downloading image bytes, against ~7.8 GB for the full split.
  The revision then lives in the URL fragment, which is stronger than a keyword argument that a
  future caller could omit — the exact failure mode Pitfall 1 names.
- **The full-scale missingness check runs before the freeze, on the developer's machine, and is
  not a Physics number.** Row counts, dtypes and finite-value counts are dataset metadata; a
  correlation, an R2 or a curvature value would be a measured statistic. The distinction is
  enforced by `run_manifest`'s prohibition and pinned behaviourally by
  `test_manifest_mode_writes_no_statistic_key`. The freeze cannot happen without this evidence,
  because `LABEL_COLUMN_MAP` and `EXPECTED_N_PHYSICS_ROWS` are among the constants being frozen.
- **`--mode manifest` may run locally; `--mode proof` may not.** The manifest downloads and counts;
  the proof fits 45 out-of-fold ridge regressions on 86,471 rows and produces the first real
  statistic of the phase. The former is data acquisition, the latter is the phase's first
  measured number and belongs on the execution host under a freeze-SHA gate.
- **The proof appends per-alignment rows as it goes.** A 45-alignment run interrupted at alignment
  30 keeps 30 rows of evidence. Buffering to the end would lose them, and the record is the
  artifact that gets transferred back from a machine we do not control.
