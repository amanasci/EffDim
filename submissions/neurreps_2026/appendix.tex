\section{Implementation notes}
\label{app:methods}

Definitions are taken from the code paths that produced the frozen artifacts; see also \texttt{METHODS\_FOR\_PAPER.md}.

Local coordinates: row-$\ell_2$ unit embeddings; $x_0$ normalised; radial component removed before nested PCA; $J\leftarrow\mathrm{orth}((I-x_0x_0^\top)J)$ (\texttt{sphere\_project\_basis}). Quadratic features $\phi_{ab}=u_au_b$ for $a\le b$; off-diagonal packed entries store $2B_{ab}$. $B^S$ columns are projected onto the complement of $\mathrm{span}(x_0,J)$. Production $K_H^{\mathrm{cross}}$ is the split-half inner product of $H$, not $\|H\|$. OOF probes are five-fold ridge ($\alpha{=}100$). Local $R^2$ uses uniform weights on finite neighbours:
\[
R^{2}_{\mathrm{local}}=1-\sum_i(y_i-\hat y_i)^2\Big/\sum_i(y_i-\bar y)^2.
\]
SSE is $\sum_i(y_i-\hat y_i)^2$; MSE is $\mathrm{SSE}/n_{\mathrm{eval}}$; MAE is $n_{\mathrm{eval}}^{-1}\sum_i|y_i-\hat y_i|$; SST is $\sum_i(y_i-\bar y)^2$. NMSE is $1-R^{2}_{\mathrm{local}}$ and is therefore not an independent estimand.

\section{Target identity and parity}
\label{app:parity}

The primary target is local OOF probe $R^{2}$ (\texttt{mag\_r\_desi\_local\_oof\_r2}). Catalog magnitude (\texttt{mag\_r\_desi\_catalog\_value}) is a distinct vector. A typed schema rejects ambiguous labels (\texttt{local\_r2}, \texttt{mag\_r\_desi}) and rejects a catalog vector that is identical or an affine rescaling of the probe-performance target. After \texttt{sample\_id} alignment, controlled Spearman correlations at $k{=}2048$, $n{=}512$ match the frozen audit to the reported rounding: $\rho_{12}{=}+0.143$, $\rho_{16}{=}-0.240$, $\rho_{20}{=}-0.233$. Reconstructed $R^{2}_{\mathrm{local}}$ matches the frozen geography table with maximum absolute difference $0$.

\section{Scale subset and reliability}
\label{app:scale}

Chart positions were fixed from held-out variance ($\tau{=}0.80,0.85$ and the midpoint), not by maximising $|\rho|$. Table~\ref{tab:scale} uses the rank-sweep $128$-anchor refits. \section{Excluded analyses}
\label{app:excluded}

DESI spectroscopic labels are omitted because embedding-table identity alignment is unproven; equal row counts are not a join. Catalog-magnitude associations are retained only as a negative control against silent substitution and are not a discovery result. Adaptive-run physics labels other than the frozen probe target are not used for the claim. An earlier catalog-label substitution motivated strict target typing; it is not part of the scientific narrative of the main text.
