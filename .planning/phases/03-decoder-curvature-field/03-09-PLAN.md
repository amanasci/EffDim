<objective>
Compute the phase's actual deliverable: a per-point mean-curvature field `||H||` over the PU cloud,
with its metric conditioning beside it, across three seeds, and run the independent
finite-difference bridge against it at real scale.

This is ROADMAP steps 2 and 3. Step 2 is descriptive — a histogram and a map, no verdict. Step 3 adds
the seed spread, the near-singular flags, the non-zero-and-finite second-derivative confirmation, and
the no-extrapolation guarantee.

Two things this step is specifically positioned to catch. First, `||H||` small and `cond(g)` large
look identical in a histogram and mean opposite things — genuine near-zero curvature from principal
curvatures cancelling in the trace, versus a decoder differential that is nearly rank-deficient. Only
reporting both per point tells them apart, which is why `cond(g)` goes next to `||H||` and never
instead of it. Second, plan 03-05's forward-versus-reverse equivalence test compares two autodiff
paths and structurally cannot see a bug they share; the finite-difference bridge can, and this phase
is the first ever to edit `chart_curvature.py`.

The wall-clock cost of curvature at `d = 20, D = 768` over the full cloud has never been measured
anywhere in this milestone. It gets measured here.

Purpose: produce the field, and produce the evidence that makes it readable.
Output: `--field` and `--bridge` modes, executed, with their tables recorded.
</objective>
