# Amendment 02 — Falsifier condition (b) re-read; the falsifier fires

**Committed before the revised recommendation applies it.** Ordering is provable by git ancestry,
not asserted — the same discipline `057b084` and `9e4b274` established for this milestone.

This amendment changes a **falsifier** after the evidence that decides it already exists. That is
the highest-risk rule change the pre-registration contemplates, and §6 states plainly how a reader
should discount it. Read §6 before accepting §3.

## 1. Overridden

**1.1 Falsifier condition (b), reading of "materially below".** As pre-registered:

> (b) the `(p,q)` ladder never dropping `DISTORTION` materially below `q=0` (the baseline that
> already failed at m=0.412071) at any rung up to 40.

`DISTORTION` is defined on the representation's own squared distances. For the pseudo-Euclidean
candidate those are computed under the indefinite form `Σ_a sign(λ_a)(x_i,a − x_j,a)²`. Under that
definition the ladder does drop — 0.065190 at (40,25) against a q=0 best of 0.079864, an 18.4%
relative reduction — and (b) does not trip. That is the reading `02.1-RECOMMENDATION.md` applied,
correctly, on the evidence available when it was written.

**Amended reading:** condition (b) is satisfied — the ladder counts as *not* dropping materially
below q=0 — unless the drop is realisable **in a form Phase 3's decoder can consume**. Phase 3's
decoder (DEC-01, DEC-02, CURV-01..03) has a Euclidean latent and a C2-smooth forward path; it
differentiates over a real coordinate domain under the ordinary quadratic form. A distortion
improvement that exists only under an indefinite bilinear form is not available to it.

Rationale for the change, not a justification of the outcome: the falsifier exists to ask whether
any shortlisted coordinate candidate beats the failed classical baseline *for the purpose the
representation is being chosen for*. Read purely on the indefinite statistic, (b) can be satisfied
by an improvement Phase 3 provably cannot use, which makes the falsifier unable to detect the
failure mode it was written to detect. The original wording did not anticipate that the winning
candidate's advantage would be signature-carried.

## 3. Applying the amended falsifier

**Condition (a) — trips, by a wide margin.** Real manifold `delta_rel_max = 0.383921` (n=10,000;
0.386330 at matched n=2,000) against a flat-Euclidean anchor of `0.360433` and a tree anchor of
~0, with the reading rule's threshold at `tree + 0.1 × gap = 0.036043`. The real value sits *past*
the flat anchor: this geometry is less tree-like than flat space under this estimator.
`single_curvature_defensible = False`.

**Condition (b) — trips under the amended reading.** No rung of the (p,q) ladder drops materially
below the q=0 baseline in a Euclidean-latent-consumable form. The only sub-0.079864 results in the
entire phase are (25,8) at 0.069532 and (40,25) at 0.065190, both indefinite; stripped of signature
they land at 0.393400 (full) and 0.179641 (positive block), both above the baseline.

## 6. How to discount this amendment

**6.1 The evidence predates the rule change.** `signature_transfer_43cf438bc944c509.json` was
computed 2026-08-01; this amendment is dated 2026-08-05. The rule was changed by someone who
already knew which way it would fall. The pre-registration's Prohibited Moves says exactly why that
is dangerous: "a rule edited after evidence is indistinguishable from one that was always there."
This one is distinguishable only because it is dated, public, and states its own weakness.

**6.2 The direction is against interest, which is the one structural check available.** A
self-serving rule change eases the author's path. This one fires a falsifier, discards a completed
recommendation, re-opens thirteen requirements, replaces an in-hand zero-install representation
with a branch needing two SUS installs, an unbuilt fixture, and Sinkhorn over 150,000 edges, and
converts CURV-03's extrinsic mean-curvature vector into a different mathematical object. It buys
more work, not less. That is not proof of good faith, but it is the opposite of the pattern the
prohibition guards against.

**6.3 What survives if you reject this amendment.** The un-amended reading is fully recoverable and
nothing was overwritten to reach this one:

- Falsifier (b) does not trip; (a) alone does not overturn; the coordinate-producing verdict stands.
- Krein (40,25) at 0.065190 is the measured winner under the pre-registered criterion — rejected by
  user directive in Amendment 01 §1.3, with nothing measured since beating it.
- GEOM-03's answer is unchanged either way: 41% negative mass is geometry, not measurement error,
  and correction is unprincipled here. That judgment rests on `correction_blindness` (identical
  m=0.0 on real, flat, and non-metric spectra) and on the four eliminated measurement-error
  explanations, none of which this amendment touches.
- `02.1-RECOMMENDATION.md`'s pre-amendment text is recoverable at `59742af`.

**6.4 What would settle it without a rule change.** A Phase 3 decoder trained on Krein (40,25)
coordinates and evaluated on held-out reconstruction (DEC-03/04) would measure directly whether the
18.4% is reachable. That is the check `02.1-RECOMMENDATION.md`'s "Evidence It Will Be Judged
Against" (2) already specified. `signature_transfer` is a cheap proxy for it, not a substitute —
it measures distance distortion under a Euclidean form, not decoder reconstruction. A reader who
wants the expensive answer before re-opening thirteen requirements is entitled to ask for it, and
this amendment should be revisited if that check contradicts the proxy.
