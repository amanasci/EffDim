## 1. Overridden

**1.1 Falsifier condition (b), reading of "materially below".** As pre-registered:

> (b) the `(p,q)` ladder never dropping `DISTORTION` materially below `q=0` (the baseline that
> already failed at m=0.412071) at any rung up to 40.

`DISTORTION` is defined on the representation's own squared distances. For the pseudo-Euclidean
candidate those are computed under the indefinite form `Σ_a sign(λ_a)(x_i,a − x_j,a)²`. Under that
definition the ladder does drop — 0.065190 at (40,25) against a q=0 best of 0.079864, an 18.4%
relative reduction — and (b) does not trip. That is the reading `02.1-RECOMMENDATION.md` applied,
correctly, on the evidence available when it was written.

**Amended reading:** condition (b) is satisfied — the ladder counts as *not* dropping materially
below q=0 — unless the drop is realisable in a form Phase 3's decoder can consume. Phase 3's
decoder (DEC-01, DEC-02, CURV-01..03) has a Euclidean latent and a C2-smooth forward path; it
differentiates over a real coordinate domain under the ordinary quadratic form.

Rationale for the change, not a justification of the outcome: the falsifier exists to ask whether
any shortlisted coordinate candidate beats the failed classical baseline *for the purpose the
representation is being chosen for*. Read purely on the indefinite statistic, (b) can be satisfied
by an improvement that never materialises where it would have to matter, which makes the falsifier
unable to detect the failure mode it was written to detect. The original wording did not anticipate
that the winning candidate's advantage would be signature-carried.

## 2. The measurements this rests on

### 2.1 Primary — the decoder-side check (direct)

`02.1-DECODER-PREREGISTRATION.md`; runner `notebooks/diagnostics/decoder_transfer_check.py` →
`decoder_transfer_43cf438bc944c509.json`. Four representations, one protocol, only the input
coordinates differ. Every hyperparameter fixed before any decoder trained. Pair sample and
coordinate construction verified against all three published ladder rungs before training began.

| Arm | dims | distance distortion | MSE/dim (standardized) | MSE/dim (raw) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Krein (40,25) | 65 | 0.065190 | 0.00005069 | 0.00005210 |
| classical (18,0) | 18 | 0.079864 | 0.00005143 | 0.00005216 |
| classical (40,0) | 40 | 0.179641 | **0.00005002** | **0.00005128** |
| Krein (20,20) — control | 40 | 0.137464 | 0.00005105 | 0.00005313 |

**Krein (40,25) improves held-out reconstruction over the classical (18,0) floor by +1.44%
(standardized) or +0.10% (raw), against the +18.37% its distance advantage promised.** The drop
exists in the distance statistic and very nearly vanishes in the thing the representation is being
chosen to feed.

**On using a check whose own verdict was INCONCLUSIVE.** That check's pre-registered rules split:
standardized read Rule 2 (partial), raw read Rule 3 (confirmation), they disagreed, and by its own
terms no rule fired. That inconclusiveness is about *how firmly to state a confirmation* — both
readings reject the retraction trigger. For the question condition (b) actually asks — does the
ladder drop **materially** below q=0 in a consumable form — the two readings agree: +1.44% and
+0.10% are both immaterial against a promised +18.37%. The inconclusiveness is orthogonal to (b),
and saying so is not a reinterpretation after the fact; the split and the threshold were both fixed
in advance.

### 2.2 Primary — the matched-width signature control

Krein (20,20) against classical (40,0), identical 40-dimensional input width, same protocol:
**−2.07% (standardized), −3.61% (raw)**. At equal width the signature does not merely fail to help,
it costs reconstruction in both preprocessings. So the (40,25) arm's small positive margin is
attributable to having 65 input dimensions rather than to the sign structure.

This control was declared control-only before the fact and no adoption decision rests on it; it is
used here only as evidence about what the signature does, which is exactly what it was built for.

### 2.3 Corroborating — signature_transfer, with its weight lowered

`signature_transfer_43cf438bc944c509.json` (`8562fec`):
`advantage_transfers_to_euclidean_latent = false`, `distortion_indefinite = 0.065190`,
`distortion_positive_block_only = 0.179641`, `distortion_euclidean = 0.393400`,
`flat_floor = 0.079864`, `signature_discard_penalty = 0.328210`.

Read narrowly — as a statement that the 18.4% advantage lives in the indefinite form and not in the
coordinates themselves — this stands and corroborates §2.1.

**Its limitation, stated plainly.** It was originally used to argue that a Euclidean-latent decoder
would therefore perform badly on Krein coordinates. That inference was wrong: measured directly, it
performs about as well as on anything else. A five-fold distance blow-up did not produce a
detectable reconstruction penalty. **`signature_transfer` over-predicted, and this amendment no
longer leans on it.**

### 2.4 Context — the ~0.0796 wall

Independent arms measured under Amendment 01, all at `pair_identity_verified: true`:

| Arm | DISTORTION | Family |
|---|---|---|
| metric SMACOF (d=18) | 0.079605 | stress optimiser — no eigendecomposition, no PSD constraint |
| Isomap embedding (d=18) / classical MDS (18,0) | 0.079864 | classical-MDS |
| Laplacian eigenmaps (d=18), optimally rescaled | 0.279918 | PSD-spectral |
| LLE standard (d=18), optimally rescaled | 0.420736 | PSD-spectral |
| non-metric MDS (d=18), optimally rescaled | 0.488891 | rank-order stress |
| hypersphere ambient great-circle (dim 767) | 0.769597 | ambient-Riemannian |
| hypersphere tangent PCA (best, d=65) | 0.780607 | ambient-Riemannian |

Four structurally unrelated families wall at ~0.0796; metric SMACOF reaching it with no
eigendecomposition and no PSD requirement shows the binding constraint is **flatness of the target
space, not the algorithm**. This is a distortion statement and condition (b) is a distortion
condition, so the two are commensurate: **no decoder-consumable (q=0) rung gets below the wall, and
the only sub-wall result on the entire ladder is indefinite.**

## 3. Applying the amended falsifier

**Condition (a) — trips, by a wide margin.** Real manifold `delta_rel_max = 0.383921` (n=10,000;
0.386330 at matched n=2,000) against a flat-Euclidean anchor of `0.360433` and a tree anchor of
~0, with the reading rule's threshold at `tree + 0.1 × gap = 0.036043`. The real value sits *past*
the flat anchor: this geometry is less tree-like than flat space under this estimator.
`single_curvature_defensible = False`. Untouched by anything in this revision.

**Condition (b) — trips under the amended reading.** The chain, primary evidence first:

1. **The drop is not realisable where it must be.** Directly measured: +1.44% / +0.10% of a
   promised +18.37% (§2.1).
2. **The signature is not what buys the small remaining margin.** At matched width it is net
   negative, −2.07% / −3.61% (§2.2).
3. **No consumable rung is below the wall.** Every `q=0` rung on the ladder and every independent
   flat-target arm sits at or above ~0.0796; the only sub-wall result is indefinite (§2.4).
4. Corroborated, at reduced weight, by `signature_transfer` (§2.3).

**BOTH conditions trip. The falsifier fires.**

## 6. How to discount this amendment

**6.1 The evidence predates the rule change.** `signature_transfer_43cf438bc944c509.json` was
computed 2026-08-01; this amendment is dated 2026-08-05. The rule was changed by someone who
already knew which way it would fall. The pre-registration's Prohibited Moves says exactly why that
is dangerous: "a rule edited after evidence is indistinguishable from one that was always there."
This one is distinguishable only because it is dated, public, and states its own weakness.

The decoder check of §2.1 is the one piece of evidence here that does **not** carry this problem —
its rules were fixed and committed (`e0861a1`) before any decoder trained, and it was run to test
this amendment rather than to support it. That it came back against Krein is the strongest thing
this document has.

**6.2 The direction is against interest, which is the one structural check available.** A
self-serving rule change eases the author's path. This one fires a falsifier, discards a completed
recommendation, re-opens thirteen requirements, replaces an in-hand zero-install representation
with a branch needing two SUS installs, an unbuilt fixture, and Sinkhorn over 150,000 edges, and
converts CURV-03's extrinsic mean-curvature vector into a different mathematical object. It buys
more work, not less. That is not proof of good faith, but it is the opposite of the pattern the
prohibition guards against.

**6.3 What survives if you reject this amendment.** The un-amended reading is fully recoverable and
nothing was overwritten to reach this one:

- Falsifier (b) does not trip; (a) alone does not overturn; the coordinate-producing verdict stands.
- Krein (40,25) at 0.065190 is the measured winner under the pre-registered criterion — rejected by
  user directive in Amendment 01 §1.3, with nothing measured since beating it.
- GEOM-03's answer is unchanged either way: 41% negative mass is geometry, not measurement error,
  and correction is unprincipled here. That judgment rests on `correction_blindness` (identical
  m=0.0 on real, flat, and non-metric spectra) and on the four eliminated measurement-error
  explanations, none of which this amendment touches.
- `02.1-RECOMMENDATION.md`'s pre-amendment text is recoverable at `59742af`; this amendment's
  original argument at `11de2f6`.

**6.4 The strongest reason to doubt condition (b) as an instrument at all.** The decoder check
surfaced something it was not designed to look for: **held-out reconstruction was nearly decoupled
from the distance-distortion statistic this phase ranks representations by.** Classical (40,0),
whose distortion of 0.179641 is the *worst* of the three ladder rungs, reconstructed *best* of all
four arms on both preprocessings. Distortion spanned 2.75× across the arms; MSE spanned about 6%.

Condition (b) is a distortion condition. If distortion is a poor predictor of what a decoder does
with a representation, then (b) is a weaker instrument than the pre-registration assumed — **for
firing as well as for not firing.** This cuts against this amendment's own §2.4 (the wall is a
distortion argument) as much as it cuts against the original Krein case.

Bounds on that: one `TORCH_SEED` was run, per the no-seed-shopping prohibition, so few-percent gaps
cannot be separated from initialisation noise, and decoder capacity saturation is not excluded. It
is an observation, not a verdict. But a reader looking for the real weak point of this amendment
should look here, not at §6.1.

**6.5 What would settle it properly.** A seed-sensitivity study over the decoder arms (3–5 seeds,
separately pre-registered) would establish whether the decoupling in §6.4 and the +1.44%/+0.10%
split are real or noise. That is the next experiment this document would ask for, and it has not
been run. If it showed distortion predicting reconstruction well after all, §2.4 regains its force
and §6.4 dissolves. If it confirmed the decoupling, then GEOM-04 should arguably be re-opened on
the grounds that **no** representation was ever ranked by a criterion that measured the right
thing — a larger problem than which branch won.
