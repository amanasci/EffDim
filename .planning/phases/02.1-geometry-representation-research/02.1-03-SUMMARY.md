### 2. Delta-hyperbolicity, against tree and flat-Euclidean anchors (matched n=2,000, 200,000 quadruples, seed=20260801)

| Sample | `delta_rel_max` | `delta_rel_p95` |
|---|---|---|
| Tree (n=2,000) | 0.000000 | 0.000000 |
| Real manifold (n=2,000) | **0.386330** | 0.136450 |

**Reading (02.1-PREREGISTRATION.md's Delta-Hyperbolicity Reading Rule, applied verbatim, unedited):** threshold = tree anchor + 0.1 × (Euclidean anchor − tree anchor) = 0.036043. The real value (0.386330) is nowhere near this threshold — not merely "far from the tree anchor," it actually **exceeds the flat-Euclidean anchor itself** (0.386 > 0.360). `single_curvature_defensible = False`. **Falsifier condition (a) trips unambiguously and by a wide margin**: a single constant negative curvature (pure hyperbolic target) is not supported — a stronger form of the failure the falsifier anticipated ("indistinguishable from, or closer to, the flat-Euclidean anchor"): the real value is closer to (in fact past) the Euclidean anchor than the Euclidean anchor is to the tree anchor.

Full ladder (81 rungs) and full breakdown live in `geometry_probes_43cf438bc944c509.json`.

**Reading:** the full-ladder best (0.065190 at `p=40, q=25`) is an **18.4% relative reduction** from the `q=0` baseline's best (0.079864 at `p=18`) — a real, non-zero drop, but modest in absolute terms (6.5% vs. 8.0% median relative distortion), and the best-ladder rung only captures 24.4% of total eigenvalue mass even at `p+q=65`, consistent with Phase 2's finding of a long diffuse tail. **Falsifier condition (b) does not cleanly trip** — there is a measured improvement from retaining negative directions, but whether an 18.4% relative reduction counts as "material" is a judgment this plan does not make, per its own instruction not to invent a threshold.

**Because the falsifier requires BOTH conditions and condition (b) does not cleanly trip, the falsifier as pre-registered does not fire — the coordinate-producing verdict stands.** But condition (a)'s margin (real value past the Euclidean anchor) and condition (b)'s modest-but-real improvement are close enough to the edge that 02.1-04 should read both numbers directly rather than treat this as a clean pass.

### 4. Working dimension (re-derived under the gate verdict's own kneedle elbow criterion, applied verbatim)

- Classical (`q=0`) elbow: **p = 8**
- Pseudo-Euclidean best-per-total-dimension frontier elbow: **(p, q) = (8, 0)**
- `d_frozen` (Phase 2, for reference, not adjudicated here) = **5**

**Notable, unexpected result:** the pseudo-Euclidean frontier's elbow lands on `q = 0` — retaining negative eigenvalue directions does not move the kneedle-selected working dimension at all under this criterion. The best absolute distortion (`p=40, q=25`) sits far past the elbow, in the flat tail of the curve where the kneedle criterion (correctly, by its own definition) does not select it — contradicting an implicit expectation in `RESEARCH.md`'s framing of the pseudo-Euclidean branch as offering a materially different working dimension from classical MDS. Under this measurement it offers the same elbow-selected `p`, differing only in whether `q > 0` is retained past the elbow for marginal gain.
