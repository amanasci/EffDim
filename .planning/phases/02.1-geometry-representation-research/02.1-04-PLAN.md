<objective>
Write the phase's terminal artifact: the argued judgment on what 41% negative eigenvalue mass means
and whether correcting it is principled, the one recommended representation, and the working
dimension that follows from it — plus the machine-readable handoff Phase 3 reads before it runs
anything.

Purpose: this is the plan the phase exists for. Phase 2 established, across four experiments, that
classical MDS cannot describe this geodesic geometry, and ROADMAP.md's Phase 3 section has already
been amended to say it now depends on this phase's output rather than on a Phase 2 PASS. What
Phase 3 needs is not a survey — plan 02.1-02 wrote that — and not a set of measurements — plan
02.1-03 produced those. It needs a decision it can be re-specified from without re-opening the
question.

Three of the phase's requirements land here together because RESEARCH.md's Entanglement section
says they cannot be sequenced apart. Item 4: choosing a pseudo-Euclidean/Krein representation *is*
choosing to retain rather than correct, so GEOM-03's stance and GEOM-04's recommendation answer
each other as side effects, and a document that decides them in sequence will contradict itself.
Item 2: a curved target space can represent the same structure in fewer or more dimensions than a
flat one needs, so GEOM-05's working dimension cannot be fixed before or independently of the
representation — the striking data point RESEARCH.md found is a hyperbolic embedding reaching
MAP = 0.989 in two dimensions where 200-dimensional Euclidean embeddings did worse. Representation,
dimension and evaluation criterion are one joint decision.

The trap this plan is written against is named explicitly in RESEARCH.md's Pattern 1 and Pitfall 2
and was measured by plan 02.1-03: correction methods are diagnostic-blind by construction.
Cailliez, Lingoes and eigenvalue clipping force a PSD Gram matrix and therefore a passing gate on
*any* input, curved or not. For a project whose entire purpose is to measure curvature, quietly
adopting one would delete the signal and manufacture a false PASS. The measured demonstration —
zero negative mass after correction on the real spectrum, a synthetic flat spectrum, and a
synthetic non-metric spectrum alike — turns that from a warning into a finding.

Output: `02.1-RECOMMENDATION.md`, `notebooks/diagnostics/geometry_handoff.py`, and
`notebooks/.cache/geometry_handoff_43cf438bc944c509.json`, ratified by a human reading it as the
Phase 3 planner would.

Task budget: 3 tasks. GEOM-03's judgment (~15%), GEOM-04 plus GEOM-05 plus the handoff (~25%), and a
blocking checkpoint that carries no context cost. Roughly 40-45% total.
</objective>
