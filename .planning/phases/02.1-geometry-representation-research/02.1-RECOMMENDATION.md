## GEOM-03 — What 41% Negative Mass Means

Classical MDS double-centres squared distances into `B = -1/2 J D² J`; for genuinely Euclidean `D`,
`B` is PSD and each eigenvalue is a squared extent along a principal direction. A negative
eigenvalue is a direction along which the metric behaves as though squared distance were
*subtracted* — no real vector has negative squared norm. Isomap inherits this exactly: the negative
eigenvalues say the geodesic metric itself is not Euclidean-embeddable.

The shape is the finding: **5,029 of 10,000 eigenvalues strictly negative**, `m = 0.412071`;
`r = 0.052419` passes its 0.10 bound at every k (thresholds: r pass 0.10 / marginal 0.25, m pass 0.05 / marginal 0.15) — not one short-circuit edge but 5,029 directions,
none dominant, mass comparable to the positive part. §8's threshold-independent reading: **at 41%
the negative part is not a correction to a Euclidean picture, it is comparable in magnitude to the
positive part** — true under any reasonable threshold.

Corroboration (cited, not re-argued): `CURVE_DIVERGENCE_MAX = 0.697664` at d=5 between the two
residual curves (the eigenvalue-based curve, blind to the negative 41%, over-reports variance
captured); every negative magnitude 10–11 orders above the noise floor (7.174e-09 at k=15); four
experiments closed numerics (rtol=1e-8 vs sklearn), hop inflation (m flat-to-rising over k while
GEO_AMBIENT_RATIO fell 2.83→1.86 and LONG_EDGE_FRACTION rose 0.0065→0.0139), L2 normalization
(0.28% move), column (HSC m=0.4226), and draw (disjoint m=0.411948, identical counts).

**Limit carried forward:** §6.2 closes "normalization caused it", NOT "shell geometry contributes"
— raw norms already 16.029 ± 0.504 (cv 3.1%) before normalization. Unresolved here too.

## GEOM-03 — Correction or Retention

**Mechanism:** Cailliez shift, Lingoes additive constant, and clipping force PSD — and a passing
gate — on *any* input. **Shown, not asserted** (02.1-03's `correction_blindness`):

| Input | m before | m after clip | m after Lingoes shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real spectrum (audited fit) | 0.412071 | 0.0 | 0.0 |
| synthetic_flat (n=1,000, flat R²⁰; m ~4.05e-15) | ~0.0 | 0.0 | 0.0 |
| synthetic_nonmetric (n=1,000, random symmetric, no metric structure) | 0.500649 | 0.0 | 0.0 |

A real curved metric, an already-flat one, and pure non-metric noise all correct to identical
0.000000. A 100% pass rate is what corrections are engineered to guarantee, not evidence about the
geometry. For a project whose purpose is to *measure* curvature, silent adoption deletes the signal
and manufactures a false PASS.

**Judgment, tested against a stated condition.** A correction is principled when the negativity it
removes is known on independent grounds to be measurement error rather than geometry. This data
fails that condition: the four experiments eliminated every measurement-error explanation, and the
negativity survived at essentially constant magnitude (0.4064–0.4157 over 6× k; 0.4226 HSC;
0.411948 disjoint). **Retention, not correction.** A correction keeps one role: a labelled
cannot-fail contrast baseline — the table above is that baseline.

**The literature's alternative:** Neuc-MDS (2024, bilinear-form optimisation) — abstract-only
strength, no maintained implementation; the measured top-p/top-q retention is the lower-risk
fallback, not a compromise. This section and the next answer each other by construction
(Entanglement item 4).

## GEOM-04 — Recommendation

**Pseudo-Euclidean / Krein-space retention**: coordinates in `R^(p+q)` from the top-p positive and
top-q most-negative eigenpairs of the double-centred geodesic Gram matrix; distances under the
indefinite form `Σ_a sign(λ_a)(x_i,a − x_j,a)²`.

**Against the pre-registered criterion** — `DISTORTION = median(|d2_rep − d2_geo|/d2_geo)` over
the fixed 200,000-pair sample, q=0 in the same ladder as its own baseline:

| p | q | DISTORTION | mass captured |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 0.750521 | 0.064269 |
| 8 | 0 | 0.121024 | 0.160774 |
| 18 | 0 (q=0 best) | **0.079864** | 0.182637 |
| 25 | 0 | 0.099228 | 0.192361 |
| 40 | 0 | 0.179641 | 0.208089 |
| 25 | 8 | 0.069532 | 0.209715 |
| 40 | 25 (full-ladder best) | **0.065190** | 0.244267 |

Retention buys a real improvement: (40,25) is an **18.4% relative reduction** vs the q=0 best
(0.065190 vs 0.079864; `reduction_fraction = 0.183742`). Modest in absolute terms (6.5% vs 8.0%),
and the best rung captures only 24.4% of mass at p+q=65 — consistent with a long diffuse tail —
but genuine and non-cosmetic, unlike corrections that produce identical m=0 on any input.

**Delta-hyperbolicity, rule applied verbatim:** real manifold `delta_rel_max = 0.386330` at
matched n=2,000 vs tree anchor ~0 and flat-Euclidean anchor **0.360433**; threshold
`tree + 0.1×gap = 0.036043`. The real value **exceeds the flat-Euclidean anchor** (and at
n=10,000: 0.383921 vs the same anchor). `single_curvature_defensible = False` — this geometry is
*less* tree-like than flat space under this estimator, eliminating the hyperbolic family. Krein
makes no single-curvature assumption: p and q come from measured distortion.

**Assumptions that could be false:** (1) top-p/top-q truncation ≈ optimised Neuc-MDS selection
(untested — nothing to compare against); (2) the elbow dimension is the right decoder operating
point despite the available 18.4% at much higher dimension (Phase 3 weighs explicitly); (3) the
18.4% is real signal — under a stricter reading of "materially below", falsifier condition (b)
could be judged tripped, which with (a) already tripping would overturn the verdict.

## GEOM-04 — Alternatives Rejected

None rejected on difficulty.

- **Hyperbolic (Poincare/Lorentz)** — rejected on evidence: constant negative curvature is
  contradicted by delta_rel_max 0.386330 sitting *past* the flat anchor 0.360433 (threshold
  0.036043). Also avoided: RSGD at ≥1 Isomap-fit wall-clock (78.5–104.5 s each), geoopt SUS behind
  a gate. Would change: a fit/sample/architecture whose delta lands near the tree anchor.
- **Mixed-curvature product** — same delta evidence per constant-curvature factor, plus an
  unresolved factor/signature model-selection cost; never in the pre-registered shortlist. Would
  change: a resolved signature plus a per-factor delta test.
- **Diffusion maps** — no evidence ever measured for it (recommending it would violate the
  evidence-strength prohibition); datafold heavier than the shortlist needed. Not eliminated by
  delta. Would change: running its own probe (~1 dense-eigensolve class) and measuring its
  distortion curve. Most plausible fallback if Phase 3 finds Krein wanting.
- **Ollivier-Ricci** — fork test: scalar per edge, no coordinate vector, nothing for DEC-01's
  decoder / CURV-01..02's Jacobian-Hessian path. Avoided: three SUS packages (networkx not even
  installed), an unbuilt fixture, Sinkhorn over 150,000 edges. Would change: both falsifier
  conditions tripping — the measured 18.4% is evidence *against* the switch.
- **Forman-Ricci** — identical fork disqualification; lower-cost variant of the same slot (no POT),
  not independently evaluated.

## GEOM-04 — Evidence It Will Be Judged Against

**Phase 3 checks:** (1) held-out decoder reconstruction (DEC-03/04) at the elbow dimension roughly
consistent with the ~12.1% median distortion of the (8,0) coordinates; much worse means the
representation, not the decoder, is the bottleneck. (2) Whether (40,25) — the 18.4% reduction to
6.5% — meaningfully improves reconstruction or curvature-field stability (CURV-04 conditioning,
CURV-05 non-degeneracy); no benefit would undercut the 18.4%-as-signal reading. (3) CURV-06/07
synthetic controls matched against this phase's coordinate domain, not Isomap's.

**Falsifier status.** Pre-registered: overturn requires BOTH (a) DELTA_REL far from the tree
anchor (≥ threshold) AND (b) the ladder never dropping materially below the q=0 baseline.
**(a) trips by a wide margin** (0.383921 > the flat anchor 0.360433). **(b) does not cleanly
trip** (real 18.4% reduction). One of two → **falsifier does not fire; the coordinate-producing
verdict stands as ratified.** Flagged as close to the edge: (a)'s margin eliminates hyperbolic;
(b)'s modest 18.4% is the entire quantitative basis for retention over the failed classical
baseline. If Phase 3's check (2) does not corroborate it, revisit via a dated amendment.

**Generality: architecture-specific as stated.** DINOv3 ViT-B/16 is the one variable never
varied; every number here was computed on it. The packaged 35-model sweep bears directly: other
architectures passing the gate cleanly → this recommendation is DINOv3-specific; a similar diffuse
pattern across architectures → supports generalising and strengthens the LIB-02 promotion case.

## GEOM-05 — Working Dimension

**Re-derived under the verbatim kneedle criterion: `(p, q) = (8, 0)`** on the
best-per-total-dimension frontier; the q=0 classical curve alone gives the identical
`classical_p = 8`. Reported as a pair per the rule; q=0 at this budget is itself the finding.

| Estimate | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Residual-curve elbow, frozen by Phase 2 | **D_FROZEN = 5** | 02-FINDINGS §5 (Tenenbaum curve) |
| Eigenvalue-based residual elbow (Phase 2) | 8 | §5; divergence 0.697664 at d=5 |
| This phase's classical (q=0) elbow | **8** | working_dimension.classical_p |
| This phase's pseudo-Euclidean frontier elbow | **(8, 0)** | working_dimension.krein_p/krein_q |
| Median of 8 geometric estimators (Phase 1) | 18 | 02-FINDINGS §6.4 |
| TwoNN | ~19.5 (19.48) | §6.3 |
| Local PCA 90% | ~25 (median 25.0, std 2.01, range 19–29) | §6.3 |

Two opposing readings, both stated: §6.4's (with 41% negative mass the Tenenbaum curve saturates
early — the elbow of 5 measured the failure, consistent with 0.697664 at exactly d=5) and
Entanglement item 2's (curved targets can need fewer dims — MAP=0.989 in 2 hyperbolic dims vs
200-dim Euclidean — so 18–25 does not refute 5 on its own).

**What the re-derivation settles:** two independently computed curves now agree on 8 (this phase's
distortion curve, under the identical criterion on a non-R² statistic, and Phase 2's own
eigenvalue-based curve); only the curve §6.4 already flagged as distorted by the negative mass
produced 5. **D_FROZEN = 5 is REVISED** — superseded by a directly comparable re-derivation, not
declared wrong on the 18–25 cluster's say-so. Dimension of record: **(8, 0)**.

The 18–25 cluster answers a different question (local tangent-space dimension vs a global
diminishing-returns budget). §6.4's dimension-starvation fact stands: n_components=18 sat below
the local intrinsic dimension (100% of neighbourhoods needed >18 dims for 90% local variance) and
constrains what any fixed-dimension embedding, including (8,0), represents of fine local structure.

**(8,0) vs (40,25), stated plainly:** the elbow retains zero negative directions; the best
measured distortion (0.065190 at (40,25)) sits in the flat tail — 57 extra dimensions buy 1.5
absolute points (8.0%→6.5%), exactly what the elbow rule rejects as diminishing returns. **The
pre-registered rule governs, not the lowest-absolute point** (Prohibited Moves forecloses picking
a convenient point after the evidence). This does not weaken retention: the representation still
never forces PSD, and (40,25)'s 18.4% remains available at a larger budget. Phase 3 default:
(8,0); documented alternative: (40,25) if decoder-side evidence supports the cost.
