## GEOM-03 — What 41% Negative Mass Means

Classical MDS double-centres squared distances into `B = -1/2 J D² J`; for genuinely Euclidean `D`,
`B` is PSD and each eigenvalue is a squared extent along a principal direction. A negative
eigenvalue is a direction along which the metric behaves as though squared distance were
*subtracted* — no real vector has negative squared norm. Isomap inherits this exactly: the negative
eigenvalues say the geodesic metric itself is not Euclidean-embeddable.

The shape is the finding: **5,029 of 10,000 eigenvalues strictly negative**, `m = 0.412071`;
`r = 0.052419` passes its 0.10 bound at every k (thresholds: r pass 0.10 / marginal 0.25, m pass 0.05 / marginal 0.15) — not one short-circuit edge but 5,029 directions,
none dominant, mass comparable to the positive part. §8's threshold-independent reading: **at 41%
the negative part is not a correction to a Euclidean picture, it is comparable in magnitude to the
positive part** — true under any reasonable threshold.

Corroboration (cited, not re-argued): `CURVE_DIVERGENCE_MAX = 0.697664` at d=5 between the two
residual curves (the eigenvalue-based curve, blind to the negative 41%, over-reports variance
captured); every negative magnitude 10–11 orders above the noise floor (7.174e-09 at k=15); four
experiments closed numerics (rtol=1e-8 vs sklearn), hop inflation (m flat-to-rising over k while
GEO_AMBIENT_RATIO fell 2.83→1.86 and LONG_EDGE_FRACTION rose 0.0065→0.0139), L2 normalization
(0.28% move), column (HSC m=0.4226), and draw (disjoint m=0.411948, identical counts).

**Limit carried forward:** §6.2 closes "normalization caused it", NOT "shell geometry contributes"
— raw norms already 16.029 ± 0.504 (cv 3.1%) before normalization. Unresolved here too.

## GEOM-03 — Correction or Retention

**Mechanism:** Cailliez shift, Lingoes additive constant, and clipping force PSD — and a passing
gate — on *any* input. **Shown, not asserted** (02.1-03's `correction_blindness`):

| Input | m before | m after clip | m after Lingoes shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real spectrum (audited fit) | 0.412071 | 0.0 | 0.0 |
| synthetic_flat (n=1,000, flat R²⁰; m ~4.05e-15) | ~0.0 | 0.0 | 0.0 |
| synthetic_nonmetric (n=1,000, random symmetric, no metric structure) | 0.500649 | 0.0 | 0.0 |

A real curved metric, an already-flat one, and pure non-metric noise all correct to identical
0.000000. A 100% pass rate is what corrections are engineered to guarantee, not evidence about the
geometry. For a project whose purpose is to *measure* curvature, silent adoption deletes the signal
and manufactures a false PASS.

**Judgment, tested against a stated condition.** A correction is principled when the negativity it
removes is known on independent grounds to be measurement error rather than geometry. This data
fails that condition: the four experiments eliminated every measurement-error explanation, and the
negativity survived at essentially constant magnitude (0.4064–0.4157 over 6× k; 0.4226 HSC;
0.411948 disjoint). **Retention, not correction.** A correction keeps one role: a labelled
cannot-fail contrast baseline — the table above is that baseline.

**The literature's alternative:** Neuc-MDS (2024, bilinear-form optimisation) — abstract-only
strength, no maintained implementation; the measured top-p/top-q retention is the lower-risk
fallback, not a compromise.

**Where retention now lands.** Retention was originally realised *as a representation* — Krein
coordinates carrying the sign structure. GEOM-04 below no longer recommends that, and the stance is
unchanged by the switch: retention here means **the negativity is never corrected away**, and the
graph-native branch honours that more strictly than any coordinate representation could. It never
forms `B`, never eigendecomposes it, and never asks the metric to be PSD — it measures curvature on
the geodesic graph directly, so there is nothing to correct in the first place.

## GEOM-04 — Recommendation

**Ollivier-Ricci curvature on the frozen k\*=15 geodesic graph.** Per-edge discrete Ricci curvature
`κ(x,y) = 1 − W₁(m_x, m_y)/d(x,y)`, where `m_x` is a lazy-random-walk probability measure on the
neighbourhood of `x`, `W₁` is the optimal-transport distance between neighbourhood measures, and
`d` is the graph geodesic distance already cached. No embedding, no coordinates, no flat target.

**Why this and not a coordinate representation.** The pre-registered falsifier fired
(`02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` §3): both conditions trip, and the pre-registration's own consequence is
that "the verdict is revisited toward graph-native despite its cost." The evidence underneath it:

| Arm | DISTORTION | Family |
|---|---|---|
| pseudo-Euclidean Krein (40,25), indefinite form | 0.065190 | indefinite-spectral |
| **metric SMACOF (d=18)** | **0.079605** | stress optimiser — no eigendecomposition, no PSD constraint |
| Isomap embedding (d=18) / classical MDS (18,0) | 0.079864 | classical-MDS |
| pseudo-Euclidean Krein (40,25), positive block only | 0.179641 | — signature discarded |
| Laplacian eigenmaps (d=18), optimally rescaled | 0.279918 | PSD-spectral |
| **pseudo-Euclidean Krein (40,25), Euclidean latent** | **0.393400** | — signature discarded |
| LLE standard (d=18), optimally rescaled | 0.420736 | PSD-spectral |
| non-metric MDS (d=18), optimally rescaled | 0.488891 | rank-order stress |
| hypersphere ambient great-circle (dim 767) | 0.769597 | ambient-Riemannian |
| hypersphere tangent PCA (best, d=65) | 0.780607 | ambient-Riemannian |

All arms on the same statistic and the same fixed 200,000-pair sample, `pair_identity_verified:
true` throughout.

Two readings carry the recommendation:

1. **The wall is the target, not the algorithm.** Four structurally unrelated families — classical
   spectral, stress majorization, PSD-spectral, ambient-Riemannian — reach ~0.0796 and stop. Metric
   SMACOF is decisive here: no eigendecomposition, no PSD requirement, a completely different
   optimiser, identical floor. Every one of them assumes a flat target. That assumption is the
   binding constraint, which is GEOM-01's thesis promoted from a literature argument to a measured
   one.

2. **The only sub-wall result cannot be spent.** Krein's 0.065190 is real and is purchased entirely
   by the indefinite signature. `signature_transfer_43cf438bc944c509.json`:
   `advantage_transfers_to_euclidean_latent = false`, `distortion_euclidean = 0.393400`,
   `signature_discard_penalty = 0.328210`. Phase 3's decoder has a Euclidean latent (DEC-01/02) and
   differentiates over a real coordinate domain under the ordinary quadratic form (CURV-01..03). It
   cannot consume an indefinite advantage.

   **Qualified by direct measurement — read this before weighting reading 2.** The decoder-side
   check (`02.1-DECODER-PREREGISTRATION.md`, rules fixed before any decoder trained) tested the
   proxy against the real thing. Krein (40,25) improves held-out reconstruction over the classical
   (18,0) flat floor by **+1.44%** (standardized) or **+0.10%** (raw), against the **+18.37%** its
   distance advantage promised — so the advantage is not reachable, which is reading 2's
   conclusion. But `signature_transfer`'s 0.393400 did **not** predict a decoder failure: measured
   directly, Krein reconstructs within a few percent of every other arm. The proxy pointed the right
   way for the wrong reason, and its weight should be lowered accordingly. What survives untouched
   is reading 1 — the ~0.0796 wall — and the matched-width signature control, which is negative
   (−2.07% / −3.61%): at equal input width the signature actively costs reconstruction.

Graph-native was set aside at the fork on requirement compatibility, never on quality or difficulty
(`02.1-FORK.md`: "Graph-native is set aside on requirement compatibility with its cost recorded
below, not on difficulty"). The falsifier firing is precisely the pre-registered mechanism for
revisiting that routing decision, and the fork document named this path in advance: "If both trip,
the verdict is revisited before 02.1-04's recommendation — a documented amendment, not a quiet
edit."

**Ollivier over Forman.** Ollivier is optimal-transport-based and metric-sensitive — it reads the
actual geodesic weights this milestone spent Phases 1–2 validating. Forman-Ricci is combinatorial,
cheaper (no POT dependency), and discards most of that metric information. Forman is retained as
the **cheap cross-check**, not the primary: agreement between a metric and a purely combinatorial
curvature raises confidence, disagreement localises where the weights matter. Running only Forman
would waste the validated geodesics.

**What this recommendation assumes that could be false:**

1. **That discrete Ollivier-Ricci on a kNN graph approximates the curvature of the underlying
   manifold.** The continuum-limit convergence claim (arXiv:2307.02378) is **[CITED]-strength,
   unread** — surfaced by search summary, not read this session. This recommendation does **not**
   lean on it; the case rests on the falsifier firing and on Ollivier being the only surveyed family
   that computes curvature without assuming a flat target. Any Phase 3 plan that leans on the
   continuum limit must read the paper first.
2. **That an intrinsic Ricci quantity answers the project's question.** It is not the extrinsic
   mean-curvature vector CURV-03 asks for — different mathematical objects, not one quantity
   computed two ways. This is the cost the fork document priced, and accepting the recommendation
   means accepting a rewritten CURV-03. If the milestone's purpose specifically requires an
   *extrinsic* curvature vector in the ambient 768-d space, this recommendation does not deliver it
   and the honest answer becomes that no surveyed representation does.
3. **That Sinkhorn entropic approximation preserves the curvature signal at ε small enough to be
   tractable over 150,000 edges.** Untested here. Exact OT is intractable at this scale, so the
   approximation is necessary rather than chosen; its regularisation bias must be measured against
   the fixture before any real number is trusted.
4. **That the k\*=15 graph is the right object to measure on.** It is the validated one and reuse is
   mandatory, but its own `n_components_no_headroom = true` flag and the dimension-starvation
   finding (`02-FINDINGS.md` §6.4) describe a graph built under constraints that were never
   curvature-motivated.

## GEOM-04 — Alternatives Rejected

None rejected on difficulty.

- **Pseudo-Euclidean / Krein retention** — *this document's own former recommendation.* Evaluated,
  measured, and rejected. It **won** under the pre-registered criterion: 0.065190 at (40,25), an
  18.4% relative reduction over the q=0 best of 0.079864, and it remains the only measured arm
  below the ~0.0796 wall. Rejected first by user directive (`02.1-AMENDMENT-01.md` §1.3), then on
  independent measured grounds, now tested two ways. The cheap proxy: the advantage is
  signature-carried and does not transfer to a Euclidean latent (0.393400;
  `signature_discard_penalty = 0.328210`). The expensive check, run before this phase sealed
  (`02.1-DECODER-PREREGISTRATION.md`): decoders trained on each representation under one protocol
  give Krein (40,25) a **+1.44%** held-out reconstruction improvement over the classical (18,0)
  floor on standardized inputs and **+0.10%** on raw, against the **+18.37%** its distance advantage
  promised. **The measured decoder-side margin is therefore roughly an order of magnitude smaller
  than the distance statistic implied, and within noise of zero on one of two preprocessings.** At
  matched 40-dimensional width the signature is actively negative (−2.07% / −3.61%). Also weighing
  against it: the elbow rung is (8,0) — zero negative directions, i.e. the ordinary top-8 classical
  embedding — so the pre-registered working dimension would have put Phase 3 on a flat
  representation anyway, with the 18.4% available only at (40,25), 57 extra dimensions into the flat
  tail. **Would change the answer:** a Phase 3 decoder with an indefinite or signature-aware latent,
  or a Neuc-MDS-style optimised bilinear selection measurably better than top-p/top-q truncation.
  This is the closest loser by a wide margin and the first thing to revisit if the graph-native
  branch stalls.
- **Metric SMACOF** — the best decoder-consumable arm at 0.079605, statistically indistinguishable
  from Isomap's own 0.079864. Rejected because it *is* the wall: it reproduces the classical result
  that Phase 2 already gated as FAIL (m = 0.412071), by a different route. Adopting it would carry
  the failed geometry into Phase 3 under a new name. **Would change:** nothing available — its
  result is evidence about the target space, not about the optimiser.
- **Hyperbolic (Poincaré/Lorentz)** — rejected on evidence: constant negative curvature is
  contradicted by `delta_rel_max = 0.383921` (n=10,000; 0.386330 at matched n=2,000) sitting *past*
  the flat-Euclidean anchor 0.360433, against a threshold of 0.036043. This geometry is *less*
  tree-like than flat space under the pre-registered estimator; `single_curvature_defensible =
  False`. Also avoided: RSGD at ≥1 Isomap-fit wall-clock (78.5–104.5 s each), geoopt SUS behind a
  gate. **Would change:** a fit, sample, or architecture whose delta lands near the tree anchor.
- **Mixed-curvature product** — same delta evidence per constant-curvature factor, plus an
  unresolved factor/signature model-selection cost; never in the pre-registered shortlist. **Would
  change:** a resolved signature plus a per-factor delta test.
- **Ambient Riemannian via geomstats (hypersphere)** — measured and lost by an order of magnitude:
  great-circle 0.769597, tangent PCA 0.780607 at its best d=65. Not a verdict on the library. Both
  arms measure *ambient sphere* geometry: great-circle distance ignores the ~20–25-d manifold inside
  S^767, and tangent PCA at the Fréchet mean is a global linear projection. geomstats has no
  fit-to-a-geodesic-matrix primitive — a toolkit for calculus on an already-specified manifold,
  potentially useful to Phase 3 *after* a representation is chosen, wrong instrument for choosing
  one. Carries a known defect as a dependency: 2.8.0 imports `numpy.trapz`, removed in numpy 2.0,
  ImportError under the pinned 2.5.1, unfixed upstream, needs a shim. **Would change:** committing
  to a specific manifold and fitting points to it — Phase 3 pipeline work this phase excludes.
- **Laplacian eigenmaps, LLE, non-metric MDS** — measured and lost even after re-scoring each with
  an optimal isotropic scale fitted to its own advantage (0.279918 / 0.420736 / 0.488891). Non-metric
  MDS is the instructive one: the textbook answer to non-Euclidean input, and its rank-order-only
  objective discards exactly the metric information being measured — it barely improved under
  optimal scaling (0.517754 → 0.488891), so its poor result is real and not a scale artifact.
  **Would change:** nothing; these are structural properties of the objectives.
- **Diffusion maps** — never measured, so recommending it would violate the evidence-strength
  prohibition. Not eliminated by delta; datafold is Supporting-tier and heavier than the shortlist
  needed. Remains the most plausible *coordinate-producing* fallback if the graph-native branch
  stalls, but it is a flat-target method and the SMACOF result predicts it hits the same wall.
  **Would change:** running its own probe (~1 dense-eigensolve class) and measuring its distortion
  curve.
- **Forman-Ricci** — not rejected. Retained as the cheap combinatorial cross-check on the
  recommended branch (see GEOM-04 above). It loses the *primary* slot to Ollivier because it
  discards the validated geodesic weights.

## GEOM-04 — Evidence It Will Be Judged Against

**Checks Phase 3 can run, in order:**

1. **Fixture before data.** A synthetic weighted tree (known negative curvature) and a near-regular
   expander (known near-zero/positive) at matched scale and edge count. No curvature number computed
   on the real graph is trusted until the estimator recovers the known sign and rough magnitude on
   both. This fixture is **design-only today** — building it is Phase 3's first task, not an
   assumed input.
2. **Sinkhorn bias.** The curvature distribution measured at ≥3 regularisation strengths ε, with the
   trend reported. If the sign structure moves with ε, the entropic approximation is contaminating
   the signal and the result is not usable at this scale.
3. **Ollivier–Forman agreement.** Per-edge rank correlation between the metric and combinatorial
   curvatures. Broad agreement raises confidence; disagreement localises where the geodesic weights
   carry information the combinatorics miss, which is itself a reportable finding.
4. **Curvature-distribution shape against the 41% negative mass.** The pre-registration scores
   graph-native on "whether they answer CURV-01..03's question and on curvature-distribution shape",
   explicitly **not** on the distortion axis — scoring it there is a category error the Prohibited
   Moves forbid. A predominantly negative Ollivier-Ricci distribution on the k\*=15 graph would be
   independently consistent with 5,029 negative eigenvalues carrying 41% of mass; a
   predominantly-positive or near-zero distribution would be a genuine tension between two
   independent measurements of the same geometry, and would need resolving before Phase 3 proceeds.

5. **The evaluation criterion itself needs re-examining on this branch.** The decoder-side check
   surfaced something no rule attaches to and this document must not bury: **held-out reconstruction
   was nearly decoupled from the distance-distortion statistic the whole phase ranked
   representations by.** Classical (40,0), whose distance distortion of 0.179641 is the worst of the
   three ladder rungs, reconstructed *best* of all four arms on both preprocessings. Distortion
   spanned 2.75× across the arms; MSE spanned about 6%. One seed was run, so few-percent gaps cannot
   be separated from initialisation noise, and decoder capacity saturation is not excluded — this is
   an observation, not a verdict. But if a distance statistic does not predict what a decoder does
   with a representation, then ranking representations by it is weaker evidence than this phase
   treated it as, in **both** directions: it weakens the original case for Krein and it weakens the
   distortion-framed half of the case against it. The graph-native branch is scored on
   curvature-distribution shape rather than distortion (Prohibited Moves forbids the distortion axis
   here), so this does not transfer directly — but Phase 3 should not assume the criterion that
   selected its predecessor was measuring the right thing.

**Falsifier status.** Under `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md`'s amended reading of condition (b): **(a) trips**
(0.383921 past the flat anchor 0.360433, threshold 0.036043) and **(b) trips** (no rung drops
materially below the q=0 baseline in a Euclidean-latent-consumable form). Both → the falsifier
fires and the coordinate-producing verdict is overturned. Under the *un-amended* reading (b) does
not trip and the coordinate-producing verdict stands with Krein as its winner; that reading is fully
recoverable and `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` §6 states how to discount the amendment.

The pre-registration's symmetric falsifier now governs this branch: **graph-native is overturned by
a coordinate candidate whose DISTORTION drops materially below the classical failure at a tractable
working dimension** — in a form Phase 3 can consume, per the amended reading. Krein at (40,25) meets
the first half and fails the second. A signature-aware decoder would reopen it.

**Generality: architecture-specific as stated.** DINOv3 ViT-B/16 is the one variable never varied;
every number here was computed on that one architecture's embedding of this one astronomical
population. The packaged 35-model cross-architecture sweep (`sweep/`,
`02-MODEL-SWEEP-PREREGISTRATION.md`, not yet run) bears directly: other architectures passing the
Phase 2 gate cleanly → this recommendation is DINOv3-specific; a similar diffuse negative-mass
pattern across architectures → supports generalising, and strengthens the LIB-02 promotion case.

## GEOM-05 — Working Dimension

**The graph-native branch has no embedding dimension. `D_FROZEN = 5` is DISCARDED as inapplicable —
not declared wrong.**

Ollivier-Ricci assigns a scalar to each of the k\*=15 graph's 150,000 edges. There is no coordinate
vector, no target space, and therefore no dimension to freeze or sweep. The question GEOM-05 asks
dissolves on this branch rather than receiving a different numeric answer, and saying so is the
honest reading of the pre-registered prohibition — which forbids *declaring* `D_FROZEN = 5` wrong or
*inheriting* it silently, and is satisfied by neither happening here.

**The re-derivation was still run, and is preserved.** Under the verbatim kneedle criterion on this
phase's own distortion-vs-dimension curve, the coordinate branch lands on `(p, q) = (8, 0)`; the
q=0 classical curve alone gives the identical `classical_p = 8`. That number is the answer GEOM-05
would carry if `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` is rejected, and it is recorded here so rejecting the
amendment costs no re-derivation:

| Estimate | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Residual-curve elbow, frozen by Phase 2 | **D_FROZEN = 5** | 02-FINDINGS §5 (Tenenbaum curve) |
| Eigenvalue-based residual elbow (Phase 2) | 8 | §5; divergence 0.697664 at d=5 |
| This phase's classical (q=0) elbow | **8** | `working_dimension.classical_p` |
| This phase's pseudo-Euclidean frontier elbow | **(8, 0)** | `working_dimension.krein_p/krein_q` |
| Median of 8 geometric estimators (Phase 1) | 18 | 02-FINDINGS §6.4 |
| TwoNN | ~19.5 (19.48) | §6.3 |
| Local PCA 90% | ~25 (median 25.0, std 2.01, range 19–29) | §6.3 |

Two opposing readings of `D_FROZEN = 5` were and remain on the record: §6.4's (with 41% negative
mass the Tenenbaum curve saturates early — the elbow of 5 measured the failure, consistent with
`CURVE_DIVERGENCE_MAX = 0.697664` at exactly d=5) and Entanglement item 2's (curved targets can need
fewer dimensions than flat ones — MAP = 0.989 in 2 hyperbolic dims vs 200-dim Euclidean — so 18–25
does not refute 5 on its own). On the coordinate branch the re-derivation settled it: two
independently computed curves agree on 8, and only the curve §6.4 already flagged as distorted
produced 5. On the recommended branch neither reading applies to anything Phase 3 builds.

**What replaces dimension as the branch's scale parameter.** Three quantities Phase 3 must fix and
report, none of them a dimension:

- **The graph:** `k* = 15`, exactly 150,000 edges, `codiag_k15_43cf438bc944c509.npz`. Frozen and
  mandatory — rebuilding at k≠15 reopens the closed k-sensitivity question.
- **The random-walk laziness α** in `m_x = α·δ_x + (1−α)·uniform(N(x))`, the standard Ollivier
  parameter. Must be pre-registered before any curvature number is computed, and its sensitivity
  reported.
- **The Sinkhorn regularisation ε**, with the bias check of Evidence item 2 above.

**The intrinsic-dimension cluster keeps a role, but not this one.** Local PCA ~25, TwoNN ~19.5, and
the estimator median of 18 remain the best available description of the manifold, and
`02-FINDINGS.md` §6.4's dimension-starvation fact stands: `n_components = 18` sat below the local
intrinsic dimension (100% of neighbourhoods needed >18 dims for 90% local variance). On this branch
that fact describes the *graph's* construction, not a representation's width — a caveat on how well
the k\*=15 neighbourhoods resolve local structure, which is exactly what Ollivier-Ricci integrates
over. It belongs in Phase 3's threats, not in its dimension.

## Phase 3 Re-specification Brief

**Representation:** Ollivier-Ricci discrete curvature on the frozen k\*=15 geodesic graph, with
Forman-Ricci as a combinatorial cross-check. Intrinsic, per-edge, no embedding.

**Working dimension:** none — not applicable on this branch. `D_FROZEN = 5` discarded as
inapplicable. Scale parameters instead: `k* = 15` (frozen), random-walk laziness α
(pre-register before measuring), Sinkhorn ε (with bias sweep).

**Retention stance:** retain. Never corrected; on this branch never even formed as a Gram matrix.

**First step — build the fixture, not the measurement.** A synthetic weighted tree and a
near-regular expander at matched scale and edge count, with the estimator validated to recover known
sign and rough magnitude on both, before any curvature is computed on the real graph. Then the
Sinkhorn ε sweep. Only then the k\*=15 graph.

**Swiss roll sanity check — required, and it is not the fixture.** `CLAUDE.md` names curvature
estimators explicitly: any new manifold-learning model ships with
`notebooks/<phase>_swiss_roll_<model>_check.ipynb` before it is considered done. Neither template
in that rule fits a per-edge curvature — there is no reconstruction to plot and no embedding to
unroll — so the check takes the estimator's own known-answer form: **the Swiss roll is a
developable surface, intrinsically flat, so Ollivier-Ricci on a kNN graph over it must return a
curvature distribution centred on zero.** A systematically non-zero result on a flat sheet means
the implementation, the laziness α, or the Sinkhorn ε is producing curvature that is not there.
This is distinct from, and additional to, the tree-and-expander fixture of Evidence item 1: the
fixture tests that known *non-zero* curvature is recovered with the right sign, the Swiss roll
tests that known *zero* curvature is not manufactured. Both are cheap; neither substitutes for the
other.

**Installs required, each behind its own blocking `checkpoint:human-verify` gate — none
pre-approved by this document:**

- `GraphRicciCurvature` — absent, SUS.
- `POT` (Python Optimal Transport) — absent, SUS.
- `networkx` — **already present** at 3.6.1 in `.venv`, arrived transitively.
  `02.1-FORK.md` recorded it as "absent entirely"; that is now out of date, and the branch's
  install cost is two packages, not three.

selected):**
