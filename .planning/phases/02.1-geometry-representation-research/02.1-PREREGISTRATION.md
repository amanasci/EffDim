## Fork Test

A candidate is **coordinate-producing** iff it yields, per point, a finite real coordinate vector
from which a twice-differentiable map into R^768 can be fitted — what DEC-01's C2 decoder and
CURV-01..03's batched Jacobian/Hessian path consume. Per-node/per-edge scalars only =
**graph-native**. This is a *type* question (can Phase 3's decoder be built at all), not a quality
question — quality belongs to the criterion below. Failing relocates, not disqualifies: a
coordinate-producing winner re-specifies Phase 3's input (DEC/CURV intact, "Isomap coordinates"
amended); a graph-native winner replaces CURV-03's extrinsic mean-curvature vector with intrinsic
Ricci and re-opens the requirement set. Forecloses equating "graph-native lost" with "was worse" —
this is a routing decision with an asymmetric consequence.

## Evaluation Criterion

**Two arguments, not one score.** Coordinate-producing: relative squared-geodesic distortion over
the fixed 200,000-pair sample (`r2_pair_seed = 20260731`, cached `geo_pairs_r2`). Graph-native:
NOT scored on that axis (they do not attempt distance preservation — a number there is a category
error); judged on whether they answer CURV-01..03's question and on curvature-distribution shape.
Registering the incommensurability now removes the option of inventing a unified score whose
weights flatter a branch. Forecloses any single ranked table across both branches.

## Distortion Statistic

    DISTORTION        = median( |d2_rep - d2_geo| / d2_geo )
    DISTORTION_SIGNED = median( (d2_rep - d2_geo) / d2_geo )

Both over the 200,000-pair sample, on **squared** distances (what classical MDS and its
pseudo-Euclidean generalisation reconstruct exactly). The signed companion exposes systematic
bias the absolute median hides. Forecloses reporting one without the other.

## Working-Dimension Rule

*(GEOM-05.)* Re-derived **per candidate** from its own distortion-vs-dimension curve, with the
kneedle criterion quoted verbatim from `gate_verdict_43cf438bc944c509.json`:

> "Maximum-curvature (kneedle) elbow on the Tenenbaum residual-variance curve (1 - R^2 between
> geodesic and embedded pairwise distances): both axes are normalized to [0, 1] by their own
> range, and the elbow is the point of greatest perpendicular distance from the chord connecting
> the curve's first and last normalized point. Swept over d = 1..40 (K_EFF, bounded by
> D_SWEEP_MAX=40 and N_POSITIVE=4971); ties broken to the lower d (ELBOW_TIE_BREAK='lower')."

Pseudo-Euclidean dimension is a **pair (p, q)**, never collapsed — collapsing discards the sign
distinction the representation exists to make. **D_FROZEN=5 is neither inherited nor declared
wrong** — re-derived and placed side by side: §6.4 shows 5 is the outlier vs three estimates (local PCA ~25, TwoNN ~19.5, estimator median 18), but a curved
target can need fewer dimensions than a flat one, so 18-25 does not refute 5 either; only the
re-derivation settles it without begging the question. Forecloses stating a dimension without
running this rule on that candidate's own curve.

**The (p, q) ladder:**

    P_LADDER = (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 18, 25, 40)
    Q_LADDER = (0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 25, 40)

Ceiling 40 = Phase 2's `d_sweep_max` = cached `eigvecs_top` width, so the ladder costs one
bottom-40 eigensolve. `q = 0` retained deliberately — it IS the classical-MDS baseline, in the
same table so a reader sees what the negative directions buy.

## Delta-Hyperbolicity Reading Rule

    DELTA_REL = 2 * delta / diam_sample        (Gromov four-point; max and 95th pct)
    DELTA_QUADRUPLES = 200_000
    DELTA_SEED = 20260801

Read against two same-estimator, matched-n anchors: a synthetic weighted tree (delta ≡ 0) and a
flat 20-d Euclidean sample. Pure hyperbolic supported iff real value ≤ tree anchor + 0.1 × (gap to
Euclidean anchor); otherwise mixed-curvature/product or pseudo-Euclidean preferred. Two anchors
control for estimator bias at this n and quadruple count — the same move as Phase 2's refusal to
threshold `GEO_AMBIENT_RATIO` after seeing k=15. Forecloses any threshold invented after the
number exists.

## Shortlist Rule

At most three candidates into the evidence stage, named now on fork test + RESEARCH.md maturity —
never on scores (none exist yet):

1. **Pseudo-Euclidean/Krein retention** — hand-rolled from the cached spectrum + one bottom-40
   eigensolve; zero new dependencies; the only candidate needing no install.
2. **Hyperbolic/mixed-curvature** (`geoopt`, torch-native) — evaluated first via the delta rule,
   which needs no fit and no install, before any embedding cost is spent.
3. **Ollivier-Ricci** (`GraphRicciCurvature` + `POT` + `networkx`) — if ever pursued, reuse of the
   k*=15 graph and its 150,000 edges is **mandatory** (another k re-opens the closed
   k-sensitivity question, `02-REFIT-PREREGISTRATION.md` Rule A).

Diffusion maps and Forman-Ricci are surveyed (GEOM-02 needs all six families) but not carried:
datafold is Supporting-tier and heavier than needed; Forman-Ricci is a lower-cost variant of the
already-covered graph-native slot.

## Falsifier

*(A verdict without a falsifier is a preference.)*

**Overturns coordinate-producing:** BOTH (a) real DELTA_REL far from the tree anchor —
indistinguishable from or closer to the flat-Euclidean anchor than the rule allows — AND (b) the
(p,q) ladder never dropping DISTORTION materially below q=0 (the baseline that already failed at
m=0.412071) at any rung up to 40. Both together mean no shortlisted coordinate candidate beats the
failed classical baseline, and the verdict is revisited toward graph-native despite its cost.

**Overturns graph-native (symmetry):** a coordinate candidate's DISTORTION dropping materially
below the classical failure at a tractable working dimension — the geometry IS coordinatizable,
and graph-native's strictly higher cost (three SUS installs, a fixture, Sinkhorn over 150,000
edges) is unjustified over the requirement-preserving alternative.

## Prohibited Moves

- **Revising any rule here after the fork/survey/probes exist** — a rule edited after evidence is
  indistinguishable from one that was always there; changes are dated amendments with a re-run.
- **Scoring graph-native on the distortion axis** — category error per the criterion.
- **Treating a clean UMAP/t-SNE picture as resolution** — their solvers structurally cannot expose
  the statistic; reassurance from them smuggles the flat-target assumption back in.
- **Adopting a correction (Cailliez/Lingoes/clipping/flipping) as the representation without an
  explicit principled-here argument** — they force a passing gate on any input; silent adoption
  deletes the signal.
- **Declaring D_FROZEN=5 wrong instead of re-deriving** — 18-25 does not refute 5 on curved-target
  reasoning; only the rule settles it.
- **Rebuilding the graph at k≠15** — the k-sensitivity question is closed (FAIL at every k, m
  flat-to-rising); reopening it reintroduces a controlled variable.
