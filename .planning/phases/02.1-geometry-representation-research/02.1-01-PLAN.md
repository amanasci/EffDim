<constants_pre_registered>
Fixed by the planner before any evidence examined; Task 1 transcribes with reasoning.

- **Fork test:** coordinate-producing iff each of the 10,000 points gets a finite real coordinate
  vector a twice-differentiable map into R^768 can be fitted from; per-node/per-edge scalars only =
  graph-native. Failing relocates, not disqualifies.
- **Evaluation criterion:** two arguments, not one score. Coordinate-producing: relative
  squared-geodesic distortion over the fixed 200,000-pair sample (seed 20260731). Graph-native: NOT
  scored on that axis (category error); judged on whether they answer CURV-01..03's question and
  curvature-distribution shape. Incommensurability registered in advance.
- **Distortion statistic:** `DISTORTION = median(|d2_rep − d2_geo| / d2_geo)` plus the signed
  median, both on squared distances (what classical MDS and its pseudo-Euclidean generalisation
  reconstruct exactly).
- **Working-dimension rule (GEOM-05):** re-derived per candidate from its own
  distortion-vs-dimension curve with Phase 2's verbatim kneedle criterion; pseudo-Euclidean
  dimension is a pair (p, q), never collapsed. D_FROZEN=5 neither inherited nor declared wrong —
  re-derived and placed side by side (a curved target can need fewer dimensions than a flat one, so
  18-25 does not refute 5 on its own).
- **Delta-hyperbolicity reading rule:** `DELTA_REL = 2·delta/diam_sample` at max and 95th pct over
  `DELTA_QUADRUPLES = 200_000` quadruples, `DELTA_SEED = 20260801`; read against two same-estimator
  anchors (weighted tree = 0; flat 20-d Euclidean). Pure hyperbolic supported iff real value ≤ tree
  anchor + 0.1×(gap to Euclidean anchor).
- **(p,q) ladder:** `P_LADDER = (1,2,3,5,8,13,18,25,40)`, `Q_LADDER = (0,1,2,3,5,8,13,25,40)`,
  ceiling 40 = d_sweep_max = cached eigvecs_top width; q=0 is the classical-MDS baseline row.
- **Shortlist rule:** at most three candidates, named before evidence, on fork test + RESEARCH.md
  maturity.
- **Dependency scope constant:** nothing outside the venv (numpy 2.5.1, scipy 1.18.0, scikit-learn
  1.9.0, torch 2.13.0+cpu, joblib, pytest 9.1.1); all seven candidate packages SUS; genuine need =
  blocking human-verify checkpoint, never a quiet pip install.
- **Falsifier:** stated before the verdict — what finding overturns each branch. A verdict without a
  falsifier is a preference.
