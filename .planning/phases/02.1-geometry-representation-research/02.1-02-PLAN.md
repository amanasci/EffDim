<objective>
Write the phase's two survey deliverables: the flat-target class-membership analysis (GEOM-01) and
the non-flat-target candidate survey (GEOM-02).

Purpose: GEOM-01 exists to close a specific escape route. After Phase 2's FAIL the tempting next
move is to reach for a different manifold-learning method, get a clean-looking picture, and treat
that as the problem resolved. RESEARCH.md's Pitfall 1 and Summary are blunt about why that fails:
LLE, Hessian LLE, LTSA, Laplacian eigenmaps and MVU are PSD-constrained by construction, and t-SNE
and UMAP are probability-based — none can produce the negative-eigenvalue statistic that made the
problem visible in the first place. Their silence is structural, not evidential. Stating that
precisely turns Phase 2's result from "our Isomap run failed" into "this method class cannot
describe this geometry," the claim Phase 3 is being re-specified on.

GEOM-02 exists so the recommendation in plan 02.1-04 is a choice among costed alternatives rather
than an assertion. The fork verdict from plan 02.1-01 has already established which branch the
milestone is on; the survey must still describe both branches fully, since GEOM-02's requirement
text asks for every candidate that does not assume a flat target, with assumptions, cost and
Phase 3 demand, and a future reader who wants to reopen the fork needs the losing branch's numbers.

This plan writes prose, not pipeline code. Its verification is structural (every required section
and candidate present, every cost cell quantified) plus human reading — the honest position for a
narrative deliverable, exactly what `02.1-VALIDATION.md` records.

Output: `.planning/phases/02.1-geometry-representation-research/02.1-SURVEY.md`.

Task budget: 2 tasks, ~20% context each. Roughly 40-45% total. The reading is the expensive part,
not the writing.
</objective>
