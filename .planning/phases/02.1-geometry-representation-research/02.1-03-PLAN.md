<objective>
Measure the three things that can be measured cheaply and honestly from artifacts already on disk,
so plan 02.1-04's judgments rest on numbers rather than on literature alone.

Purpose: `02.1-VALIDATION.md` is explicit that four of this phase's five requirements are narrative
and verified by human reading. It is equally explicit about what *is* automatable — any illustrative
computation the phase chooses to run — and about why it matters: an illustrative number that is
silently wrong is worse than no number. This plan is that computation, deliberately confined to what
the frozen Phase 2 cache already supports.

Three probes, each earning its place against a specific requirement:

**Correction blindness (GEOM-03).** RESEARCH.md's Pattern 1 and Pitfall 2 say Cailliez, Lingoes and
eigenvalue clipping force a passing gate on any input, curved or not, and are therefore
self-defeating for a project whose goal is to *measure* curvature. That can be demonstrated rather
than cited, in a handful of lines, by applying two corrections to the real spectrum and to two
synthetic controls and showing the negative mass is zero in every case. A demonstration is much
harder to wave away than an argument.

**Krein reconstruction and the (p, q) ladder (GEOM-03, GEOM-05).** The pseudo-Euclidean branch is the
one candidate whose evidence is nearly free: the eigenspectrum, the top-40 eigenvectors and the
200,000-pair geodesic sample are already cached, so the only new cost is one bottom-block eigensolve.
That buys a real distortion-versus-dimension curve for a representation that keeps both eigenvalue
signs, with the `q = 0` column sitting in the same table as the classical-MDS baseline. That curve is
what GEOM-05 actually asks for: a working dimension re-derived from the chosen representation's own
diagnostic rather than inherited from a flat embedding's elbow.

**Delta-hyperbolicity (GEOM-04).** RESEARCH.md's Pitfall 3 warns against fitting a single constant
negative curvature to data whose curvature is likely heterogeneous, and its Assumption A7 concedes
the heterogeneity claim is an inference from the diffuse-tail shape, not a measurement. Sampled
Gromov relative delta, read against a tree anchor and a flat-Euclidean anchor computed by the same
estimator at matched n, turns that inference into a measurement — cheaply, from a matrix on disk.

What this plan deliberately does not do: fit a hyperbolic or product-manifold embedding, or run
graph-native curvature. Both are pipeline work, ROADMAP.md states no production pipeline code is
expected in this phase, and both would require installing packages that all returned SUS. The survey
costs them; this plan measures only what the frozen cache already supports.

Output: a tested probe module, its Wave 0 fixtures, a runner, and two cached artifacts carrying every
number the recommendation will cite.

Task budget: 2 tasks. The tested module (~25% context) and the runner with its real-data execution
(~20%). Roughly 45% total.

**Two numerical gotchas Phase 2 already paid for**, recorded in STATE.md's decision log:

1. `np.asarray(dist_matrix_, dtype=np.float64)` on a read-only memmap returns a **view**, not a copy,
   because the dtype already matches — in-place arithmetic then fails or corrupts. Use
   `np.array(dist_matrix_, dtype=np.float64, copy=True)`. `gate_diagnostics.py`'s
   `spectrum_from_distmatrix` carries the fix with the comment explaining it; copy that shape.
2. Mean-form double-centring is what Phase 2 used, verified equal to the literal `-0.5 * J D2 J` form
   to `rtol = atol = 1e-12` on two independent inputs. Use the mean form:
   `B = -0.5 * (D2 - row_mean - col_mean + grand_mean)`.

    `sampled_delta_hyperbolicity(D, n_quadruples, seed)` draws quadruples vectorised, rejecting any
    quadruple with a repeated index, computes the three pair sums `d(a,b)+d(c,d)`, `d(a,c)+d(b,d)`,
    `d(a,d)+d(b,c)`, sorts them, and takes `delta = (largest - second largest) / 2`. Report
    `delta_max`, `delta_p95`, `delta_mean`, the sampled diameter, and the two relative forms
    `delta_rel = 2 * delta / diam_sample` at max and at the 95th percentile, plus `n_quadruples`. `D`
    may be a memmap; index it with fancy indexing and never materialise a copy. RESEARCH.md names the
    wrong way explicitly — exhaustive enumeration of all quadruples, intractable at n = 10,000 — so
    the sampling is the method and not a shortcut. Say so in the docstring, alongside the literature's
    reported practice of a few hundred to a few thousand quadruples and this project's much larger
    pre-registered count.

    `krein_mass_capture(eigvals, p_ladder, q_ladder)` returns, for each `(p, q)` rung, the fraction of
    total absolute eigenvalue mass captured by the `p` largest positive and the `q` most negative
    eigenvalues. This is the spectral half of the dimension question, needs no eigenvectors, and is
    therefore cheap and independently checkable.

    `pseudo_euclidean_sq_distances(eigvals_sel, eigvecs_sel, rows, cols)` computes the indefinite
    reconstruction: with coordinates `X = eigvecs_sel * sqrt(abs(eigvals_sel))` and signs
    `s = sign(eigvals_sel)`, the reconstructed squared distance for a pair is
    `sum_a s_a * (X[i, a] - X[j, a]) ** 2`. Return the signed squared distances. Do not take an
    absolute value and do not take a square root: in a pseudo-Euclidean space a squared distance is
    genuinely allowed to be negative, and hiding that behind `abs` or `sqrt` would erase the property
    the representation exists to express. State that in the docstring.

    `distortion_stats(d2_rep, d2_geo)` implements the pre-registered statistic exactly:
    `median(abs(d2_rep - d2_geo) / d2_geo)` and `median((d2_rep - d2_geo) / d2_geo)`, plus the 95th
    percentile of the absolute form.

    `correction_blindness(eigvals)` applies two corrections and reports `m` before and after each.
    Clipping sets every negative eigenvalue to zero. The Lingoes-type additive shift adds
    `abs(min(eigvals))` to every eigenvalue, the spectral form of adding a constant to squared
    off-diagonal distances, guaranteed to leave nothing negative. Return a dict carrying `m_before`,
    `m_after_clip`, `m_after_shift`, and the shift constant. The docstring must state that both
    corrections succeed on any input by construction, that this is why they cannot serve as evidence
    of flatness, and that Cailliez's constant-shift belongs to the same family for the same structural
    reason but is not implemented here because its exact constant requires an eigenproblem on a
    `2n x 2n` matrix — at n = 10,000 that is a 20,000 x 20,000 solve, and it would demonstrate nothing
