## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in frontmatter for the full, precise record. In short: `train_topoae` was not a faithful translation of the paper's training loss (missing jointly-trained `latent_norm`, missing per-batch ambient normalization, a spurious batch-size division, and a missing `1/2` factor); all four were fixed, tested, and the sweep + notebook were re-run against the corrected code. Float64 remains a deliberate, unchanged determinism divergence from the reference's float32.
