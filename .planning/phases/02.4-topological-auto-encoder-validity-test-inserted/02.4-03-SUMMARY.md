## Known Limitations (carried forward to 02.4-04's pre-registration)

These three caveats are durable, named limitations of this measurement -- not defects to fix, but facts plan 02.4-04 must inherit when it writes `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` and any later plan must account for when reasoning from these numbers.

**1. Absolute `r = 0.680` remains below the 0.8 bound originally set.** TopoAE beats the matched baseline (`0.471`) by a wide margin, but it does not clear the absolute bar the notebook also reports. Both facts stand together: the baseline win is real, and so is the absolute miss. Neither should be read as cancelling the other.

**2. The lambda selection rule is mis-specified for this method.** The rule ("largest lambda whose held-out `mse_per_dim` stays within 10% of the zero-lambda fit") cannot be satisfied by a model that degrades reconstruction by design -- it bound at the grid floor on both the pre-correction (broken) loss and the corrected loss, across both the original and the redrawn grid. `lambda=0.1` is not vacuous -- it buys a ~3.7x improvement in `loss_x_to_z` (5.394 -> 1.456) relative to the zero-lambda arm -- but the rule is functionally operating as "pick the smallest positive grid value," not as a principled selection criterion. **This is documented, not fixed.** The rule was correctly not retuned after seeing results, on either checkpoint round; changing it now, with the sweep already known, would forfeit exactly what pre-registration exists to protect. The rule stands as pre-registered, with this limitation named for 02.4-04 to carry forward verbatim.

**3. The gate measures a different normalization than training optimizes.** Training normalizes with `d_x/d_x.max()` (per-batch ambient) and the jointly-trained `latent_norm`; the T1 gate and this notebook's structural check instead use raw `d_x` (whole-set, unnormalized -- a deliberate, documented divergence for cross-model comparability, see `topological_fidelity`'s docstring) plus `latent_unit_scale(z)` (a separate, non-fitted statistic). The comparability rationale for this divergence is sound and stands. Its consequence: **`loss_z_to_x` is scale-sensitive and is measured under a normalization the model was never trained against -- it is NOT clean evidence about the model on its own.** The persistence-pair edge-length correlation `r` is scale-free (invariant to any global rescale of either side, per `test_ambient_normalization_is_invariant_to_global_rescale`) and is the trustworthy number this plan's approved read rests on. Anything downstream (including any later phase reasoning about TopoAE's `loss_x_to_z`/`loss_z_to_x` split) must account for this before treating those two numbers as decisive on their own.

**Float64 confirmation (per explicit request):** the training loss now matches the reference implementation (github.com/BorgwardtLab/topological-autoencoders) exactly in every respect fixed by the round-1 correction (the `1/2` factor, ambient/latent normalization, no batch-size division). Float64 (vs. the reference's float32) remains the **one deliberate, retained divergence**, kept for the tie-break determinism the whole `persistence_pairs`/`topological_loss` machinery depends on (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1/10). It was not touched by either correction round and is not a limitation -- it is a stated design choice.
