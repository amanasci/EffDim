structure, and every *local*-scoped gate that was measured has passed.** This is not a
new measurement — every number below is copied from its own sealed source artifact and
independently re-verified against that artifact while writing this document, not
asserted from memory or from the prompt that requested it. No sealed verdict is
reopened, softened, or recomputed by this document. Phase 2, Phase 02.2, and Phase 02.4

*mean* for work downstream — specifically, Phase 3's re-scoped goal (`.planning/ROADMAP.md`'s
dated re-scope entry, written alongside this document) of estimating curvature
**locally, per point**, rather than from a single global chart.

## 2. Four points, each supported by the record rather than asserted

**2.1 No local-scoped gate has ever failed in this milestone.** Two local-scoped gates
have been measured — 02.2's chart-transition cycle residual (T2) and 02.4's rank
structure (T3) — and both passed, comfortably (`1.089366` against a `2.0` ceiling;
`0.671980` against a `0.90` ceiling). Zero local-scoped gates have failed. Every FAIL
recorded in this milestone (Phase 2's `m`, 02.2's T1/T3, 02.4's T1/T2) tested a
whole-set or whole-embedding statistic.

**2.2 Phase 2's negative eigenvalue mass is the expected signature of a *curved*
manifold, not evidence of "no structure."** Classical MDS assumes the geodesic
distance matrix is Euclidean-embeddable; a manifold with nonzero curvature has geodesic
distances that are *not* exactly Euclidean-embeddable in any finite dimension, which is
precisely what shows up as negative eigenvalue mass in the double-centred Gram matrix.
`GATE_VERDICT = FAIL` on `m = 0.412071` tested *flatness* and found *not-flat* — this
was subsequently read, through Phases 02.1/02.2/02.4, as "this manifold has no usable
structure a decoder could parameterise." The record supports only the weaker claim: "not
a globally flat, single-chart-Isomap-coordinatizable manifold." For a milestone whose
stated purpose (`PROJECT.md`) is measuring curvature, that distinction is material — a
curved manifold is exactly the kind of object this milestone exists to characterise, not
a degenerate case it should have ruled out.

Two pieces of corroboration, both already on the record and neither newly measured
here:

- `D_FROZEN = 5` is separately flagged suspect in `02-FINDINGS.md` §6.4 and in
  `.planning/STATE.md` ("`D_FROZEN=5 IS SUSPECT`") against three independent intrinsic-
  dimension estimates clustering at **18–25** (local PCA median `25.0`, TwoNN `19.5`,
  Phase 1's eight `compute_dim` geometric estimators at `18`) — the residual-curve elbow
  that produced `D_FROZEN=5` is the outlier, not the consensus, and the suspected cause
  (per `STATE.md`) is that the Tenenbaum residual curve saturates early under 41%
  negative mass, so the elbow measured the flatness failure, not the manifold's real
  dimension.
- 02.4's own T3 rank-structure ratio improves (falls, since lower is better under this
  gate's inversion) **monotonically with latent dimension across every rung from 8 to
  32** — `0.7696` (`d=8`) → `0.7660` (`d=16`) → `0.6897` (`d=24`) → `0.6409` (`d=32`),
  ticking back up slightly to `0.6647` at `d=40` (`topoae_verdict_43cf438bc944c509.json`
  `context_metrics.frontier.rungs`, re-read directly, not estimated). A representation
  that keeps getting *better* at preserving local neighbourhood rank as it is given more
  room, up to `d=32`, is weak but genuine independent evidence that the intrinsic
  dimension this manifold actually needs sits well above `D_FROZEN=5` — consistent with,
  not identical to, the 18–25 cluster above. This is reported as corroboration, not as a
  re-measurement of intrinsic dimension; no new statistic was computed to produce it.

**2.3 Phase 02.1's falsifier overturned the *coordinate-producing* fork, but it tested
only *global* coordinates — local charts were never in its scope.** `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md`
§3 fires the falsifier on two conditions, both computed on `DISTORTION`, a **global**
pairwise-distance statistic (`median(|d²_rep − d²_geo|/d²_geo)` over 200,000 sampled
pairs across the whole embedding) — the falsifier never evaluates any local-chart or
per-neighbourhood quantity. Reading "no global coordinates beat the classical wall" as
"no coordinates of any kind survive" over-reads what was tested. Separately,
`02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` §6.4 itself records the falsifier's own ranking criterion as
suspect: held-out reconstruction was found "nearly **decoupled**" from the `DISTORTION`
statistic the falsifier and the whole phase ranked candidates by — classical `(40,0)`,
the *worst*-distortion rung of the three tested, reconstructed *best* of all four arms
on both preprocessings (distortion spanned `2.75×` across arms; MSE spanned about `6%`).
One seed was run, so this is recorded there as an observation, not a verdict — but it
means the very statistic that produced the falsifier's global "no coordinates work"
finding is itself flagged, on the sealed record, as a possibly poor predictor of what a
decoder actually does with a representation.

**2.4 02.2's Chart Auto-Encoder is structurally an atlas of local charts, its local
chart-transition gate passed, and its Swiss roll notebook reconstructs the roll well —
so its FAIL is specifically about global gluing, not about local charts.** The CAE
architecture (`02.2-PREREGISTRATION.md`, `cae.py`) is, by the paper's own design
(arXiv:1912.10094), a set of over-specified local chart encoder/decoder pairs plus a
chart-predictor partition of unity — a local representation by construction, not a
single global embedding. Its local-consistency gate (T2, chart-transition cycle
residual) passed with room to spare (`1.089366 < 2.0`). Its mandatory Swiss roll sanity
check (`notebooks/02.2_swiss_roll_cae_check.ipynb`, its committed output cells read
directly while writing this document, not re-executed) reports: held-out relative
reconstruction error `4.8%` (bound `<10%`), `2.2×` better than a matched plain-AE
baseline, `8/8` charts surviving pruning, and prints "Swiss roll recovered: True." What failed for 02.2 (T1 global geodesic distortion,
T3 global reconstruction margin against baselines on the real PU data) is specifically
the *global* reconstruction/distortion measurement on real data at scale — not the local
chart machinery, which both the T2 gate and the Swiss roll check show functioning as
designed.

## 3. What this licenses, stated plainly — and what it does not

**Does not:** reopen, soften, or recompute any sealed verdict. `GATE_VERDICT = FAIL`
(Phase 2), `CAE_VERDICT = FAIL` (02.2), and `TOPOAE_VERDICT = FAIL` (02.4) are unchanged
by this document and remain the milestone's permanent, measured record. No threshold,
constant, seed, or fit is touched here.

**Does:** change what those three FAILs are read to *mean* for work downstream. All
three are failures of a **global** statistic — flat-target Euclidean embeddability
(Phase 2), whole-embedding distance/reconstruction fidelity (02.2's T1/T3), and
whole-held-out-set topological/reconstruction fidelity (02.4's T1/T2). None of them is a
failure of *local* structure, and every local-scoped gate measured in this milestone
(02.2's T2, 02.4's T3) passed. A downstream use case that depends on local fidelity —
concretely, estimating mean curvature at a point, which by definition depends only on an
arbitrarily small neighbourhood of that point (see the accompanying `ROADMAP.md`
re-scope of Phase 3) — is not automatically disqualified by these FAILs the way a use
case depending on global coordinates would be. This is the distinction the coordinator's
directive asked to be made explicit and not collapsed into a generic "FAIL, therefore
nothing survives" reading.
