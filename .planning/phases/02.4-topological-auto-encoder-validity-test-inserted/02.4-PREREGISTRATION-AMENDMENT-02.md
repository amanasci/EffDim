# Phase 02.4 Topological Auto-Encoder -- Pre-Registration Amendment 2

**Ratified 2026-08-07.** This document withdraws a *stated justification* in
`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md`'s prose. It changes no pre-registered constant, threshold,
seed, ladder entry, or fit schedule, and no fit is re-run. Per Section 10 of
`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` -- "none of its constants may move without a fresh,
separately-ratified pre-registration and a full re-run of all sixteen fits" -- this
amendment is filed openly, as its own separately-committed document with its own
ancestry proof, precisely because that is the correct procedure even for a
prose-only correction that touches no constant: silently editing the pre-registration's
prose after the fact would be indistinguishable from a rule that was never really fixed.
`744c1c1d73a9e788a67768e2b397ad453045062a`, `9f5bd9e` (Erratum 1), and
`9f9a74ad5dce169ac8900f8238db07c198f2781d` (Amendment 1) all remain in the record,
unmodified and unsuperseded. `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` and
`02.4-PREREGISTRATION-AMENDMENT-01.md` are not edited by this amendment.

## 1. The withdrawn justification

`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 8, Known Limitation 2, states (per the "Reframing, per
the coordinator's decisive context on Task 1" paragraph, itself propagated from plan
02.4-04): the lambda sweep grid `(0.0, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0, 3.0)` spans the paper's own
log-uniform `[0.1, 3]` search range (Moor et al., ICML 2020), so `LAMBDA_TOPO = 0.1` is
"the smallest lambda the paper's own authors considered" -- not an arbitrary grid floor,
but the authors' own searched minimum.

**This justification is withdrawn.** It was the orchestrator's error, propagated through
plan 02.4-04 rather than measured by any plan's own fit. `02.4-07-SUMMARY.md`'s
"Correction" section and `WINDOWS.md` entry #2 already record the withdrawal for
plan 02.4-07's own output; this amendment is the pre-registration-side correction that
`02.4-07-SUMMARY.md` explicitly deferred to this plan ("`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` itself
is corrected by plan 02.4-08, not by this plan").

## 2. Why it is withdrawn -- the fifth fidelity gap

Re-audit of `notebooks/pu_manifold/topoae.py`'s training loop against the reference
implementation (`github.com/BorgwardtLab/topological-autoencoders`,
`src/models/submodules.py`) found a fifth fidelity gap, beyond the four gaps plan
02.4-03 already closed:

- The reference's reconstruction term is `self.reconst_error = nn.MSELoss()` --
  **mean** over both the batch dimension and the ambient feature dimension.
- `topoae.py`'s `train_topoae` (line ~375) computes
  `((xb - model.decode(z.float()).double()) ** 2).sum(-1).mean()` -- **sum** over the
  ambient feature dimension, mean over the batch dimension only.

EffDim's reconstruction loss is therefore `D` times the reference's (`D = 768`, the
ambient dimension for the PU data; `D = 3` for the Swiss roll fixture), which
reparameterizes `lambda_topo` by a factor of `D` relative to the paper's own convention:
`LAMBDA_TOPO = 0.1` (EffDim's convention) corresponds to a paper-convention lambda of
roughly `0.1 / D` -- about `0.033` on the 3-dimensional Swiss roll fixture (`02.4-03`'s
lambda sweep), and about `1.3e-4` on the 768-dimensional PU embedding (every fit plan
02.4-05/06/07 trained) -- **both far below** the paper's searched `[0.1, 3]` range, not
at its floor. The withdrawn justification had the direction backwards: `LAMBDA_TOPO =
0.1` is not the paper's own minimum searched value; under the paper's own convention for
what the reconstruction term measures, it sits roughly three to four orders of magnitude
below that range.

## 3. Both halves of the correction stand together, deliberately

**Half one:** the "paper's own minimum searched value" justification for
`LAMBDA_TOPO = 0.1` in `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 8 Known Limitation 2 is
withdrawn.

**Half two:** this does **not** mean the topological term was inactive or negligible
during training. The topo-to-recon *ratio* -- `_transfer_ratio_from_history`, the
quantity `02.4-03-SUMMARY.md`'s lambda sweep and `02.4-05-SUMMARY.md`'s PU fits both
report -- is **invariant** under this feature-sum-vs-feature-mean reparameterization,
because it is a ratio of the two terms as actually computed under EffDim's own
convention, not a comparison to an external paper-convention scale. That ratio was
measured at `0.227701`-`0.313072` across the eight PU ladder rungs
(`02.4-05-SUMMARY.md`, `02.4-06-SUMMARY.md`) and `0.422840` on the Swiss roll fixture
(`02.4-03-SUMMARY.md`'s lambda sweep table, `lambda=0.1` row) -- the topological term
carried real, comparable weight in both settings, never an order-of-magnitude gap
between the two. **Gap #5 therefore does not explain 02.4-07's sealed `T1 = 1.026379`
FAIL** (the topological term losing to the matched baseline on whole-set topological
fidelity) -- the term was demonstrably active and weighted comparably to the Swiss roll
run that passed its own topological structural check. `TOPOAE_VERDICT = FAIL` is
unaffected by this correction and stands exactly as `02.4-07-SUMMARY.md` sealed it.

- **The ladder:** `LADDER` and `D_PRIMARY` unchanged.
- **The seeds:** `SEEDS_PRIMARY` and the sensitivity-rung seed (`20260806`) unchanged.

**Explicitly not touched, per the integrity constraint governing this document:** the

10's own stated consequence -- not authorised here. Gap #5 is recorded, not fixed.

## 5. Gap #5 recorded as a known, still-open divergence from the reference

Alongside the four deliberate, previously-recorded divergences from the reference
implementation:

1. **Float64** (vs. the reference's float32) -- retained for the tie-break determinism
   `persistence_pairs`/`topological_loss` depends on (`02.4-03-SUMMARY.md`).
2. **SiLU activation** (vs. the reference's ReLU-family choice, where used) -- required
   by this project's C2-smoothness rule (DEC-02, CURV-01..03), the same substitution and
   reporting obligation CAE-06 established (`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 4).
3. **No `BatchNorm` layer.** `notebooks/pu_manifold/cae.py`'s MLP builder (the backbone
   `topoae.py` reuses per Pattern 3, `02.4-RESEARCH.md`) contains no `nn.BatchNorm*`
   layer anywhere in the encoder or decoder -- confirmed by direct inspection of
   `cae.py` for this amendment. This is understood, per inherited context, to diverge
   from the reference implementation's own architecture; this session has no network
   access to re-fetch and re-read the reference source directly, so the reference
   side of this comparison is recorded as reported context, not independently
   re-verified against the reference repository in this amendment.
4. **No `Tanh` output activation.** `cae.py`'s MLP builder places an activation between
   linear layers and applies no final output activation -- confirmed by direct
   inspection for this amendment. Same caveat as (3): the reference side of this
   comparison is recorded as reported context, not re-verified here.

**Gap #5, this amendment's subject, joins this list as a fifth, newly-identified
divergence** -- unlike (1)-(2), which are deliberate design choices, and like (3)-(4),
gap #5 was not a deliberate choice but a translation defect found on re-audit
(`02.4-07-SUMMARY.md`'s "Correction" section). It remains **open**: recorded here and in
`WINDOWS.md` entry #2, not closed, since closing it (changing the reconstruction-term
reduction) requires a fresh pre-registration and a full re-run, which this amendment
does not authorise.
