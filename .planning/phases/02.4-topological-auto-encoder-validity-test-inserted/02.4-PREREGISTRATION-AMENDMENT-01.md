# Phase 02.4 Topological Auto-Encoder -- Pre-Registration Amendment 1

**Ratified 2026-08-07, after a defect found in the first full sixteen-fit PU run and
confirmed from the cached fit histories.** This document is an amendment, not an erratum:
Erratum 1 (`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 11) corrected a *misstatement about
already-implemented code*; this amendment *changes a rule* -- `train_topoae`'s stopping
behaviour -- and therefore follows `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 10's own stated
consequence for exactly this case: "none of its constants may move without a fresh,
separately-ratified pre-registration and a full re-run of all sixteen fits." Per the user's
decision, that is handled here as an openly-ratified amendment: a new, separately-committed
document with its own ancestry proof, not a silent fix and not folded into Erratum 1.

`744c1c1d73a9e788a67768e2b397ad453045062a` and `9f5bd9e` remain in the record, unmodified and
unsuperseded. `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` itself is not edited by this amendment.

## 1. The defect, confirmed from the cached fit histories

Every one of the eight TopoAE fits in the first sixteen-fit run (all three primary-rung
seeds at `d=20`, and one seed each at `d=8,16,24,32,40`) halted at **`epochs_run=15`** with
**`lambda_t` stuck at `0.05`** -- exactly half of the pre-registered `LAMBDA_TOPO = 0.1`.
Identical across six latent dimensions and three seeds, which is arithmetic, not
convergence.

The mechanism, confirmed from the cached `d=20`, `seed=20260806` history:

| epoch | lambda_t | recon | topo | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 0.000 | 0.053698 | 39.521551 | **0.053698** &larr; `best_loss` set here, under a recon-only objective |
| 10 | 0.010 | 0.066076 | 1.383911 | 0.079915 (plateau 1) |
| 11 | 0.020 | 0.054497 | 0.238511 | 0.059267 (plateau 2) |
| 12 | 0.030 | 0.052009 | 0.215766 | 0.058482 (plateau 3) |
| 13 | 0.040 | 0.049855 | 0.177496 | 0.056955 (plateau 4) |
| 14 | 0.050 | 0.048281 | 0.152376 | 0.055900 (plateau 5) &rarr; **STOP** |

With `WARMUP_FRAC=0.25`, `RAMP_FRAC=0.25`, `MAX_EPOCHS=40` (the resolved value from the
timing probe's pre-registered rule), warm-up is epochs 0-9 (`lambda_t=0`, so `total ==
recon`) and sets `best_loss = 0.053698` at epoch 9, the lowest recon-only value reached.
The ramp then adds `lambda_t * topo` starting epoch 10, so `total` restarts at `0.079915`
and, even though it falls every single epoch through epoch 14, never beats the epoch-9 mark
within `EARLY_STOP_PATIENCE = 5`. Warm-up (10 epochs) + patience (5 epochs) = 15, every
time -- exactly the observed `epochs_run=15` at every rung and seed.

**Two consequences, both load-bearing:**

1. **The fits were truncated mid-descent, not converged.** `recon` was still falling
   (`0.0499 -> 0.0483`) and `topo` had collapsed `39.5 -> 0.152` -- a 260x reduction in five
   ramp epochs -- and was still dropping steeply when the run stopped. The topological term
   was working, and working fast; it was cut off while still improving, at half the
   pre-registered lambda. This corrects plan `02.4-05`'s original Task 2 report, which read
   this as "converged cleanly, D-19's contingency did not fire" -- that reading was itself
   an artifact of the bug: nothing had converged, and the wrong contingency (wall-clock, not
   plateau-against-a-non-stationary-objective) was the one D-19 anticipated.
2. **The decisive one: the two arms did not get the same training budget.** TopoAE fits:
   `epochs_run=15`, `early_stopped=True` (all eight). Baseline fits: `epochs_run=40`,
   `early_stopped=False` (all eight) -- baselines never early-stop, since with no
   topological term `best_loss` keeps improving for the full budget under ordinary
   reconstruction training. But T1, T2, and T3 are **all model/baseline ratios**
   (`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 1), so the gate would have compared a 15-epoch model
   against a 40-epoch baseline -- a 2.7x budget asymmetry. `THRESH_T2 = 1.00` ("must beat,
   not tie") could not have produced an interpretable result under that confound: a
   model given 2.7x less training could plausibly lose to its baseline on reconstruction
   for a reason having nothing to do with the method being tested.

`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md`'s only stopping-related clause (Section 4, D-19's convergence
contingency) covers `wallclock_truncated` -- a different condition triggered by a different
mechanism (the wall-clock ceiling, not the plateau detector). The document did not
anticipate this interaction: two individually sound pre-registered rules -- the
warm-up/ramp schedule (Section 7, "Warm-up and ramp resolution") and the plateau early-stop
rule (Section 4, `EARLY_STOP_PATIENCE`/`EARLY_STOP_MIN_DELTA`, inherited from CAE-03's
protocol) -- interact to make `LAMBDA_TOPO` unreachable and the baseline comparison
unmatched.

## 2. The rule that changes, and its justification

**Changed:** `notebooks/pu_manifold/topoae.py`'s `train_topoae` stopping rule.

Plateau detection is only a convergence test against a *stationary* objective. While
`lambda_t` is ramping (and during warm-up, where it is held at a different constant, zero),
the per-epoch objective changes shape every epoch, so a plateau test against a `best_loss`
set under a different objective is not a convergence test at all -- it is an arithmetic
coincidence of how many epochs of warm-up preceded the ramp. Two changes, both required:

1. **Early stopping cannot fire before the ramp completes** -- not before `epoch >=
   floor(WARMUP_FRAC * MAX_EPOCHS) + floor(RAMP_FRAC * MAX_EPOCHS)`, the identical
   threshold `lambda_schedule` already uses to hold `lambda_t` constant at `LAMBDA_TOPO`.
   `best_loss`/the plateau counter are left untouched during warm-up and ramp.
2. **`best_loss` (and the plateau counter) reset at the epoch the ramp completes**, to that
   epoch's own loss under the now-stationary objective. Suspending the stop alone would
   still leave a stale `best_loss` carried over from the recon-only warm-up objective, which
   the post-ramp objective might never beat (exactly the failure mode observed) -- the reset
   is what makes the plateau detector measure the objective it is actually meant to.

This changes only *when* the plateau detector starts counting and what it is compared
against. It does not change `MAX_EPOCHS_CANDIDATES`, the `MAX_EPOCHS` selection rule, the
`wallclock_ceiling_s` check, `EARLY_STOP_PATIENCE`, `EARLY_STOP_MIN_DELTA`, or any other
pre-registered constant.

## 4. Limitations and Erratum 1, carried forward

All three named limitations from `02.4-03-SUMMARY.md` (Section 8 of
`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md`) carry forward verbatim and apply unchanged to the re-run fits:

1. Absolute persistence correlation `r=0.680` on the Swiss roll beat the matched baseline
   (`r=0.471`) but still missed the originally-set `0.8` bound.
2. The `<=10%` reconstruction-degradation lambda-selection rule is mis-specified for a
   method that trades reconstruction for topology by design; it bound at the grid floor on
   both the pre- and post-fidelity-correction loss. Documented, not fixed.
3. The T1 gate measures a different normalization than training optimizes
   (`AMBIENT_DIST_NORM="none"`, whole-set, at the gate; `d_x/d_x.max()` plus
   `latent_norm`, per-batch, in training) -- `loss_x_to_z`/`loss_z_to_x` are scale-sensitive
   and are not clean evidence on their own; the scale-free correlation `r` is the trustworthy
   quantity. Any output reporting `loss_x_to_z`/`loss_z_to_x` must restate this caveat at the
   point of use.

Erratum 1 (`02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 11) also carries forward unchanged: the
correction it made (Section 1's ambient-normalization scope) is orthogonal to this
amendment's subject (the stopping rule) and remains in force exactly as stated there.

## 5. Cache-key discipline for the re-run

`notebooks/diagnostics/topoae_train_run.py`'s fit artifacts for the corrected stopping rule
are written under a **new stem suffix**, `AMENDMENT_TAG = "amend01"` (e.g.
`topoae_fit_43cf438bc944c509_amend01_seed20260806_d20.npz`), rather than reusing the
pre-amendment stems (`topoae_fit_43cf438bc944c509_seed20260806_d20.npz`). This is
deliberate: the pre-amendment cfg dict at the pre-amendment stem is unchanged (same
`FIT_KEY`, same architecture/compute constants), so silently reusing that stem with a
changed `train_topoae` behind it would either raise `cache._manifest_matches`'s mismatch
guard (if the cfg dict also changed) or -- worse -- silently return the stale, buggy
fifteen-epoch artifact as a false cache hit (if the cfg dict did not change, since
`train_topoae`'s internal stopping logic is not itself a cfg key). The new stem sidesteps
both failure modes: the pre-amendment artifacts remain on disk, intact and unreferenced by
the amended runner, not overwritten -- a complete, inspectable record of the defect this
amendment fixes.

## 6. Ancestry

This document is committed alone (no file under `notebooks/` in the same commit, verified
via `git show --stat`), mirroring `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md`'s own precedent. Its adding
commit is resolved the same way, before any fit trained under the corrected stopping rule
runs:

```bash
git log --diff-filter=A --format=%H -- \
  .planning/phases/02.4-topological-auto-encoder-validity-test-inserted/02.4-PREREGISTRATION-AMENDMENT-01.md \
  | tail -1
```

taking the last line, then, proved -- not asserted:

```bash
git merge-base --is-ancestor <that SHA> HEAD
```

which must exit 0 before any `amend01`-tagged fit runs. `topoae_train_run.py` re-resolves
this SHA the same way at every invocation and raises, not warns, on a nonzero exit -- the SHA
is never hard-coded, exactly mirroring the discipline `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md`'s own
ancestry precondition already established. This is in addition to, not a replacement for,
the existing precondition proving `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` itself (`744c1c1`) an ancestor
of HEAD -- both must hold before any amended fit runs.
