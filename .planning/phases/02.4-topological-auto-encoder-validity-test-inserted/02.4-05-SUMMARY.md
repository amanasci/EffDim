## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed / coordinator-directed issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug, directed by coordinator review] Transfer-ratio estimator mismatch against the Swiss roll sweep**
- **Found during:** the Task 3 checkpoint round-trip -- the coordinator's verification of Tasks 1-2 compared `notebooks/diagnostics/topoae_lambda_sweep_run.py:197-199`'s transfer-ratio computation against this plan's original `topoae_train_run.py` computation and found they were different estimators, not the same quantity read from two datasets.
- **Issue:** The runner computed `transfer_ratio` as an **unweighted mean** of the per-epoch topological and reconstruction loss terms, averaged across **every** training epoch -- including the ten warm-up epochs where `lambda_t=0` and the topological term was not being optimized at all, so its raw (un-regularized, freely growing) value had no business entering an estimate of the trained topo/recon balance. The sweep's estimator is different on two independent counts: it is **lambda-weighted** (`topo_term = lambda_t * topo`, not raw `topo`), and it reads a **single, designated post-ramp epoch** (falling back to the last recorded epoch only when training stopped before reaching it), never an all-epoch average. Comparing the two uncorrected produced a reported PU `transfer_ratio=130.037` vs. the Swiss roll sweep's `0.422840` at the same `lambda=0.1` -- an apparent ~307.5x gap that the first checkpoint round described as "the transfer failure D-11 flagged." That description was itself a measurement artifact of the estimator mismatch, not a finding about the model.
- **Fix:** Added `_transfer_ratio_from_history`, reproducing the sweep's `_run_arm` estimator verbatim: `post_ramp_epoch = floor(warmup_frac * max_epochs) + floor(ramp_frac * max_epochs)`; look up the history entry at that epoch; if training stopped before reaching it, fall back to the last recorded epoch (exactly the sweep script's own documented behavior, not a new approximation); `transfer_ratio = (lambda_t * topo) / recon` at that entry. `_run_topoae` now records `transfer_ratio` plus `transfer_ratio_epoch_used`, `transfer_ratio_lambda_t_used`, `transfer_ratio_post_ramp_epoch_target`, and `transfer_ratio_reached_post_ramp_epoch`, so a downstream reader can tell a like-for-like comparison from a partial-ramp one without re-deriving it from the raw history.
- **Files modified:** `notebooks/diagnostics/topoae_train_run.py`
- **Verification:** `python -c "ast.parse(...)"` and the plan's full acceptance-criteria grep set re-run clean; `git diff --quiet -- notebooks/pu_manifold/cae.py notebooks/pu_manifold/topoae.py .planning/phases/02.4-*/02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` clean throughout; `PYTHONPATH=notebooks python -m pytest notebooks/pu_manifold/tests/ -q` -- 106 passed, unaffected (the fix touches only the runner, never `pu_manifold/`); the already-cached `topoae_fit_43cf438bc944c509_seed20260806_d20` artifact was corrected in place from its stored `history` (no retraining) and a subsequent re-invocation of the runner returned a cache hit with the corrected value intact.
- **Committed in:** `9f8f7c2`
- **Corrected result, reported as measured:** under the shared estimator, **PU `transfer_ratio=0.157801`** (read at epoch 14, `lambda_t=0.05` -- the primary fit's early-stopping plateau rule fired at epoch 15/40, before its own post-ramp epoch 20, so this falls back to the sweep's documented last-recorded-epoch rule) vs. the **Swiss roll sweep's `0.422840`** at the same `lambda=0.1` (read at its post-ramp epoch 150, reached in full, since that arm ran its complete 300-epoch schedule) -- **a ratio of ~0.373x, not ~307.5x.** Under the corrected, shared estimator there is **no order-of-magnitude transfer gap visible at this rung; if anything the PU value is smaller than the Swiss roll's.** The comparison is not perfectly matched even so: the PU fit's comparison point sits mid-ramp (`lambda_t=0.05`, half of `LAMBDA_TOPO=0.1`) because training converged before reaching the nominal post-ramp epoch, while the Swiss roll arm's comparison point is at the full, constant lambda. This is reported exactly as measured, with the caveat stated plainly, rather than extrapolated or adjusted toward either the originally-reported reading or any other expected outcome. All eight TopoAE fits show the same pattern (early stop at epoch 15/40, fallback to epoch 14/`lambda_t=0.05`); `transfer_ratio` ranges `0.157801`-`0.185603` across all eight rungs/seeds -- see the full-fits table below.
