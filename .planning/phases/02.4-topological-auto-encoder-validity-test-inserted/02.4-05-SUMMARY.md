## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed / coordinator-directed issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug, directed by coordinator review] Transfer-ratio estimator mismatch against the Swiss roll sweep**
- **Found during:** the Task 3 checkpoint round-trip -- the coordinator's verification of Tasks 1-2 compared `notebooks/diagnostics/topoae_lambda_sweep_run.py`'s transfer-ratio computation against this plan's original `topoae_train_run.py` computation and found they were different estimators, not the same quantity read from two datasets.
- **Issue:** The runner computed `transfer_ratio` as an unweighted mean of the per-epoch topological and reconstruction loss terms, averaged across every training epoch, including warm-up epochs where `lambda_t=0`. The sweep's estimator is lambda-weighted and reads a single, designated post-ramp epoch. Comparing the two uncorrected produced a reported PU `transfer_ratio=130.037` vs. the sweep's `0.422840` -- an apparent ~307.5x gap that was itself a measurement artifact.
- **Fix:** Added `_transfer_ratio_from_history`, reproducing the sweep's estimator verbatim (lambda-weighted, post-ramp epoch, documented last-recorded-epoch fallback).
- **Files modified:** `notebooks/diagnostics/topoae_train_run.py`
- **Verification:** full acceptance-criteria grep set clean; `git diff --quiet` on sealed files clean; `pytest` 106/106 (unaffected, fix touches only the runner); the already-cached primary-rung artifact corrected in place from its stored history (no retraining), confirmed by a subsequent cache-hit re-invocation.
- **Committed in:** `9f8f7c2`
- **Corrected result at the time (under the still-buggy pre-amendment stopping rule):** `transfer_ratio=0.157801` at the primary rung, vs. the sweep's `0.422840` -- a ratio of `~0.373x`, not `~307.5x`. This reading was itself superseded by Deviation 2's fix (below), since the comparison point (`epoch 14`, `lambda_t=0.05`) was a fallback caused by the stopping-rule bug, not a fully-converged measurement.

**2. [Rule 4 - Architectural/rule change, escalated to the user, plan reopened] `train_topoae`'s plateau-based early stopping fires against a non-stationary warm-up/ramp objective**
- **Found during:** orchestrator verification of the first complete sixteen-fit run (after Deviation 1's fix), which found every one of the eight TopoAE fits halted at `epochs_run=15` with `lambda_t` stuck at half of `LAMBDA_TOPO` -- identical across six latent dimensions and three seeds.
- **Issue:** `train_topoae`'s plateau detector set `best_loss` under the recon-only warm-up objective (`lambda_t=0`, ten epochs). The ramp then restarted the objective at `recon + small_lambda*topo`, which could not beat that warm-up-era mark within `EARLY_STOP_PATIENCE=5` even though both `recon` and `topo` were falling every single epoch (`topo` collapsed 260x across five ramp epochs -- see the amendment's full loss table). Warm-up (10) + patience (5) = 15, every time. The two consequences: (a) every TopoAE fit was truncated mid-descent, not converged -- this plan's original Task 2 report ("D-19's contingency did not fire, converged cleanly") is corrected here: nothing had converged, and the wrong contingency (wall-clock) was the one D-19 anticipated; (b) baselines never early-stop, so every rung's two arms received different effective training budgets (15 vs. 40 epochs, a 2.7x asymmetry) -- a confound T1/T2/T3's model/baseline ratio construction cannot tolerate.
- **Escalation and disposition:** this is a rule change, not a threshold or constant tune, so it was escalated to the user per `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 10's own stated consequence ("none of its constants may move without a fresh, separately-ratified pre-registration and a full re-run of all sixteen fits"). The user selected: amend the pre-registration and re-run all sixteen fits, handled as an openly-ratified amendment, not a silent fix and not folded into Erratum 1 (which corrected a misstatement about already-implemented code, not a rule).
- **Fix:** Two changes to `notebooks/pu_manifold/topoae.py`'s `train_topoae`, both required: (1) early stopping cannot fire before `epoch >= floor(warmup_frac*max_epochs) + floor(ramp_frac*max_epochs)` -- the identical threshold `lambda_schedule` uses to hold `lambda_t` constant at `lambda_topo`; `best_loss`/the plateau counter are left untouched during warm-up and ramp; (2) `best_loss` and the plateau counter reset to that epoch's own loss at the moment the ramp completes, so the plateau detector measures the final, stationary objective from a mark set under that same objective, not a stale one. Suspending the stop alone would still have left the post-ramp objective racing against the warm-up-era mark.
- **Files modified:** `notebooks/pu_manifold/topoae.py`, `notebooks/pu_manifold/tests/test_topoae.py`, `notebooks/diagnostics/topoae_train_run.py` (STEP 0c ancestry precondition, `amend01`-tagged cache stems, `STEP 4` budget-parity check), `.planning/phases/02.4-topological-auto-encoder-validity-test-inserted/02.4-PREREGISTRATION-AMENDMENT-01.md` (new, committed alone).
- **Verification:** three new tests (`test_train_topoae_early_stop_never_fires_before_ramp_completes`, `test_train_topoae_best_loss_resets_at_ramp_completion`, `test_train_topoae_still_improving_at_ramp_boundary_is_not_halted`) -- the reset test explicitly replays the pre-fix (reset-free) plateau logic against a real fixture's history to prove the reset changes the outcome, not merely that the fixture happens not to plateau. Full suite: 109/109 (106 + 3 new). `notebooks/pu_manifold/cae.py` and `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` untouched throughout (`git diff --quiet` confirmed after every step). Both ancestry preconditions (`744c1c1` and the amendment's `9f9a74a`) confirmed ancestors of HEAD before, during, and after the re-run.
