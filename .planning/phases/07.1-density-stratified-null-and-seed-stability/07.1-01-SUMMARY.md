**Task 1 — D-08 gating constant set (option selected: `ratify-as-proposed`).**
- Positive-control target grid widened to 9 points: `(0.010, 0.015, 0.020, 0.025, 0.030, 0.040, 0.050, 0.10, 0.20)`, bracketing both the measured `d=20` (-0.02419) and `d=32` (-0.02172) residuals on either side of Phase 7's unresolved 0.021-0.05 detection-floor gap.
- Positive control runs in **both** directions (`POSITIVE_CONTROL_DIRECTIONS = ("positive", "negative")`), because `07.1-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 1 measured that coarse strata bias the null MEAN positive — liberal on the negative tail — and every residual this phase adjudicates is negative. `POSITIVE_CONTROL_RULE` records that the reported power quantity is read off the negative-direction search.

- Rationale for auto-selecting the first option over `positive-arm-only`: the option's own stated con for the alternative (power argument measured in the opposite direction from every adjudicated residual, with a measured mechanism predicting asymmetry) outweighs the marginal simplicity gain, and the plan text itself frames `ratify-as-proposed` as the recommended default.

**Task 2 — D-11/D-15 verdict vocabularies and unanimity rule (option selected: `ratify`).**
