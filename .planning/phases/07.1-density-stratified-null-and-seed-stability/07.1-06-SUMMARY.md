- **Found during:** Task 1 self-check against the acceptance criteria.
- **Issue:** The plan's acceptance criterion is `grep -ciE 'waive|waiver|exempt|not applicable' 07.1-FINDINGS.md` printing `0` "in the Swiss-roll section." Section 8's own sentence -- "not a waiver, an exemption, a deferral, or a 'not applicable here'" -- necessarily contains those words once, in order to negate them. `07-FINDINGS.md` Sec 4 (the reference implementation this plan's action text says to mirror) has the identical property: "This section is a declaration of an already-satisfied gate, not a waiver." A literal `grep -c` count of `0` is not achievable by any sentence that states the negation in prose, only by one that never mentions the concept at all -- which would be a weaker, less legible declaration.
- **Fix:** Kept the negation sentence (matching the reference implementation's house style exactly) rather than editing it to dodge the naive grep. The section substantively declares the gate satisfied WITH its reason (`02.6_swiss_roll_plainae_curvature_check.ipynb`, no new manifold model) and explicitly states it is not a waiver -- which is the prohibition's actual intent (a declaration that omits the reason is indistinguishable from a skip; this declaration does not omit the reason).

- **Verification:** `grep -n` confirms the sole match (line 304) sits inside Section 8 itself, in the negation sentence, immediately following the artifact name and reason -- not a bare "not applicable" standing alone.
