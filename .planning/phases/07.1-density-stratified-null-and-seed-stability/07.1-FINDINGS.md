### What the real data measured, against what was predicted

`07.1-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 1 measured, on a synthetic lognormal fixture matching PU's density
skew, that band width is flat across `S=10..500` (std 0.0098-0.0117) while the null MEAN is
strongly POSITIVE at `S=10` (+0.033 to +0.034 across three seeds), decaying toward +0.007-0.012
by `S=100-500`. That prediction mattered because a positive-biased null is LIBERAL on the
negative tail every residual in this phase sits in — it makes a negative observed value look
more extreme than it should, i.e. easier to clear.

The real PU data, measured at its own 7.8-order density spread (`density_p05 =
60706.772750776814`, `density_p50 = 2286608337.724516`, `density_p95 = 3631776851546.029`,
`density_ratio_p95_p05 = 59824903.993097804`), shows **null_mean NEGATIVE at every `S` for every
`d`**, non-monotonic across `S`, not decaying from a positive value: `d=20` and `d=32` get
slightly MORE negative from `S=10` to `S=20` and then roughly plateau to `S=50`; `d=25`'s
`null_mean` is small throughout and non-monotonic (-0.00155 at `S=10`, -0.00302 at `S=20`,
-0.00205 at `S=50`). `null_std` IS flat across `S` for every `d` (roughly 0.0097-0.0102
everywhere) — the one part of the synthetic fixture's prediction that DOES reproduce here.

**This matches NEITHER prior prediction, and where the measurement contradicts D-02's stated
mechanism, that is stated plainly rather than re-asserted over the data.** D-02's stated
rationale for choosing `S=20` was that finer strata narrow the null band (making the test MORE
sensitive), not more conservative. On this measurement, the band's WIDTH does not move
meaningfully with `S` at all (`null_std` is flat, matching the synthetic fixture, not D-02's
band-width mechanism); what moves is the MEAN, and it moves in the opposite direction and shape
from what the synthetic fixture predicted. D-02's stated justification for choosing `S=20` — "finer
strata narrow the band" — is not the mechanism the real data shows; the real mechanism the grid
exposes here is a small, `d`-dependent, non-monotonic mean shift, not a width change in either
direction. **D-02's CHOICE of `S=20` may well stand on its own** — it sits in the middle of the
defensible range and every clearance status is stable across the whole grid — but its stated
rationale does not hold on this measurement, and this document does not re-assert that rationale
as though it did.

## 6. What the null is and is not

D-05's recorded trade-off, verbatim: `h` and `m` are each permuted independently within strata,
mirroring `scipy.stats.permutation_test(permutation_type="pairings")`'s global re-pairing
confined to one stratum at a time. Because `h`'s residualization also varies per permutation, the
resulting band mixes both residualization sides and is **not attributable to the MKNN link
alone**. The considered alternative — shuffling `m` only, holding `h`'s joint distribution with
density exactly fixed — was evaluated and **not chosen**.

`07.1-RESEARCH.md`'s Assumptions Log A1 is carried forward, not resolved by citation: restricted
(within-block) permutation is the standard technique for conditioning a permutation null on a
nuisance covariate in ANOVA/Mantel-style settings (Anderson & ter Braak blocked-permutation
framework, `vegan`'s `strata=` convention), but whether it transfers cleanly to a
rank-residualized partial correlation specifically is not established by that citation alone.
The positive control (Section 4) is the arbiter here, not the citation — it demonstrates the
constructed null can recover a planted effect at the magnitude this phase's residuals sit at,
which is the operational check available, independent of whether the general methodology's
citation-level justification transfers exactly.

## 9. Accepted limitations

- **The three `d=25` fits share one architecture and one training split.** `SPLIT_SEED` and
  `HOLDOUT_FRACTION` are held fixed across all three seeds (D-09) so that any difference between
  the three fields is attributable to `TORCH_INIT_SEED` alone — but this means the three-seed
  spread measured in Section 5 (Spearman 0.72-0.85 pairwise) is a measure of **fit-to-fit field
  instability under one architecture and one split**, not of sampling variability across
  different splits, architectures, or data subsets. A different measure of variability (e.g.
  across splits) would answer a different question than the one this phase asked.
- **The seed axis was replaced at `d=25` only.** Phase 7's `SEED_HANDLING_RULE =
  "single_seed_across_d_sweep"` accepted limitation still stands everywhere else in the phase's
  record — at `d=20` and `d=32`, and for the raw statistic at every `d`. Only the `d=25`
  density-controlled partial has now been measured across three seeds; nothing here licenses an
  inference that `d=20` or `d=32` would show similar (or different) seed stability.
- **The field is still evaluated on all 10,000 rows, including the 8,000 the decoder trained on**
  — inherited unchanged from Phase 7's own `FIELD_EVALUATED_ON` convention. This is not a
  fresh-holdout evaluation of the curvature field itself, only of the reconstruction loss used to
  fit it.
- **No ground truth for PU curvature exists anywhere in this record.** Phase 7's own analytic
  validation gives a fidelity RANGE (`+0.53` to `+0.99`), never a point estimate, and nothing in
  this document licenses treating any of 07.1's numbers as measuring "true" PU curvature to any
  finer precision than that range already bounds.
- **Deferred items** — named as deferred, not as answered: the full 3x3 seed x `d` grid (~7h vs
  ~2h for `d=25` alone, explicitly out of scope by the ROADMAP); a stratified null for the RAW
  statistic (a like-for-like raw-vs-partial comparison under one null construction, not required
  to answer either D7.1 requirement); and CKA as a second alignment probe (out of scope by
  Phase 7's D7-07, not a 07.1 decision).
