# Phase 02.5 Stage 1 -- Feasibility Boundary Findings

**`CURVATURE_VERDICT = FAIL`, measured exactly as the sealed contract in
`02.5-PREREGISTRATION.md` produces it, on the base cell (`d=20, D=768, n=10000,
n_bumps=8, k=30, density_correct=True, seed=20260807`).** `spearman_rho = 0.5205`
clears its `0.5000` threshold; `quantile_bin_concordance = 0.4444` does not clear its
`0.4750` threshold. Section 3h's option-scale-C requires both to clear, so the cell
fails. **This FAIL is not robust to seed** -- see Section 3 -- and the document states
that caveat as plainly as it states the FAIL itself. Neither fact cancels the other.

## 3. Where the PU regime sits, and which axis is responsible

**The base cell fails on `quantile_bin_concordance` alone** (`spearman_rho` clears with
a `+0.0205` margin; `quantile_bin_concordance` misses by `-0.0306`). Taken as a bare
verdict this reads as a narrow, specific miss on the region-scale gate. It is not that
simple, for two reasons that must be read together, not separately.

**3a. The verdict is not robust to seed -- the single most important caveat in this
report.** At the *identical* base-cell configuration, only the seed changes: `2 of 3`
seeds tested **clear** both gates. `quantile_bin_concordance` reads `0.4444` (base seed
`20260807`, FAILS), `0.4837` (seed `20260808`, clears), `0.5236` (seed `20260809`,
clears). The across-seed spread is `0.5236 - 0.4444 = 0.0792`, which **exceeds** the base
cell's own distance to the threshold (`0.4750 - 0.4444 = 0.0306`) by more than 2.5x. The
FAIL therefore rests on the base seed being measurably the unluckiest of the three
tested, not on a robust separation from the threshold.

This is reported as `CURVATURE_VERDICT = FAIL` exactly as the sealed contract on the
base cell produces it -- the gate was fixed in advance on that one cell, and it fails,
and that stands. But the honest reading in the same breath is: **a single-seed base cell
cannot distinguish a real FAIL from a false one at this configuration.** The
pre-registration exposes its own gap here -- Section 9 ratified one base seed
(`20260807`) with no replication requirement on the cell that actually decides the
phase's routing. It is worth naming explicitly that this defect was **not** dug up by a
post-hoc audit: the sealed sweep's *own* `seed` axis (three seeds, ratified in Section 9
as "3 seeds at the base cell") is what discovered it. The evidence sits inside the
ratified design.

**3b. Ambient dimension `D` is irrelevant to the failure -- it is entirely an
intrinsic-`d` effect.** `quantile_bin_concordance` and `spearman_rho` are **bit-identical**
(`0.4444459259456792` / `0.5204686765526867`, to the last printed digit) at
`D = 28, 50, 200, 768` -- a 27x change in ambient dimension moves nothing. This is
expected from the graph-of-function fixture's exact-zero ambient padding (pinned by
`test_graph_fixture_padding_and_codimension`), but it is worth stating as a finding
rather than an assumption: **the failure is driven entirely by intrinsic `d`, not by
ambient dimension `D`.** This corrects a framing risk in
`02.5-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 13b, whose curse-of-dimensionality mechanism (halving
the neighbourhood radius at `d=20` costs `2^20` times more points to hold local sample
density constant) is itself about sampling a `d`-dimensional neighbourhood -- an
intrinsic-`d` effect that Section 13b's own prose could be misread as blaming on
ambient scale. `02.5-NOTE-randomized-trace.md` already records the corollary for the
decoder arm (02.5-08): a decoder differentiates an analytic map rather than sampling a
neighbourhood, so it does not inherit this constraint, and Section 13b's finding must
not be carried across to it.

**Which axis binds, stated plainly:** `d` is the axis whose crossing point the PU regime
sits on the wrong side of (last clear `d=10`, first fail `d=20`; the PU regime's own
`d=20` sits exactly at the first failing rung). `n` and `k` both improve the statistics
monotonically within the tested range but neither reaches the floor at any tested value
(Section 2) -- extrapolating past the tested range is not something this report claims
to know, only that the trend, if it continued, points toward eventually clearing at
some larger `n` or `k` than tested. `n_bumps` also binds (`n_bumps=1` clears,
`n_bumps=8` fails) -- see Section 4. `D` does not bind at all (3b, above). Given the
seed instability (3a), the single most defensible summary is: **the base cell is a
narrow, seed-sensitive miss driven by intrinsic dimension and problem complexity
(`n_bumps`), not by ambient scale, and not decisively separated from a PASS at this
exact configuration.**

## 4. The two fixture variants

`n_bumps=1` (codimension 1) clears both gates (`rho=0.5890`, `qbc=0.5135`);
`n_bumps=8` (codimension 8, the base cell) fails (`rho=0.5205`, `qbc=0.4444`). **The two
frontiers disagree** -- this is the empirical answer to `02.5-RESEARCH.md` Open
Question 2, and the disagreement is itself the finding, not an inconvenience to explain
away. A low-codimension (`n_bumps=1`) fixture's clear does **not** transfer to the
higher-codimension (`n_bumps=8`) fixture at the identical `d, D, n, k`, and by extension
any read of the real `D=768`, effectively-high-codimension PU regime from a
codimension-1 fixture is unsupported. The base cell -- the PU-matched regime -- uses
`n_bumps=8` precisely because it is the harder, more representative setting; that is the
frontier that routes the phase.

## 5. The density correction (D-06)

The clean D-06 proof (a flat, linearly-embedded fixture cannot exhibit density-induced
fake `||H||` at all, because an exactly rank-`d` cloud's SVD-recovered tangent basis is
weighting-invariant) was established analytically and empirically in `02.5-02`, on a
dedicated `make_flat_fixture` fixture -- not re-run by this sweep. This runner's own
`_make_fixture` only ever builds the Swiss roll anchor or the graph-of-function fixture
(`AMPLITUDE=1.0` is constant throughout; no cell in this sweep has zero true curvature),
so the flat-fixture proof is not part of the artifact this document reports on; it is
correctly attributed to `02.5-02` and not re-claimed here.

What this sweep *does* measure is the correction's effect on the genuinely-curved
graph-of-function fixture, at every cell, both corrected and uncorrected, per D-06's
"run at every cell" requirement:

- **At the base cell** (`density_skew=0.0`, i.e. uniform sampling): `density_correct=False`
  gives `rho=0.5237, qbc=0.4428, median_relative_error=0.8752`;
  `density_correct=True` gives `rho=0.5205, qbc=0.4444, median_relative_error=0.8704`.
  The correction moves each number by well under 1%, in mixed directions (very slightly
  worse on `rho`, very slightly better on `qbc` and `median_relative_error`) -- at
  `density_skew=0.0` there is no density artifact to correct, so a near-null effect is
  exactly what is expected.
- **On the deliberately skewed cell** (`density_skew=3.0`, `realized_skew=2.94`):
  `density_correct=False` gives `rho=0.5131, qbc=0.4339, median_relative_error=0.8683`;
  `density_correct=True` gives `rho=0.5075, qbc=0.4268, median_relative_error=0.8690`.
  Here the correction makes both gating statistics measurably **worse**, not better
  (`rho` down `0.0056`, `qbc` down `0.0071`), and `median_relative_error` is a wash
  (`0.8683` vs `0.8690`). This is the opposite of the modest, positive effect
  `02.5-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 6 anticipated ("on a genuinely curved, strongly-skewed
  fixture the correction measurably, though modestly, ~8-10% relative, reduces median
  relative error") -- that anticipated effect is not reproduced at `d=20`, `n_bumps=8`.
  Stated plainly: **at the PU-matched working dimension, the density correction is not
  merely small, it is not reliably helpful**, unlike at the easier configurations
  Section 6's own derivation drew on. This does not overturn Section 6's proof that a
  flat fixture cannot be fooled (a different, narrower claim, unaffected); it is a new,
  disclosed finding about the correction's effect specifically on the curved,
  high-`n_bumps` fixture at `d=20`.

## 6. The two estimators (D-05)

**This section cannot report an empirical centroid-versus-quadric agreement across the
`d` ladder, because the non-gating quadric cross-check did not complete at any tested
cell except the `d=2` Swiss roll anchor.** This is a genuine limitation of the run, not a
silent gap: `quadric_mean_curvature`'s per-point least-squares fit
(`quadric_n_coefficients = d(d+1)/2`) is measured (the runner's own module docstring) at
roughly 6-8 minutes of CPU time for a single PU-regime-scale call, so the runner
deliberately time-boxes it far more tightly than the two gating statistics
(`OTHER_CELL_QUADRIC_TIMEOUT_S=2.0`, `BASE_CELL_QUADRIC_TIMEOUT_S=120.0`) to keep the
whole sweep inside the ratified 30-minute wall-clock budget. Even the base cell's
extended 120-second budget was not enough (its own quadric attempt is recorded at
`elapsed_s=134.95`, just past the 120s allowance) -- so `quadric_spearman_rho`,
`agreement_spearman`, and `agreement_median_rel_diff` are all `null` for every cell at
`d >= 5`, including the base cell itself. Only `d=2` (Swiss roll, `quadric_n_coefficients=3`,
trivially cheap) completed: `agreement_spearman=0.7750`,
`agreement_median_rel_diff=0.1198` (`density_correct=True`) -- consistent with, but far
too thin a sample to generalize from.

## 7. Limitations

Carried forward from `02.5-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 12, restated because they bear
directly on how to read Section 2-6 above:

(a) The Swiss roll evidence (`d=2`) is the easy case; the tangent-basis estimate gets
substantially harder as `d` grows, so a Swiss-roll-calibrated intuition must not be read
as the real-data threshold -- borne out directly by this sweep's own `d`-axis crossing
between `d=10` and `d=20`.

(b)-(c) The graph-of-function family is a synthetic proxy with a chosen, not measured,
curvature scale, and its padded ambient directions are exactly flat -- both bear on why
Section 3b's `D`-irrelevance finding is a property of this fixture family and not
necessarily of the real PU manifold's normal space.

(d) The analytic ground truth and the estimator are both written by this phase; a shared
conceptual error would be invisible to either statistic.

(f) `quantile_bin_concordance`'s own null differs by cell scale (`n=1000`'s null is
narrower than `n=10000`'s, since cross-true-bin pairs among a fixed `200,000`-pair draw
are bounded by `n^2` at small `n`) -- visible in this sweep's `n`-axis rows (Section 2),
where the null term, not only the floor, moves with `n`.

(g) The noise-oracle-ceiling calibration technique saturates on `BASE_CELL`'s own
heavy-tailed true-curvature field (max/median `4708.8x`); the `null_threshold` term, not
the floor, is what actually discriminates at `d=20` -- directly relevant to reading
Section 3's margins as noise-scale, not saturation-scale, numbers.

**New limitations this run itself revealed, not anticipated in the pre-registration:**

(h) **`ladder_d` is non-monotone, and this is noise, not structure.** `d=20` fails
(`qbc=0.4444`) but `d=24` clears (`qbc=0.4814`) -- a value *larger* than `d=20`'s. This
sits inside the same `~0.04-0.08` noise band the seed spread occupies (Section 3a),
sloshing around the `0.4750` threshold. It must not be read as evidence that `d=24` is
genuinely easier than `d=20`; it is the same instability Section 3a already names, seen
on a different axis.

(i) **`k > d` is a hard structural floor of the point-cloud estimator, not a
measurement.** `k = 10, 15, 20` are recorded `infeasible_k_le_d` at `d=20` (and `k=30`
at `ladder_d = 32, 40`) -- not FAILs, not PASSes, simply not computable, because
`local_tangent_basis`'s SVD needs strict redundancy (`k` strictly greater than `d`) to
average a tangent direction over at all. The estimator is squeezed between three
simultaneous requirements: `k` must exceed `d`, `k` must be large enough for usable
statistics, and `k` must stay small enough to remain local (`O(r^2)` bias grows with the
neighbourhood radius, which grows with `k` -- Section 13b(a)'s already-measured
peak-then-decline shape). This is a real, load-bearing finding about what the
point-cloud estimator can even attempt at the PU regime's own `d=20`, independent of
whether it passes or fails once it can run.

(j) **The non-gating quadric cross-check could not be measured beyond `d=2`** within
this sweep's wall-clock budget (Section 6) -- a real evidentiary gap, not a silent one.

## 8. Routing

**Outcome: FAIL, marginal and seed-sensitive, on the axis this document names plainly.**
Per `02.5-PREREGISTRATION.md` Section 10, the base cell does not clear (Section 2), so
the phase **halts for a user decision with no auto-fallback**. This is a complete and
reportable outcome for stage 1, not a stall -- exactly the negative-result-is-legitimate
posture the ROADMAP set for this phase before any cell was measured.

**What this document does not do:** it does not soften the FAIL, does not hunt for a
sweep cell or reading that reverses it, and does not reinterpret `option-scale-C`'s
both-gates rule after the fact. It also does not overstate the FAIL as decisive --
Section 3a's seed-spread finding means the honest characterization is a **marginal**
FAIL, inside the noise floor the sweep's own seed axis measured, not a wide, robust
miss. Stating both of these together, without letting either one erase the other, is the
document's job.

**The two live options, named per Section 10's own instruction, neither chosen here:**

1. **Ollivier-Ricci / intrinsic curvature** -- graph-native, defined on the k-NN graph
   via optimal transport, never touches the ambient space, and so is not subject to the
   sampling constraint (`k > d`, `O(r^2)` bias, the curse-of-dimensionality mechanism of
   Section 13b) that just failed on the point-cloud estimator.
2. **Stage 2's Arm B (the decoder / chart-curvature path), per
   `02.5-NOTE-randomized-trace.md`** -- differentiates an analytic decoder map rather
   than sampling a neighbourhood, so it does not inherit the point-cloud estimator's `k
   > d` structural floor or its intrinsic-`d` sampling constraint (Section 3b). The
   note explicitly records that stage 1's FAIL does not automatically transfer to this
   arm, and that Section 13b's curse-of-dimensionality finding must not be carried
   across to it.

This document does **not** choose between them -- per the plan's own instruction, that
choice belongs to the user at the Task 3 checkpoint. **No "recommended `k`" is emitted**,
per D-04 and Section 8 of `02.5-PREREGISTRATION.md`'s own prohibition: naming one would
read as post-hoc licence to change `k*=15`, and this document does not do that. No
sealed verdict is reopened, softened, or recomputed -- `GATE_VERDICT`, `CAE_VERDICT`,
and `TOPOAE_VERDICT` all remain FAIL exactly as previously measured, and
`CURVATURE_VERDICT = FAIL` (this document's own new measurement) stands as recorded in
