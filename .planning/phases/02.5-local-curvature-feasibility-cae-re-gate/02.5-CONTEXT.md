# Phase 02.5: Local Curvature Feasibility & CAE Local Re-Gate - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Two stages, the first gating the second.

**Stage 1 — feasibility probe.** Establish empirically whether a **local** curvature field is
recoverable at the PU data's sampling density, measured against manifolds whose curvature is known
analytically. Emits a feasibility boundary, not a bare verdict.

**Stage 2 — head-to-head substrate comparison.** Only if stage 1 clears: determine whether curvature
computed through the CAE's charts beats curvature computed directly on raw points, both scored
against known ground truth, under a fresh pre-registration.

**What this phase does NOT do.** It does not reopen, soften, or recompute any sealed verdict —
`GATE_VERDICT` (Phase 2), `CAE_VERDICT` (02.2), and `TOPOAE_VERDICT` (02.4) all remain FAIL exactly
as measured. It adds a new, differently-scoped measurement. It does not compute a curvature field on
PU data for downstream consumption — that is Phase 3. It does not touch Ollivier-Ricci / intrinsic
curvature, which is a separate deferred phase.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The central correction this discussion produced

- **D-00:** **The ROADMAP re-scope's framing of the binding constraint is wrong, and planning must not
  inherit it.** The re-scope records that a local quadratic fit needs `d(d+1)/2` coefficients per
  normal direction — 210 at `d=20` — against `k* = 15` neighbours, and calls this "badly
  underdetermined." That count is the cost of the **full second fundamental form**. Mean curvature is
  only its *trace*, and the classical identity `Δ_M x = H` (equivalently: the centroid of a
  neighbourhood is displaced from its centre point along the mean curvature vector, by roughly
  `r²/(2(d+2))·H`) estimates the trace as **an average over `k` vectors** — one unknown with `k`
  samples, not 210 unknowns with 15 equations. The underdetermination may therefore not bind at all.
  This reframes whether the phase is feasible and must be stated in the pre-registration. —
  **Reversibility:** costly — the entire estimator choice (D-05) and the phase's feasibility case rest
  on it; if the identity were misapplied, stage 1's design would need rebuilding from scratch.

### Feasibility criterion (stage 1)

- **D-01:** **Spearman rank correlation between estimated and true `|H|` is the gating statistic**;
  median relative error on `|H|` is computed and reported as **non-gating** evidence. Rationale: Phase
  4 partitions by `|H|` quantiles, so it consumes ordering, not magnitude — rank agreement is a weaker
  and more achievable bar that still matches the downstream use. Follows the gating/context split
  established in 02.2 and 02.4. — **Reversibility:** one-way — once the gating statistic is
  pre-registered with ancestry proof, changing which statistic gates is post-hoc tuning, the exact
  garden-of-forking-paths move this milestone's discipline exists to prevent.

- **D-02:** **The Spearman threshold is calibrated against a permutation null**, not invented. Shuffle
  true curvature values among points, recompute Spearman a few hundred times to obtain the chance
  distribution, and set the bar above it. Still pre-registered and ratified before the probe runs, per
  02.2's D-10 ordering (pre-registration committed first, ratification second, execution third, proved
  by git ancestry). Phase 4 already uses a permutation null for MKNN, so the milestone stays
  internally consistent. — **Reversibility:** one-way — same pre-registration logic as D-01.

- **D-03:** **Fixtures: the Swiss roll as the mandatory `d=2` anchor, plus a graph-of-function family**
  `M = {(x, f(x))}` for `x ∈ ℝᵈ` embedded in `ℝᴰ`, where `H` follows analytically from `f`'s Hessian.
  A Swiss roll is intrinsically 2-dimensional and cannot reach the `d = 18–25` regime where the
  coefficient count would bite; a `d`-sphere has *constant* `H`, so Spearman is undefined on it. The
  graph construction gives spatially varying analytic ground truth at arbitrary `(d, D)`.
  **CLAUDE.md's "always use the Swiss roll, do not substitute another toy dataset" is honoured** — the
  Swiss roll remains the mandatory sanity-check fixture and the `d=2` anchor; the family *extends* the
  probe rather than substituting for it. Planning must not drop the Swiss roll.

- **D-04:** **Stage 1 emits a feasibility boundary, not a binary verdict.** Sweep `(d, D, n, k)`,
  locate where Spearman crosses the null-calibrated threshold, and report where the PU regime sits
  relative to that frontier. A boundary says how far off the regime is and which axis is responsible;
  a binary verdict is unactionable. **On the wrong side: halt for user decision, no auto-fallback**
  (02.2's D-02 pattern), and the report must explicitly name Ollivier-Ricci / intrinsic curvature as
  the live remaining option, with the reason: it is defined on the k-NN graph via optimal transport,
  never touches the ambient space, and so is not subject to the sampling constraint that just failed.
  Do **not** emit a "recommended `k`" — that risks reading as post-hoc licence to change `k*`.

### Estimator

- **D-05:** **The centroid / Laplace–Beltrami estimator gates; the local quadric ("bowl") fit is run
  alongside and its agreement reported as non-gating evidence.** Agreement between two independent
  routes hardens the result; divergence is itself informative. Rejected: quadric-only with ridge or
  low-rank regularization, because the regularization strength becomes a dial that would have to be
  pre-registered blind. — **Reversibility:** one-way — the gating estimator is pre-registered.

  Known caveats, all to be stated in the pre-registration: bias grows like `r²` at finite radius;
  sensitivity to non-uniform sampling density (see D-06); yields `H` and `|H|` but not the full `II`
  (Phase 4 consumes only `|H|` quantiles, so this costs nothing here).

- **D-06:** **Density correction is pre-registered up front AND proven on deliberately non-uniform
  fixtures.** The centroid estimator assumes neighbours are spread evenly; where points bunch on one
  side, the centroid drifts that way and the drift is **indistinguishable from curvature**. Uncorrected,
  this phase could measure embedding clustering and report it as geometry. Foundation-model embeddings
  cluster, so this is a live risk, not a theoretical one. **A Swiss roll is sampled evenly by
  construction and would NOT catch this** — the fixtures must be non-uniformly sampled on purpose.

- **D-07:** **Working dimension: primary `d = 20` as the gating rung, with a sensitivity ladder
  (02.4-style, e.g. `{8,16,20,24,32,40}`) reported as non-gating.** The centroid gap must be projected
  onto the normal space to isolate `H`, which requires a local tangent estimate and hence a working
  dimension. `D_FROZEN = 5` is **barred from inheritance** (flagged suspect in `02-FINDINGS.md` §6.4
  and `STATE.md`: the Tenenbaum residual curve saturates early under 41% negative eigenvalue mass, so
  the elbow measured the flatness failure rather than the manifold's dimension). **02.2's D-04 already
  resolved this exact question** — `d=20` from the local intrinsic-dimension cluster (TwoNN 19.5,
  local-PCA median 25.0), explicitly refusing the global elbow because "charts are local objects; local
  estimates are the matching referent" — and that reasoning transfers directly. Rejected: per-point
  `d`, because it makes `|H|` less comparable across points, and Phase 4's entire operation is ranking
  points against each other.

- **D-08:** **The estimator pre-registers its own neighbourhood rule**, with an explicit bias–variance
  justification, rather than silently inheriting `k* = 15`. Phase 1 froze `k*` by a pre-registered
  plateau rule targeting **Isomap graph connectivity and stability** — a different objective from
  curvature bias–variance on the same number. Inheriting it is conservative but not automatically
  correct, and nobody has checked. Sweep `k` in the probe and report sensitivity as non-gating; the
  **gating `k` is fixed in advance**.

### Local CAE gate design (stage 2)

- **D-09:** **Stage 2 gates on a head-to-head against known `H`: CAE charts versus raw points.** This
  **supersedes the ROADMAP re-scope's conception** of stage 2 as "a locally-scoped gate on the CAE."
  If the centroid estimator works on raw 768-d points, no learned model is needed at all — so the
  question that serves Phase 3 is which **substrate** yields a trustworthy curvature field, not whether
  the CAE is a good autoencoder.

  The CAE's genuine value is noise: curvature is second-order, so signal grows like `r²` while noise
  falls only like `σ/√k`; a model fitted to all 10,000 points averages noise away, and its chart
  decoder can be differentiated **exactly** via `torch.func` rather than estimated statistically. The
  counter-risk: a neural network's second derivative can wiggle where the true manifold does not — a
  smooth-looking reconstruction guarantees nothing about its second derivative. **Only fixtures with
  analytic `H` can distinguish these.** This framing also makes "the CAE is not needed" a legitimate,
  reportable outcome rather than a phase failure. — **Reversibility:** one-way — pre-registered gate.

- **D-10:** **Re-measure 02.2's three sealed cached fits** (`cae_fit_43cf438bc944c509_seed20260803`,
  `…04`, `…05`) rather than training new ones. They were deliberately built with **C²-smooth
  activations** (SiLU; ReLU explicitly disqualified per DEC-02/CURV-01..03) precisely because
  downstream curvature work needed twice-differentiable decoders — they are curvature-ready by
  construction. Costs minutes rather than hours, and introduces **no new training hyperparameters that
  could be tuned after seeing curvature results**. The sealed `CAE_VERDICT = FAIL` is untouched; this
  measures a different property of the same artifacts.

- **D-11:** **A retrain fallback exists but is fenced: the firing trigger is pre-registered before
  anything runs; if it fires, the retrain's hyperparameters are ratified at a blocking human-verify
  checkpoint before the retrain executes.** "Retrain because the first answer was bad" is choosing a
  method after seeing results; fixing the trigger in advance converts it into a pre-committed branch,
  following 02.4's D-19 convergence-contingency pattern. **Residual risk to state plainly in the
  pre-registration:** the retrain's hyperparameters are ratified with the failing result already
  visible, so that checkpoint is precisely where post-hoc pressure would enter. The checkpoint must
  record what was known at the time it was made. — **Reversibility:** costly — a retrain invalidates
  the "no new tunable knobs" property that D-10 buys.

- **D-12:** **Decision rule: charts must beat raw points' Spearman by a pre-registered margin AND agree
  across 02.2's three sealed seeds within a stated tolerance.** The margin follows 02.4's `THRESH_T1`
  shape (baseline-relative with a margin, rather than a bare must-beat). The seed-stability clause is
  material here specifically because the CAE's claimed advantage **is** noise-averaging — a curvature
  field that varies across seeds would refute exactly that claim. — **Reversibility:** one-way.

  **Outcome routing:**
  - **Charts win** → Phase 3 uses CAE charts.
  - **Raw points win, or tie inside the margin** → open the Ollivier-Ricci phase (see Deferred Ideas).
    This is *not* "the CAE is unnecessary, move on" — it is a fork into the intrinsic-curvature route.
  - **Neither clears the null** → **NOT explicitly decided in this discussion.** Defaults to 02.2's
    D-02 (halt for user decision, no auto-fallback). **Flagged for resolution at pre-registration
    ratification** — planning must surface this, not silently pick.

### What a PASS unblocks

- **D-13:** **Phase 3's dependency is retargeted by a dated ROADMAP amendment.** It currently reads
  "Phase 02.4 **PASS** (the operative precondition)" — which names a **global**-scoped PASS from a
  phase that has now sealed FAIL, making it unsatisfiable as written: a dead pointer, not a live gate.
  The amendment retargets it to 02.5's substrate verdict and states why, additively, retracting
  nothing — the same pattern as 02.4's Erratum 1 and Amendments 1 and 2. — **Reversibility:** costly —
  amending a dependency changes what Phase 3 is allowed to start on.

- **D-14:** **Any 02.5 PASS obliges a revisit of 02.1's sealed graph-native answer, by dated
  amendment.** `02.1-AMENDMENT-02.md` carries a *symmetric* falsifier overturning graph-native when "a
  coordinate candidate's `DISTORTION` drops materially below the classical failure at a tractable
  working dimension." A 02.5 PASS is not literally that condition — the falsifier is written on
  `DISTORTION`, a global distance-preservation statistic, while 02.5 measures curvature recovery — and
  **arguing that difference in the open is exactly what the amendment must do.** 02.4's own ROADMAP
  entry set this precedent. The user's decision is that **any** PASS triggers the revisit, including
  the raw-points branch, on the reasoning that a stale sealed decision should not be left standing
  unexamined. Nothing sealed is reopened by the revisit itself.

- **D-15:** **On PASS, write a machine-readable handoff naming the full estimator contract**, using
  02.4's R6 pattern: verdict artifact **always** written; handoff written **only** on PASS; any stale
  handoff **actively deleted** on FAIL. The handoff must name everything Phase 3 needs to reproduce
  the field — which substrate won, working dimension, neighbourhood rule, density correction,
  estimator variant, and the cache stems / `fit_key` it was computed from. Naming the substrate alone
  is insufficient: Phase 3 could then reconstruct the field under different settings than the ones
  that passed the gate, which is how a validated result quietly stops being validated. The R6
  machinery already exists and is tested in `notebooks/pu_manifold/topoae.py`.

### Claude's Discretion

- Exact form of `f` in the graph-of-function family (must give spatially varying `H` with tractable
  analytic derivatives; beyond that, planner/researcher's call).
- Number of permutation shuffles for the null; sweep granularity on each axis of the feasibility
  boundary; how the Swiss roll anchor and the function family reconcile into one reported frontier.
- How agreement between the two estimators (D-05) is quantified, and whether disagreement is merely
  reported or blocks.
- Whether the tangent projection uses local PCA or an alternative.
- Specific numeric values for the D-02 threshold, D-12 margin, D-12 seed tolerance, and D-11 trigger —
  all proposed by planner/researcher with justification, then **ratified by the user at a blocking
  checkpoint before anything runs** (02.2's D-10 ordering).

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- **The user's own framing that opened this phase:** local charts work, so estimate the second
  fundamental form on each local patch and assemble the field piecewise. That is geometrically sound —
  mean curvature is a local invariant, and a manifold need not admit any global chart to have
  well-defined curvature everywhere (the sphere is the standard counterexample). It corrected an
  over-broad reading in which the milestone's global-coordinate failures were treated as blocking a
  curvature field entirely.
- **The user's observation on the two Swiss roll notebooks** — that the CAE visibly reconstructs the
  roll well while TopoAE does not — is borne out numerically: the CAE prints `Swiss roll recovered:
  True` at 4.8% relative error, 2.2× better than a matched plain-AE, 8/8 charts surviving; TopoAE fails
  its reconstruction checks and passes only on topology. This is why the CAE, not TopoAE, is stage 2's
  candidate substrate.
- **The user's routing decision on a raw-points win** — do not treat it as "the CAE is unnecessary,
  move on", but as a fork into an Ollivier-Ricci phase that finally exercises 02.1's sealed
  recommendation.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **NEW PHASE — Ollivier-Ricci / intrinsic curvature (user-proposed).** Triggered if raw points beat
  the CAE, or recommended if stage 1 fails outright. Applies Ollivier-Ricci curvature to the PU k-NN
  graph and compares autoencoder logic against it. Finally uses 02.1's sealed `GEOM-04 = Ollivier-Ricci
  graph-native` recommendation, unused since 2026-08-05.

  **Important distinction — this is not a third estimator of the same quantity.** Mean curvature `H` is
  **extrinsic**: how the manifold bends in the ambient `ℝ⁷⁶⁸`. Ollivier-Ricci is **intrinsic**: defined
  on the k-NN graph via optimal transport between neighbourhood measures, with no ambient space
  involved. 02.1's own record states this explicitly — CURV-03's "extrinsic mean-curvature vector
  becomes an intrinsic Ricci scalar — a different mathematical object, not a renaming." Phase 4's
  question (does MKNN alignment vary with curvature?) could be asked under either notion, **and the two
  could disagree** — a richer milestone result than committing to one blind. Note this would require
  amending Phase 4's requirement text, which currently refers to `|H|` quantiles.

- **Phase 2's `D_FROZEN = 5` record.** Three phases now diverge from it and 02.4's T3 dose-response
  added corroboration that the intrinsic dimension exceeds 5. Not addressed here: Phase 2 is sealed and
  its dimension freeze was one-way. D-07 handles the practical need by naming `d = 20` in this phase's
  own pre-registration. Whether the milestone should consolidate the accumulated evidence into one
  place is a records question for a later pass.

- **Gap #5 in `topoae.py`** (reconstruction term sums over ambient features where the reference
  implementation means over them) is recorded in `02.4-PREREGISTRATION-AMENDMENT-02.md` as a known,
  deliberately unfixed divergence. Fixing it requires a fresh pre-registration and a full re-run.
  Out of scope here.

</deferred>
