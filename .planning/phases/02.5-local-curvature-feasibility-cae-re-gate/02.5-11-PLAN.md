<objective>
Measure Gate A: the head-to-head between CAE charts and raw points, both scored against a fixture
whose mean curvature is known analytically, at the PU-matched regime stage 1 established.

Purpose: this is the measurement D-09 asks for and the reason stage 2 exists. If the centroid
estimator works as well on raw 768-d points as it does through a learned atlas, no learned model
is needed at all — and that is a legitimate, reportable outcome, not a failure. Only a fixture
with analytic `H` can tell the CAE's noise-averaging benefit apart from a network's second
derivative wiggling where the manifold does not.

Output: `notebooks/diagnostics/cae_local_regate_run.py` through its Gate A stage, and the cached
per-seed Arm A / Arm B measurements.
</objective>
