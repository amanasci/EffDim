# Note — how the stage-2 substrate gets chosen: Swiss roll gates, PU decides

**Status:** design note for plan 02.5-10 (the stage-2 pre-registration) to adopt, reshape, or
decline. **Gates nothing as written.** The candidate substrate list is deliberately left OPEN —
see Section 5. Written 2026-08-10, while plan 02.5-09 sits at an open blocking checkpoint.

## 1. Why this note exists

Plan 02.5-08 built Arm B against the **CAE** chart decoder. Plan 02.5-09 then measured that
decoder failing its Swiss roll check (curvature Spearman `-0.0604` against the raw-point
baseline's `0.6712`, at 8/8 charts). Separately, the quick tasks measured that the CAE's
40-dimensional embedding preserves H0 merge structure roughly 5x WORSE than a plain
auto-encoder's latent at the same dimension (`~0.106` vs `~0.53` on the same 2000 rows), using
one eighth the parameters on the plain side.

Arm B's structural argument — a decoder escapes stage 1's locality wall because it
differentiates an analytic map and never forms a neighbourhood — **does not depend on charts**.
It holds for any smooth decoder. So the substrate is genuinely open in a way it was not when
02.5-08 was planned, and 02.5-10 is the last point it can change before thresholds seal.

## 2. The asymmetry that dictates the design

**On the Swiss roll there is a known answer. On PU there is not.**

- Swiss roll: analytic `H` in closed form (`curvature_probe.swiss_roll_analytic_H_scaled`),
  known intrinsic dimension, known contractibility.
- PU: no analytic curvature, no known Betti numbers, no ground truth of any kind.

This is why stage 2's ratified gate is COMPARATIVE (`chart_vs_raw_margin`, `seed_spread`) rather
than an absolute accuracy threshold — an absolute one is not computable on PU. It is also why
the Swiss roll cannot simply be skipped as a toy: **it is the only place in this entire phase
where a curvature method can be checked against a correct answer at all.**

But it is a GATE, not the deliverable. The phase's question is about PU. A method that aces the
Swiss roll has earned an entry ticket, nothing more.

## 3. The two-stage structure

### Stage A — admission (Swiss roll, known answer, pass/fail per substrate)

Purpose: does this decoder recover curvature that provably exists? Run per candidate substrate,
under the CLAUDE.md sanity-check rules (`notebooks/<phase>_swiss_roll_<model>_check.ipynb`,
model code imported unchanged, trained in-notebook, no `.cache` access, ~15 cells).

Required read-out per substrate, per seed — **all four, reported separately, never collapsed**
(`02.5-NOTE-randomized-trace.md` Addendum C):

1. **direction** — cosine similarity of `H_model` against analytic `H`
2. **magnitude** — `||H_model|| / ||H_true||`, median AND per-point CV
3. **calibration slope** — regress `||H_model||` on `||H_true||`, check `a ~= 1`, `b ~= 0`
4. **rank** — Spearman, alongside the above and never instead of them

Rank alone cannot see amplitude attenuation: a decoder compressing every magnitude by a constant
scores `1.0`. The point-cloud estimator's own Swiss roll numbers give the reference scale —
median ratio `0.905` at CV `0.250` is "attenuated but calibratable"; CV `2.250` with a
non-monotone median is "destroyed".

**Seeds are mandatory, not optional.** 02.5-09 measured Spearman running `-0.0604 / -0.1444 /
0.8665 / 0.4250` across four torch seeds at one configuration. A single-seed admission result
would be noise. Report the spread; a substrate whose across-seed spread exceeds its margin to
threshold has not passed, it has drawn well.

**Failing Stage A disqualifies a substrate from Stage B.** That is the whole point of a gate,
and it is what 02.5-09 correctly did to the CAE.

### Stage B — decision (PU, comparative, decides the phase)

Only substrates that cleared Stage A enter. No ground truth here, so every quantity is
relative — to the raw-point estimator, to a matched plain-AE baseline, and to a null.

Carried forward from stage 1's hard-won lessons, all three of which cost a re-run to learn:

- **Every statistic needs a null and a floor derived from something external.** `0.4444` only
  became interpretable once it had one.
- **Seed replication is not optional.** `02.5-PREREGISTRATION-AMENDMENT-01.md` exists because a
  single-seed verdict at a marginal configuration is not identified by its own data. Whatever
  Stage B gates on, it gates on a replicate-aware statistic.
- **Ambient dimension is free statistically and expensive computationally** — bit-identical
  gating values at `D = 28..768`, at ~9x the wall-clock at `D = 768`.

## 4. What must NOT be inferred from Stage A

Recorded because the inverse error has already been made twice in this phase.

- **Swiss roll topology does not transfer.** The roll is contractible as a manifold, yet its
  ambient Vietoris-Rips diagram carries a genuine `beta_1 = 1` from near-touching spiral arms.
  Scoring a `d=2` latent on "preserve the ambient H1" penalises correct unrolling.
- **Reconstruction quality validates nothing about curvature.** A decoder learning
  `y = 0.7 a x^2` where the truth is `y = a x^2` has tiny reconstruction error and a second
  derivative 30% wrong. The CAE reconstructs the roll 2.96x better than a matched plain AE and
  still produced structureless curvature.
- **H0 preservation is not curvature fidelity.** They are different properties measured by
  different instruments, and nothing yet measured connects them.

## 5. Candidate substrates — OPEN, deliberately

The list is not fixed here. What IS fixed is that every candidate passes through Section 3's
Stage A before reaching Stage B, and that each candidate's admission notebook is required by
CLAUDE.md in its own right ("the model is not done until its Swiss roll notebook exists and
passes").

Currently on the table, with what is known:

| substrate | H0 on PU | Swiss roll curvature | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAE (16 charts) | `0.183` retained | **FAILED** (rho `-0.0604`) | measured; Stage A failed |
| plain AE | `~0.53-0.63` | **untested** | strongest H0 result per parameter |
| TopoAE (02.4) | `~0.55-0.67` | **untested** | already built and sealed in 02.4 |

Effect sizes worth weighing before adding candidates: TopoAE beats a plain AE by ~6% on H0
retention, while a plain AE beats the CAE by ~3.4x. **The large effect is single-chart versus
multi-chart, not which topological loss is attached.** A candidate justified only by a better
topological loss is arguing inside the 6%.

Adding a substrate that does not already exist in this repo (RTD-AE, Witness AE, TopoAE++, or
anything else) is **phase-sized work, not a substitution**: a new model, its own Swiss roll
admission notebook, and its own validity gate. That cost is part of the comparison, not a
footnote to it.

**Open until discussed.** The list above is the floor, not the ceiling.

## 6. What this note does not decide

Not the substrate. Not the Stage B statistic. Not the thresholds. Those belong to 02.5-10's
ratification checkpoint, under the same seal-before-measure discipline stage 1 followed, with
the ordering-transparency obligation that every number already in hand at ratification is
disclosed there — and by then that includes 02.5-09's four-seed table and everything the quick
tasks measured.
