<discretion_decisions>
- **Wave B reuses Wave A's anchor set, neighbourhood panel, out-of-fold predictions and controls
  unchanged.** None of them depends on the autoencoder fit — the anchors come from a seeded split,
  the neighbourhoods are over the ambient rows, and the probe is a global ridge on the embeddings.
  Recomputing them per seed would make the three cells differ by more than the seed and would
  make the wave measure something other than fit variability. The runner asserts they are
  byte-identical to the Wave A anchor table rather than assuming it.
- **The pairwise-Spearman diagnostic between seed fields is added, and it is not optional.** Phase
  5 recorded a case where three "seeds" produced bit-identical maps because the axis that varied
  was not the one being reported, and its SUMMARY had to say so in its accepted gaps. Measuring
  the disagreement directly means this phase either has three genuinely different fields or knows
  that it does not.
- **A failed or non-finite seed is recorded and splits its cell.** Dropping it would let the
  remaining two agree by construction, which is exactly the upgrade `05-03-DECISION.md` forbids.
- **The scope comes from the record, not from a flag.** `WAVE_B_TRIGGER_RULE` was frozen before
  any number existed; letting an operator pass a `d` list would let the scope be widened after
  seeing Wave A, which is selection on the outcome.
- **`WAVE_B_NOT_TRIGGERED` is a recorded outcome, not an absence.** A reader of the record must be
  able to tell "no cell fired in Wave A, so seed stability was not tested" from "seeds were run
  and agreed", and a missing row cannot make that distinction.
</discretion_decisions>
