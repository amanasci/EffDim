<discretion_decisions>
- **The notebook recomputes nothing.** Rendering from the record rather than from the arrays means
  the notebook and `--mode verdict` cannot disagree, and it means the whole report can be
  regenerated from the transferred bundle without the execution host. A notebook that refits would
  also violate the freeze discipline by producing a number outside the gated path.
- **The verdict sentence is quoted, never paraphrased.** `VERDICT_SENTENCE_RULE` was frozen before
  any number existed precisely so the wording could not drift once the number was known. A
  markdown restatement in the notebook would be a second, ungoverned wording.
- **Accepted gaps are a required section, not an appendix.** This milestone has already had to
  retract a claim whose caveat was recorded somewhere other than beside the number
  (`05-02-SUMMARY.md`'s distinct-value claim). Naming the five gaps in the same document as the
  verdict is what keeps them attached to it.
- **A `checkpoint:human-verify`, not a decision.** Nothing is being chosen here — the verdict is
  whatever the frozen rule returned. What needs a human is whether the sentence overstates, which
  is a judgement about wording that no automated check can make.
- **The ROADMAP edit is scoped to the Phase 9 section.** A whole-file rewrite would destroy the
  other sixteen phase entries; scoped replacement is the only permitted form.
</discretion_decisions>
