Two further values enter the same freeze and are put here rather than in a second checkpoint:

- **Sentinel set.** `SENTINEL_VALUES = (-99.0,)`, applied uniformly to every label before any
  statistic. `stellar_mass` documents `-99.0`; applying it to all four is conservative and costs
  nothing where the sentinel does not occur, and Task 1's per-column `n_sentinel` counts show
  exactly where it does occur.
- **Alignment margin.** `ALIGNMENT_MARGIN_R2 = 0.10` — the planner's proposed value for D9-07's
  "pre-registered margin", with no measured precedent. Reasoning: the pass rule is
  `R2(shift 0) - max(every other alignment) > margin`, strictly. Under any wrong alignment the
  out-of-fold R2 of a 768-dimensional ridge at n=86,471 is approximately zero with a sampling
  standard error of order 0.005, so 0.10 is roughly a twenty-sigma floor — high enough that noise
  cannot clear it, low enough that a genuinely aligned but weakly predictable label is not failed
  for being weakly predictable. The margin is a floor on the GAP, not a threshold on the aligned
  R2 itself.

**Undo cost if wrong:** the column mapping is one-way. It is read by the alignment proof, the
out-of-fold probe, every local R2, every control and every verdict cell. Changing it after any
number exists means a fresh freeze commit and a complete re-run of the execution-host workload —
four autoencoder fits at 86,471 rows plus every gate. The sentinel set and the margin carry the
same cost for the same reason.
  </context>

<discretion_decisions>
- **One checkpoint, three values.** The column mapping, the sentinel set and the alignment margin
  all enter the same freeze commit and all carry the same undo cost. Splitting them into three
  checkpoints would be verification fatigue for one decision surface; combining them into a single
  `checkpoint:decision` with a three-way `resume-signal` keeps the developer's attention on the one
  thing that is genuinely contested — how `09-CONTEXT.md`'s out-of-scope line reads.
- **No checkpoint is raised for D9-08, D9-10, D9-11 or D9-17**, though `09-CONTEXT.md` rates all
  four one-way. Those doors were already walked through in `/gsd-discuss-phase`: the developer
  chose SEARCH over halt, chose the no-magnitude-threshold verdict rule, chose `||H_tan||` to
  carry the verdict, and inherited the do-not-pool ratification. Re-asking would be gating a
  decision already made. Their ratings are carried on the tasks that implement them, for the
  record.
- **`ALIGNMENT_MARGIN_R2 = 0.10` is the planner's proposed value, not a measured one**, and it is
  labelled as such in the checkpoint. It is a floor on the GAP between the assumed alignment and
  the best wrong alignment, not a threshold on the aligned R2, so it does not encode any
  expectation about how predictable r-band magnitude is from a 768-dimensional embedding.
- **The manifest runs on the developer's machine, not the execution host.** It is data acquisition
  producing metadata; the execution-host rule constrains where Physics numbers are produced. The
  host will re-download for its own runs, which is why the loaders are cache-keyed and
  environment-configurable.
</discretion_decisions>
