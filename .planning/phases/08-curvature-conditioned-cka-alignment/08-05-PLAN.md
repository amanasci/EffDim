<objective>
Run Phase 8 on real PU geometry, in the order the spike-findings estimator-validation protocol
prescribes: the controls first, the result last. The positive control (D8-18) measures what the test
*could* have seen at PU's realized dynamic range; the negative control (D8-19) measures how often the
machinery manufactures a gap from nothing; only then does the sweep (D8-09/D8-13/D8-15) produce the
per-`d` and per-seed verdicts.

first is what makes their numbers available *beside* the headline rather than after it, which is the
whole of D8-21's mechanism. `08-CONTEXT.md` states the consequence in its own words: *"Phase 8 has no
structural mechanism preventing an artifact from being written up as a result. Every safeguard is now
a reporting obligation."*

`notebooks/.cache/08_cka_alignment.jsonl`.
