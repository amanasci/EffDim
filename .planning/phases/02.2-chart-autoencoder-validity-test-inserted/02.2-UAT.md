### 4. eq. 5 pre-training does not do what the paper's pre-training does
expected: |
  Paper: "we use furthest point sampling (FPS) to select N points x_alpha from the training
  set as seeds for each chart. Then, we separately pre-train each chart encoder and decoder
  pair, such that x_alpha is at the center of the chart space."
  Implementation (cae.py train_cae stage one, FPS_PRETRAIN_EPOCHS = 5):
  - FPS runs on model.encode(x_train) — the UNTRAINED initial encoder's output — not on the
    training set in ambient space
  - all 16 charts train jointly in one optimizer step, not "separately"
  - 5 full-batch AdamW steps at lr 3e-4 on 16 points total
  Its own history shows the stage is near-inert: recon 2.073 -> 1.679 over 5 steps, xent flat
  at 2.7732 = ln(16) — the chart predictor was never moved off uniform before the main loop
  began. The paper's stated purpose ("so that the range of each decoder lies somewhere on
  the manifold and the probability that a randomly sampled point lies in each chart is
  approximately equal") was not achieved.

### 5. The paper's chart-death mechanism is numerically inert in this run
expected: |
  Paper obtains the chart count a posteriori: over-specify N, let weight decay drive the
  weights of unused charts to zero, "remove these charts when the norm of the chart decoder
  weights falls below some tolerance".
  Implementation uses AdamW with weight_decay 1e-4 and lr 3e-4. AdamW's decoupled decay
  shrinks a weight by lr*wd = 3e-08 per step. Over 36 epochs x 125 batches = 4,500 steps the
  total shrinkage is ~0.014%. Measured chart-decoder spectral products span 8.93 .. 17.93 —
  a 2x spread, nowhere near a PRUNE_TOL = 0.01 ratio.
  So "16/16 charts survived on all three seeds" (02.2-FINDINGS.md section 4) is not evidence
  that the atlas was healthy; the pruning mechanism could not have fired regardless of chart
  health. That finding is currently interpreted as informative and is not.
  Separately, the criterion itself deviates: paper says norm of the decoder weights against
  an absolute tolerance; implementation uses the product of per-layer spectral norms as a
  ratio against the largest chart.

### 6. Undocumented deviations in the loss definitions
expected: |
  (a) eq. 5 third term, paper verbatim: "+ sum_beta delta_{alpha beta} log(p_beta)".
      Implementation uses -log(p_alpha). The implementation's sign is the sensible one (the
      paper's literal sign would push p_alpha to zero — almost certainly a typo), but the
      correction is nowhere recorded as a deviation from the source.
  (b) eq. 3's target distribution ell = softmax(-e) is .detach()ed in chart_loss. The paper
      writes ell as a function of the network's own reconstruction errors and does not
      specify a stop-gradient. Detaching removes the gradient path that would push the e_alpha
      apart across charts — directly relevant to a run whose charts stayed identical.
  Neither (a) nor (b) appears in 02.2-PREREGISTRATION.md's deviation list or in FINDINGS
  section 5 "Deviations and assumptions carried forward".

### 8. Confirmed faithful, plus accepted minor deviations
expected: |
  Faithful to the paper, verified line by line:
  - eq. 3: min_alpha e_alpha - sum_beta ell_beta log(p_beta), e_alpha = ||x - y_alpha||^2 — exact
  - eq. 4: max_alpha(prod_k ||W||_2) + mean_alpha(prod_k ||W||_2), chart encoders only — exact
  - eq. 8: both directions, unsquared L2 — exact
  - eqs. 19/20/21: recon error, unfaithfulness, coverage — exact (l = 1000 vs paper's 100,
    documented)
  - architecture topology: E: R^m -> R^l, E_alpha: R^l -> (0,1)^d, D_alpha: Z_alpha -> R^l,
    ONE shared D: R^l -> R^m, softmax P on z, argmax-p_alpha final output — matches the paper
  - l = 2d (L_EMBED 40, D_CHART 20) per the Nash-Kuiper motivation — matches
  - lr 3e-4, batch 64, Lipschitz penalty 1e-2 — match the paper's section 5 verbatim
  Minor deviations, judged acceptable but worth your sign-off:
  - MAX_EPOCHS 40 vs the paper's 100 (all CAE fits early-stopped, so not binding; the plain-AE
    and MDS controls DID hit the 40 cap, so the controls that beat the CAE were themselves
    under-trained)
  - eq. 6 (R_coords, PCA chart-orientation regularizer) omitted — the paper marks it optional
    ("if desirable"), but consistent chart orientation is exactly what Phase 3's curvature
    decode would want

    "the Lipschitz constant of ReLU is 1". SiLU is ~1.0998 and the sigmoid output layer is
    0.25, so the penalty no longer bounds the quantity the paper says it bounds. The
    activation swap is documented (CAE-06) and measured; this consequence for eq. 4 is not.
