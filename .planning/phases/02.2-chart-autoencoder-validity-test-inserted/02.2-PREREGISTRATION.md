# Phase 02.2 Chart Auto-Encoder — Pre-Registration

**Written and committed before any CAE fit runs.** Fixes the three gating metric definitions,
their numeric thresholds, and every architecture/compute constant in advance, mirroring
`02-REFIT-PREREGISTRATION.md` (committed 057b084 before any re-fit ran). A CAE trained without
a prior rule is retry-until-pass wearing a different name.

## 1. Trigger

02.1's Recommendation closed Phase 3's representation question by recommending Krein
retention, but that recommendation is not this phase's subject: this phase empirically tests
whether the Chart Auto-Encoder method (Schonsheck, Chen, Lai — arXiv:1912.10094) is a valid
*alternative* representation of the PU embedding manifold, standalone, against its own
pre-registered thresholds (D-01). Its motivating Theorem 1 says a plain single-chart
autoencoder cannot ε-faithfully represent a manifold with non-contractible topology — a
different assumption than the *global* Euclidean embeddability Phase 2's FAIL falsified,
since CAE only assumes *local* Euclideanness via overlapping charts. CAE-01 requires this
ordering proved by git ancestry: pre-registration document first, human ratification second,
training third.

## 2. Architecture rationale

**Chart latent dimension `D_CHART = 20`.** Justified by the local intrinsic dimension cluster
measured in `02-FINDINGS.md` §6.3/§6.4: TwoNN estimate 19.5, local-PCA median 25.0 (mean 24.5,
std 2.0, 5-95% range 21-28, no neighbourhood above 29). Charts are local objects covering
local neighbourhoods of the manifold, so the matching referent for their dimension is a local
intrinsic-dimension estimate, not a global one. 02.1's global pseudo-Euclidean elbow `(8, 0)`
is deliberately **NOT** inherited for the chart dimension: that elbow answers a global
diminishing-returns budget question over the *whole* geodesic spectrum, a different question
than "how many coordinates does one local chart need to cover its neighbourhood." `D_CHART=20`
sits inside the measured local range 19-29, close to the TwoNN point estimate of 19.5.

**Initial-encoder embedding dimension `L_EMBED = 40`.** Approximately `2 * D_CHART`, following
the paper's Nash-Kuiper motivation for `l ≈ 2d`: a near-isometric embedding into a space of
roughly twice the target intrinsic dimension.

**Initial chart count `N_CHARTS_INIT = 16`.** Over-specified deliberately, then pruned a
posteriori via weight decay plus the Lipschitz regularizer driving unused chart-decoder
weights toward zero, with a stated norm tolerance (`PRUNE_TOL`) deciding which charts survive.
Phase 1 confirmed the PU point cloud is a single connected component at k*=15; 16 initial
charts gives ample headroom over the paper's own 4-chart double-torus example. The surviving
chart count after pruning is a **reported outcome of the fit, not a tuned input** — it is not
adjusted to hit any target number.

**Activation: `SiLU` throughout, per CAE-06.** Every hidden layer in every encoder and decoder
(`InitialEncoder`, `ChartEncoder`, `ChartDecoder`, `EmbeddingDecoder`) uses SiLU, replacing the
paper's reference-implementation ReLU. ReLU's second derivative is identically zero almost
everywhere, which is incompatible with Phase 3's downstream `torch.func` Jacobian/Hessian
curvature computation (DEC-02/CURV-01..03) — a curvature field derived from a piecewise-linear
decoder is degenerate. SiLU is C-infinity smooth and a standard drop-in replacement. The
chart-encoder output layer uses `sigmoid` so chart coordinates land in the open unit cube
`(0,1)^20` as the paper's chart latent space requires. The chart predictor's output layer uses
`softmax` to produce partition-of-unity probabilities over surviving charts. The substitution
cost — the reconstruction-quality delta this activation swap introduces — is measured against
a ReLU-activated control fit trained at `MAIN_SEED` under the identical protocol and reported
in the verdict artifact, not merely asserted to be small.

**Theorem 2's compactness/reach hypothesis — recorded as an unverified assumption, not a
verified fact.** The paper's Theorem 2 states that a CAE with more charts than the intrinsic
dimension ε-faithfully represents a manifold `M`, under the hypothesis that `M` is **compact
with positive reach `tau`**, and its sample-complexity bound (`n > beta_1 * (log(beta_2) +
log(1/nu))`, with `beta_1, beta_2` depending on `epsilon`, `tau`, and the density ratio
`C = vol(M) / vol(B_1^d)`) has never been checked against this phase's `n = 10,000` because
neither `tau` nor `C` has ever been estimated for the PU point cloud. Theorem 2 is cited in
this document and in `02.2-FINDINGS.md` as **motivation** for attempting the CAE architecture,
never as evidence that this specific fit will or did succeed — that evidence is exclusively
the three measured gate metrics in Section 4 below.

### Gating metric T1 — geodesic distortion

Computed on the **initial encoder's global embedding `z = E(x)` in `R^40`**, for all 10,000
rows — **never** on chart-local coordinates `z_alpha in (0,1)^20` (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3: two
points assigned to different charts have coordinates in unrelated parameterisations, so a
distance between them in chart-local space carries no geometric meaning).

Pairs are the frozen cached 200,000-pair sample. The runner re-draws them with
`draw_geo_pairs` under `PAIR_SEED = 20260731` and **halts** unless the redraw is bit-identical
to the cached `geo_pairs_r2` array. `d2_geo = geo_pairs_r2.astype(np.float64) ** 2` — the
cached array holds **unsquared** geodesic distances (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 5).
`d2_rep[i] = ||z[rows[i]] - z[cols[i]]||^2`.

Because a neural encoder carries no built-in constraint tying its output scale to the
geodesic scale, exactly **one global isotropic scale factor `s`** is granted and pre-registered
here — fit on training-split pairs only, one scalar, applied to all pairs:

```
s = median(d2_geo / d2_rep)   over the subset of pairs with BOTH endpoints in the training split
```

`s` is then applied to all 200,000 pairs and reported verbatim in `cae_verdict_{fit_key}.json`'s
`global_scale_factor` field. The statistic is
`geometry_probes.distortion_stats(s * d2_rep, d2_geo)["median_abs_rel"]` — the same function
02.1 used, imported not reimplemented.

**PASS iff `median_abs_rel < THRESH_DISTORTION_MAX = 0.15`.**

Argument for the threshold: 02.1's classical q=0 baseline at the matched dimension p=40 is
`median_abs_rel = 0.179641`, and its best rung at p=8 is `0.121024`. `0.15` sits materially
below the matched-dimension linear baseline (0.179641) without demanding the CAE beat the
best classical rung anywhere (0.121024). Granting the CAE one free global scale factor is a
disclosed generosity the linear baselines did not need — their scale comes from the
eigendecomposition by construction, so there is no analogous free parameter for them.

Also report, **non-gating**: the same statistic restricted to pairs with both endpoints in the
holdout split.

### Gating metric T2 — chart-transition cycle residual

`R_cycle(x)` is the paper's eq. 8 verbatim: the sum of two unsquared L2 norms obtained by
encoding and decoding `x` through chart `alpha`, then re-encoding and decoding that result
through chart `beta` and comparing to `x`, plus the symmetric `beta`-then-`alpha` direction.
Evaluated on **holdout rows only**.

For each holdout row, the pair `(alpha, beta)` is the two highest chart-predictor
probabilities among surviving (post-pruning) charts. A row is admitted to the overlap
evaluation set only if **both** `p_alpha >= OVERLAP_P_MIN = 0.05` **and**
`p_beta >= OVERLAP_P_MIN` — this cutoff is pre-registered here because without it the
evaluation set is ill-defined and could be narrowed to high-confidence single-chart points
that trivially give near-zero residual. If fewer than `OVERLAP_MIN_POINTS = 200` holdout rows
qualify, **T2 is undefined and the run halts** rather than reporting a value.

The matched base quantity is `E_base(x) = 2 * ||x - yhat(x)||` — twice the single-pass
argmax-chart reconstruction norm, matching eq. 8's two-term unsquared form.

**PASS iff `mean(R_cycle) < THRESH_RCYCLE_RATIO_MAX * mean(E_base)` with
`THRESH_RCYCLE_RATIO_MAX = 2.0`.**

Argument for the threshold: no absolute reference value for `R_cycle` exists in the paper or
this codebase, so the bound is relative to the model's own reconstruction noise —
round-tripping through a second chart must not more than double the base error.

### Gating metric T3 — held-out reconstruction versus baselines

`mse_cae` is the mean over holdout rows of `||x - yhat(x)||^2 / 768`, with `yhat` the
argmax-chart reconstruction.

Two gating baselines at matched capacity:

- `mse_plain_ae20` — the paper's own eq. 22 deterministic autoencoder architecture (three
  hidden layers of width 250 each side, bottleneck 20, N=1 chart, no cross-entropy term),
  trained under the identical protocol.
- `mse_mds_dec8` — a decoder fitted from the cached classical-MDS coordinates
  `mds_coords[:, :8]` back to `R^768` on the training rows, evaluated on holdout, with the
  decoder matched to the plain-AE decoder half.

Record explicitly: `sklearn.manifold.Isomap`'s `.reconstruction_error()` method is **NOT**
the baseline (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4 — it reports a residual-variance statistic of the graph
embedding, not a held-out reconstruction back to the 768-d ambient space); classical MDS has
no native inverse map to ambient space, so one is fit explicitly.

**PASS iff `mse_cae < (1 - THRESH_RECON_MARGIN) * mse_plain_ae20` AND
`mse_cae < (1 - THRESH_RECON_MARGIN) * mse_mds_dec8` with `THRESH_RECON_MARGIN = 0.10`.**

Argument for the threshold: CAE-03 says a CAE that merely ties the control has not
demonstrated that charts bought anything — a strict 10% relative margin over **both** controls
is required.

Non-gating companions reported: `mse_plain_ae40`, `mse_mds_dec20`, a linear least-squares MDS
decoder at p=8 as a floor, and the per-output-dimension MSE distribution (mean, median, p95,
max over the 768 dimensions) for the CAE and each baseline.

## 5. Interpretation rule — fixed before any fit

`CAE_VERDICT = PASS` iff T1 AND T2 AND T3 all hold; otherwise `FAIL`. **Every comparison is
strict less-than — a value exactly at a threshold does not clear it.**

**Deliberate deviation from Phase 2's gate: there is no MARGINAL tier here.** D-02 routes any
non-PASS outcome to the same halt-for-user-decision consequence, so a middle tier would carry
no distinct consequence and would only add ambiguity to a binary decision.

**D-01 rule.** The Krein comparison from 02.1 (`(8,0)` pseudo-Euclidean coordinates) is
computed and reported in the verdict artifact as `krein_comparison_reported_not_gating` and is
**not** load-bearing for PASS/FAIL — this gate judges CAE only against its own pre-registered
thresholds.

**D-02 rule.** On FAIL: the run halts, findings are written to `02.2-FINDINGS.md`, the
milestone remains at the phase-2 stage, and **no downstream notebook or plan may fall back to
the 02.1 Krein handoff automatically** — the user decides the next move (adopt Krein, iterate
CAE, or something else).

**D-03 rule.** On PASS: a machine-readable `cae_handoff_{fit_key}.json` is emitted naming
exactly what Phase 3 consumes — the global embedding `z = E(x)`, the chart coordinates, the
chart assignments and probabilities, and the trained chart and embedding decoders.

## 6. Prohibitions

The thresholds and metric definitions above are **not revisable here** — a change after any
measured number from the frozen subsample exists is a separate documented amendment plus a
full re-run, never a quiet edit (mirrors `02-REFIT-PREREGISTRATION.md`'s prohibition
phrasing). In addition:

- No re-running the protocol with fresh seeds and reporting only a passing run.
- No post-hoc narrowing of the T2 overlap set.
- No tuning `PRUNE_TOL` until the surviving chart count looks agreeable.
- No handicapping the T3 controls by shorter training or smaller capacity than the CAE itself.
- No evaluating any gating metric on rows the model trained on.
- No substituting a different subsample or column than the frozen `legacysurvey` array under
  `fit_key = 43cf438bc944c509`.
- No presenting Theorem 2's epsilon-faithfulness guarantee as evidence that this fit will or
  did succeed — reach `tau` and the density ratio `C` were never estimated for the PU point
  cloud, so the theorem is cited as motivation only (Section 2).
- No auto-falling-back to the Krein/02.1 representation, and no continuing any downstream
  Phase-3 work, on a FAIL verdict (D-02) — a FAIL halts for the user's decision.

## 7. Expected cost

Per D-07: at most 2 wall-clock hours per fit on laptop CPU under torch 2.13.0+cpu. Per D-08:
three independent CAE seeds total (`SEEDS`) — verdict metrics computed on `MAIN_SEED`, with
the two replicates supplying chart-count stability and metric spread (CAE-05). One
ReLU-activated control fit at `MAIN_SEED` for the CAE-06 substitution-cost measurement. Two
plain-AE controls (`PLAIN_AE_LATENTS = (20, 40)`) and two MDS-decoder baselines
(`MDS_BASELINE_P = (8, 20)`), all small relative to the main CAE fits.

Rough envelope: 3 CAE seeds x <=2h + 1 ReLU control x <=2h ~= 8h, plus small baseline fits
(<=0.5h total) and the timing probe (a few minutes) — approximately 8.7 hours total against
D-08's overnight 8-10 hour budget.
