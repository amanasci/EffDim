## 1. What was tested and why

02.1's Recommendation closed Phase 3's representation question in favour of retaining
02.1's Krein (pseudo-Euclidean) coordinates, but that recommendation was never this
phase's subject. Phase 02.2 empirically tests, standalone and on its own pre-registered
thresholds, whether the Chart Auto-Encoder method (Schonsheck, Chen, Lai —
arXiv:1912.10094) is a *valid alternative* representation of the same PU embedding
manifold Phase 2's classical-MDS gate failed on. The method's motivating claim (its
Theorem 1) is that a single-chart autoencoder cannot ε-faithfully represent a manifold
with non-contractible topology — a different assumption than the *global* Euclidean
embeddability Phase 2 falsified, since a Chart Auto-Encoder (CAE) only assumes *local*
Euclideanness inside overlapping charts. If the CAE atlas cleared its own thresholds,
Phase 3 would decode curvature from it instead of from 02.1's Krein coordinates; that
did not happen.

Every threshold, metric definition, and architecture/compute constant below was fixed
in `02.2-PREREGISTRATION.md`, committed at `c2c4c93`, and ratified by the user on
2026-08-04 **before any fit ran** — proved by git ancestry (CAE-01), not merely
asserted. No number below informed any of those choices; the pre-registration is
unedited except for this document's own outcome section (§9 there).

## 3. Results — the three gates

**Interpretation rule (fixed before any fit, §5 of the pre-registration): `CAE_VERDICT
= PASS` iff T1 AND T2 AND T3 all hold. Every comparison is strict less-than — a value
exactly at a threshold does not clear it. There is no MARGINAL tier: any non-PASS
outcome routes to the same halt-for-user-decision consequence (D-02).**

| Gate | Metric | Measured value | Threshold | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 — geodesic distortion | `median_abs_rel` on the initial encoder's global embedding, 200,000 pairs | **0.296981** | `< 0.15` | **FAIL** |
| T2 — chart-transition cycle residual | `mean(R_cycle) / mean(E_base)`, 2,000 qualifying holdout rows | **1.089366** | `< 2.0` | PASS |
| T3 — held-out reconstruction margin | `max(mse_cae/mse_plain_ae20, mse_cae/mse_mds_dec8)` | **3.586350** | `< 0.90`¹ | **FAIL** |

¹ T3's gate value is the pre-registration's own `mse_cae < (1 - THRESH_RECON_MARGIN) *
mse_control` rule for both controls, divided through by `mse_control` on each side so
the comparison stays strict-less-than in the same direction as T1/T2 — algebraically
identical to the ratified rule (`THRESH_RECON_MARGIN = 0.10`), not a reinterpretation
of it. See `metrics.t3.recon_margin_gate_note` in the verdict artifact.

**Two of three gates failed. `CAE_VERDICT = FAIL`.**

T1's failure is not a near-miss: 0.296981 is roughly double the pre-registered
threshold, and materially worse than *both* of 02.1's classical `q=0` reference rungs
(p=40: 0.179641; p=8: 0.121024) — the CAE's global embedding is a worse isometric
coordinate system than the plain linear classical-MDS baselines it was meant to beat.

T3's failure is the more striking of the two: the 16-chart CAE's held-out
reconstruction MSE (1.254e-04) is **worse**, not merely insufficiently better, than
every one of its four comparison points —

| Model | `mse_per_dim` (holdout) |
|---|---|
| CAE (16 charts, main seed) | 1.254476e-04 |
| ReLU control (16 charts) | 1.241643e-04 |
| Plain AE, latent 20 (gating control) | 3.497920e-05 |
| Plain AE, latent 40 (non-gating) | 3.408036e-05 |
| MDS decoder, p=8 (gating control) | 5.670749e-05 |
| MDS decoder, p=20 (non-gating) | 5.153384e-05 |
| MDS decoder, p=8, linear floor | 8.146726e-05 |
| MDS decoder, p=20, linear floor | 6.668850e-05 |

— roughly 3.6× worse than the matched-capacity single-chart plain autoencoder and 2.2×
worse than the classical-MDS decoder at p=8. The atlas of 16 charts did not merely fail
to clear a 10% margin; it reconstructs markedly worse than the single-chart
alternatives it was meant to outperform.

## 4. Non-gating evidence

**Chart survival (CAE-05).** All 16 initial charts survived pruning at
`PRUNE_TOL = 0.01` on every one of the three seeds — 16/16 (main seed 20260803),
16/16 (20260804), 16/16 (20260805). No chart was pruned; the a-posteriori mechanism
found no dead chart to remove. This is itself informative alongside T3's failure: with
every chart surviving and still reconstructing worse than one chart trained alone, the
atlas structure was not the bottleneck the paper's motivation predicts weight-decay
pruning would resolve.

**Unfaithfulness / coverage (eqs. 20/21).** `unfaithfulness = 0.042333`,
`coverage = 0.024` over 1,000 samples drawn round-robin across the 16 surviving charts
(only 24 of 1,000 decoded chart-space samples had a *distinct* nearest training point —
a low coverage figure, consistent with a chart-space atlas that is not tightly wrapping
the training manifold).

**CAE-06 activation substitution cost.** SiLU (the pre-registered, C2-smooth
substitution) versus a ReLU-activated control trained under the otherwise identical
protocol: `mse_relu_control = 1.241643e-04`, `mse_silu_main = 1.254476e-04`,
`delta(relu − silu) = -1.283e-06`. The negative sign means the SiLU substitution cost a
small amount of reconstruction quality relative to ReLU — the ReLU control reconstructs
marginally *better* than the pre-registered SiLU model, though both are dwarfed by the
gap to the single-chart controls above. This measured cost is small relative to T3's
failure margin and is not the explanation for it.

**Krein comparison — reported, not gating (D-01).** Read directly from 02.1's own
cached `geometry_probes_43cf438bc944c509.json`, not recomputed:
`classical_q0_p40_median_abs_rel = 0.179641`, `classical_q0_p8_median_abs_rel =
0.121024`, working-dimension elbow `(p, q) = (8, 0)`. Both classical rungs are
materially better than the CAE's own T1 distortion (0.296981). This comparison is
carried in the verdict artifact under `krein_comparison_reported_not_gating` and
`gates_pass_fail: false` — it did not adjudicate this gate, which judged the CAE only
against its own pre-registered thresholds, and it is not offered here as the reason
for the FAIL, only as context.

**T1 holdout-only companion (non-gating).** Restricted to the 7,970 of 200,000 pairs
with both endpoints in the holdout split: `median_abs_rel = 0.296659` — materially
identical to the all-pairs figure (0.296981), so the distortion is not an artifact of
overfitting the training split.

## 5. Deviations and assumptions carried forward

- **CAE-06 (recorded per the pre-registration's own instruction).** The C2-smooth
  SiLU-for-ReLU activation substitution measured a reconstruction cost of
  `-1.283e-06` (SiLU very slightly worse than ReLU) — see §4 above. This is a genuine
  measurement, not an assumption, and its magnitude is far smaller than what would be
  needed to explain T1 or T3's failures.
- **Theorem 2's compact-manifold-with-positive-reach hypothesis — unverified, recorded
  as an assumption, not evidence.** Neither the reach `tau` nor the density ratio
  `C = vol(M)/vol(B_1^d)` has ever been estimated for the PU point cloud, so Theorem 2's
  ε-faithfulness guarantee and its sample-complexity bound were never checked against
  `n = 10,000`. The theorem motivated attempting this architecture; it predicts nothing
  about whether this specific fit passed or failed. That evidence is exclusively the
  three measured gate metrics in §3. This assumption is carried verbatim into
  `cae_verdict_43cf438bc944c509.json`'s `assumptions` array.
- **CAE-03 "matched capacity"** is operationalised as identical hidden width (250),
  identical layer count per side (3), and the identical training protocol between the
  CAE and its plain-AE/MDS-decoder controls — an assumption disclosed in the
  pre-registration, unchanged here.
- **CAE-04's overlap-pair rule** pairs each qualifying holdout row with its two
  highest-probability surviving charts; the paper supplies no rule for three or more
  charts clearing the cutoff simultaneously. All 2,000 holdout rows qualified under
  this rule (well above the `OVERLAP_MIN_POINTS = 200` floor), so T2's evaluation set
  was never at risk of being under-populated.
- **No threshold, metric definition, overlap-pair rule, or pruning tolerance was
  revised at any point after a measured number existed.** The pre-registration's §4/§5
  are byte-identical to their ratified form; only this document and §9 of the
  pre-registration were written after the fits ran.
