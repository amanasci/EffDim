## Shared Patterns

### Weight=0 as structural no-op
**Source:** `notebooks/pu_manifold/decoder_priors.py` (per RESEARCH Pattern 1) —
`decoder_prior_active` returns immediately at `weight == 0.0` without touching `cae.chart_loss`.
**Apply to:** every D-05 baseline cell in the new runner; never special-case "no prior" as
separate code, and never re-run a `weight=0.0` cell already recorded under a different mode
label (`cell_key`'s `"any"` sentinel, `swiss_roll_isometry_prior_sweep_run.py` lines 98-106).

### Resumable JSONL runner idiom
**Source:** `synthetic_control_run.py` lines 180-198 / `swiss_roll_isometry_prior_sweep_run.py`
lines 82-129 (near-identical). **Apply to:** the new D-13 runner's `load_completed`/
`append_record`, keyed by `config_id` or a `(mode, weight, seed)` tuple.

### Exact-equality anchor check before trusting a copied runner
**Source:** `swiss_roll_isometry_prior_sweep_run.py` `anchor_check` (lines 281-306), and
`synthetic_control_run.py`'s own sealed row `control_saddle_nc4_d20_ep300` /
`rho_spearman = -0.015106571347065712` (the value D-13's anchor check must reproduce with `==`).
**Apply to:** the D-13 runner, mandatorily, before any ladder cell is trusted (D-13's own text).

### Four axes, never collapsed
**Source:** `_fidelity_axes` in `synthetic_control_run.py` (lines 289-356) and `bias_check` in
the sweep runner (lines 487-534) — cosine / magnitude ratio (+CV) / calibration (slope,
intercept, R²) / Spearman rho, always printed as four separate columns, never combined into one
score. **Apply to:** the D-13 runner's Tier 2 read-out and the D-14 notebook's §5/§7.

### Two-tier mechanism-then-estimand verdict
**Source:** `mechanism_check`/`bias_check` in `swiss_roll_isometry_prior_sweep_run.py`
(lines 467-534) — the exact precedent for D-08's Tier 1/Tier 2 split, including the "if Tier 1
fails, no other column is read as evidence" refusal (line ~479-483). **Apply to:** the D-13
runner's summary/print step, retargeted to print `λ_min`/`λ_max`/`det(g)`/`cond(g)` in Tier 1
rather than `cond(g)` alone.

### Held-out reconstruction as bias guard
**Source:** `synthetic_control_run.py` `recon_stats = cae.reconstruction_stats(x_holdout64,
recon_holdout)` (lines 396-397) and its `reconstruction` field in the JSONL record (line 451).
**Apply to:** every cell of the D-13 runner, printed beside the fidelity axes per D-09.
