<objective>
Prove the combination arm's mechanism composes rather than replaces, and discharge CLAUDE.md's
mandatory Swiss roll check for the two priors this phase measures.

Purpose: two things must be true before eight hours of saddle compute is spent. First, D-12's
combination arm is two nested `with decoder_prior_active(...)` blocks and nothing else — if
nesting replaced rather than composed, the combination cell would silently be a single-mode cell.
Second, CLAUDE.md's Swiss roll rule is not optional and `03-NOTE-isometry-prior-spike.md` §6
records the obligation as **undischarged**: a Swiss roll prior check must exist and pass before
any PU fit runs with a non-zero prior weight. Running it now, on a manifold whose answer is known
in advance, is what makes a saddle null interpretable — the same logic as D-08's Tier 1.

Output: two nesting tests beside the existing 23 in `test_decoder_priors.py`, and
`notebooks/03.1_swiss_roll_decoder_prior_check.ipynb` committed executed with outputs.

**Requirement note:** the notebook reports `λ_min` / `λ_max` / `log10 det(g)` on the roll as its
structural pass/fail line, which is CURV-04's new instrumentation demonstrated on a manifold with
a known answer — the reason this plan depends on `03.1-01` rather than running beside it.

**Compute note:** the notebook targets under two minutes end to end on CPU, read as a
whole-notebook budget (CLAUDE.md's stated purpose is "cheap enough to re-run on a whim"), not two
minutes per fit. Nothing in this plan is timing-sensitive in the way `03.1-03`'s probe is.
</objective>
