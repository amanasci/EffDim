# Phase 02.6 Plan 12: Plain-AE and TopoAE Persistent-Homology Swiss Roll Notebooks Summary

**Two CLAUDE.md-mandated Swiss roll sanity notebooks for the plain-AE and TopoAE
persistent-homology candidates -- built, gate-checked, then rebuilt and re-gated a second
time after an upstream training-protocol defect was found and repaired mid-plan; both
notebooks now reconstruct the roll (6.1% / 7.2% held-out error), hold the D-03
intrinsic-vs-ambient H1 signature, and record a new observation -- a collapsed high-t
endpoint in both models' latents -- that the linear-Spearman boolean alone would have
missed.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~8h25m wall-clock (includes coordinator review holds for the protocol-repair
  correction and two checkpoint round-trips); active execution ~50min
- **Completed:** 2026-08-11
- **Tasks:** 3 (2 `type="auto"`, 1 `type="checkpoint:human-verify"`, gate closed twice)
- **Files modified:** 2 (both new)

## Accomplishments

- `notebooks/02.6_swiss_roll_plainae_ph_check.ipynb`: 15 cells, imports `cae.PlainAutoEncoder`
  and `persistence_probe.readout_matrix` unchanged from `notebooks/pu_manifold/`, generates the
  roll in-notebook (`sklearn.datasets.make_swiss_roll`, `n_samples=3000`, `noise=0.0`,
  `random_state=20260807`), trains from scratch, and prints all 16 `READOUT_CELLS` labels plus
  a chance null.
- `notebooks/02.6_swiss_roll_topoae_ph_check.ipynb`: mirrors the plain-AE notebook cell for
  cell with three changes -- `topoae.train_topoae` in §3, a genuinely matched
  `cae.PlainAutoEncoder` baseline (identical width/depth/protocol/bottleneck, retrained fresh
  under the same shared config) carried through §4 and §6, and boolean (d) requiring the
  TopoAE arm to beat both that baseline and the chance null.
- **Protocol repair mid-plan.** Both notebooks were built, executed, and had their blocking
  `checkpoint:human-verify` gate returned once under the plan's literal `CFG` (`lr=3e-4`,
  `max_epochs=150`, no early stop). The coordinator then identified that this config left
  `plainae`/`topoae` undertrained relative to the CAE arm measured elsewhere in this phase
  (`decoder_substrate_ph_screen_run.py`'s own repair, commits `d8aa0bd`/`313fbbe`, made under
  plan `02.6-11`) -- a controlled probe found a ~3.5x reconstruction-error reduction from
  letting the model converge. Both notebooks were rebuilt with `CFG` now matching that
  runner's authoritative `CFG_COMMON` verbatim (`lr=1e-3`, `max_epochs=800`,
  `early_stop_patience=25`, `early_stop_min_delta=1e-4`) and re-executed end to end. The
  checkpoint was returned to the human a second time with the new numbers; that second
  submission is the one that closed.
- **Measured improvement from the repair.** Held-out relative error: plain AE 14.7% -> 6.1%
  (`epochs_run=156`, `early_stopped=True`); TopoAE 22.8% -> 7.2% (`epochs_run=447`,
  `early_stopped=True`, inside the runner's own re-measured 447-607-epoch range). Both now
  clear the plan's `<0.10` reconstruction bar; neither did before the repair.
- **Boolean (b) reframed, not tuned.** At the coordinator's direction, the label and closing
  prose in both notebooks were changed from "spiral unrolled" to "latent PC1 linearly ordered
  by t" -- the Spearman check tests linear ordering only, which a correctly unrolled but
  curved/rotated strip would fail without being wrong about whether it unrolled. The measured
  value did **not** flip under the repair (plain AE 0.1011 -> 0.1819; TopoAE 0.1373 -> 0.0460,
  both still far below the 0.90 bar) and nothing was tuned toward moving it. (c), the D-03
  intrinsic-vs-ambient H1 signature, is named explicitly in both notebooks' closing cells as
  the better test of strip topology, and it held in both notebooks **both before and after**
  the protocol repair -- unlike (a), it was never an artifact of the training defect.
- **A new observation from the plots, recorded per the coordinator's direct examination.** In
  both notebooks' §5 latent scatters, the highest-`t` end of the roll collapses into a dense
  cluster rather than continuing the spiral: a small tight yellow blob near the origin in the
  plain-AE latent, a larger filled yellow region in TopoAE's. This is a better explanation of
  the low PC1-Spearman than "rotated or curved strip" (a rotated strip would still be linearly
  orderable; a collapsed endpoint would not), it is consistent across both substrates (a
  property of the setup/task rather than of either model's loss), and it is exactly the kind
  of structure the PH read-out should be sensitive to -- `02.6-15` should check whether the
  H0/H1 cells reflect it before treating the PH numbers and the pictures as independent
  evidence. Recorded here as a measured observation from the plots; **why** the endpoint
  collapses is not established.
