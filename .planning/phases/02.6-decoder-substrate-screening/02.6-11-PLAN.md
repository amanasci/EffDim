**Five top-level functions, same names and roles as the sealed runner:**

- `build_fixture(n, fixture_seed)` — the Swiss roll fixture built once per invocation and shared
  across every seed and candidate, exactly as the sealed runner does. Returns the fixture's ambient
  array, its spiral parameter, its global scale, the float32 training tensor, and the intrinsic-plane
  reference computed by `analytic_param.swiss_roll_intrinsic_plane` from that parameter and the
  ambient array's own ruling column. Note the ambient array's ruling column is already scaled by the
  same single global scalar, so it is passed straight through and never re-divided.

- `train_candidate(candidate, x_train, seed)` — dispatches on the candidate string. `plainae` and
  `topoae` build the identical `cae.PlainAutoEncoder(3, LATENT_DIM, hidden=HIDDEN,
  activation="silu")` after seeding torch, and train under `cae.train_plain_ae` and
  `topoae.train_topoae` respectively — the TopoAE arm is a plain autoencoder trained under a
  different loss, never a different architecture, exactly as the sealed runner states. `cae` builds
  `cae.ChartAutoEncoder` with the constants above and trains under `cae.train_cae`. Raise a
  `ValueError` naming the received value for any other string. Return the evaluated model and its
  fit artifact.

- `screen_seed(candidate, fx, seed, prescale)` — one (candidate, seed) measurement. Split with
  `np.random.default_rng(seed).permutation`, holding out `HOLDOUT_FRACTION`. Train from scratch.
  Then, on the **held-out rows only** and with the **same row indices** used for both references:
  extract the latent with `model.encode` and the decoder image with `model.forward(...)` under its
  `y` key for the two plain-autoencoder arms, and with `model.reconstruct(...)` for the CAE — that
  distinction is load-bearing and is a correction to `02.6-RESEARCH.md` Pattern 3 and
  `02.6-PATTERNS.md`, both of which claim `ChartAutoEncoder.forward` returns a `y` key; verified
  against source during planning, it does not. Take the CAE's latent from `model.encode`, which
  returns its global embedding rather than chart-local coordinates, per the ratified disposition of
  option group R. Call `persistence_probe.readout_matrix` once with the two spaces, the two
  references sliced to the identical held-out indices, and `prescale`. Return a flat dict carrying
  every key that function returns, plus the seed, the candidate, the latent dimension, the training
  wall clock, `epochs_run`, `early_stopped`, the held-out mean relative reconstruction error under
  the established per-point-ratio convention, and for the CAE arm the surviving chart count from
  `cae.chart_survival`.

- `markdown_table(rows)` — emits two GitHub-flavoured markdown tables. The first has one row per
  (candidate, seed, space, reference) — 48 data rows at the full sweep — with columns for the
  candidate, seed, space, reference, latent dimension, applied scale, and then for each degree and
  distance the normalised value, the raw value, and for bottleneck cells the saturation flag, plus

  four seeds. Never emit a column that weights, sums, averages or ranks cells together, and never
  emit a summary row. Key every column off `persistence_probe.READOUT_CELLS` rather than re-spelling
  the labels.
