<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Rank the in-repo decoder substrates on **manifold preservation, measured by persistent
homology agreement**, decided independently of how curvature is approximated — and separately
retire the confound that halted the first attempt.

This phase **measures and ranks. It promotes nothing and gates nothing.** No substrate decision
is sealed here. `02.5-10` receives a ranking, a derivative-usability table, and the separating
experiment's result, and makes the promotion decision itself under its own seal-before-measure
discipline.

**This replaces the halted axis.** The first attempt ranked substrates by agreement between
decoder-pullback curvature and analytic `H`. `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4 established that score is a
composite of three separable properties — did the architecture learn the right surface, are that
trained net's second derivatives trustworthy, is pullback the right approximator — so its
measurements cannot be attributed to substrate choice. Nothing measured under the old axis is
carried forward as evidence about any substrate.

**ROADMAP.md conflict the planner must not plan around.** Phase 02.6's roadmap **Goal** still
reads "screened against the Swiss roll admission gate (known analytic `H`)" and **SC-3** still
names the four curvature read-outs against analytic `H`. Both describe the halted axis. The Goal
additionally says "promote at most ONE", which the decisions below reverse. Only the roadmap's
**Status** paragraph carries the halt note. **The roadmap entry is stale; this document is
authoritative for scope.** The Goal and success criteria are to be rewritten from this CONTEXT
before or during planning, and the plans' coverage traced against the decisions here rather than
against SC-1..SC-5.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The ranking criterion

- **D-01:** The ranking quantity is **persistent homology agreement** — the distance between a
  model's persistence diagram and a reference diagram. Chosen over isometry distortion against
  exact geodesics and over k-NN neighbourhood overlap.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4 already records one criterion changed
  after seeing an unfavourable result. A second change to the ranking axis for the same question
  would be the third criterion tried, and the record cannot absorb it: no later reader could
  distinguish "we learned something" from "we kept going until a candidate won". Whatever this
  measures is what the phase reports.

- **D-02:** `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §4's standing objections to topology-based scoring
  were surfaced during discussion and D-01 was chosen with them visible. §4 says H0 preservation
  is not curvature fidelity, and that the roll's ambient `beta_1 = 1` (from near-touching spiral
  arms) means scoring a `d=2` latent on ambient H1 penalises correct unrolling. **D-03 is the
  specification that addresses the second objection rather than ignoring it.** The first
  objection stands unretired — see `<assumptions>` A-01.

- **D-03:** Each model's diagram is scored against **two references, reported separately, never
  collapsed**: (a) the diagram of the true 2-manifold in its exact intrinsic coordinates
  `(arc-length s(t), y)`, and (b) the diagram of the ambient 3-D Swiss roll point cloud. A
  correct unroll matches (a) and diverges from (b) — reporting both makes that distinction
  visible instead of scoring it as failure.

- **D-04:** Degrees **H0 and H1, reported separately.** H2 is excluded: ROADMAP.md records no
  power analysis at all for `beta_2`, so an H2 read-out could neither support nor refute a
  candidate.

- **D-05:** Distances: **both bottleneck and Wasserstein**, each **normalised by the reference
  diagram's own max persistence** so the absolute Swiss roll bar is scale-free and comparable
  across candidates. Both are reported because they disagree exactly when one feature dominates,
  and which of those is signal is not knowable in advance.

- **D-06:** Scored on **both the encoder latent and the decoder image, reported separately.**
  The topological losses train the latent; `02.5-10` seals thresholds on a decoder. Collapsing
  the two hides which one failed.

- **D-07:** Bars: **absolute on the Swiss roll, comparative on PU.** An absolute bar is possible
  on the roll precisely because its answer is exact; `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §2
  establishes that an absolute bar is not computable on PU, so the PU side stays relative to a
  matched plain-AE baseline and a null.

- **D-08:** The already-built distortion instruments (`cae.embedding_distortion`,
  `cae.fit_global_scale`, `geometry_probes.distortion_stats`) are **not computed in this phase.**
  Rationale accepted during discussion: a second geometric number invites post-hoc
  axis-switching, which is the exact failure `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4 recorded.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — the code exists and is pre-registered from Phases 02/02.2, so
  the decision is cheap to reverse in code, but adding the column later means re-running the full
  screen, and doing so *after* seeing the PH ranking would reproduce the §4 failure mode.

**Read-out matrix consequence (stated, not a separate decision):** D-03 × D-04 × D-05 × D-06 =
2 references × 2 degrees × 2 distances × 2 spaces = **16 numbers per seed per candidate**, 64
across four seeds, 192 across the three candidates — Swiss roll side only. The runner must emit
this table and the findings document must read it.

### Ratification and promotion discipline

- **D-09:** The criterion, its bars, and the read-out matrix are **written down and committed
  before any measurement on the new axis**, but ratified as **explicitly non-gating**: the
  criterion ranks, it does not promote. No promotion is sealed under it. The promotion decision
  moves wholly to `02.5-10`, with every number already in hand disclosed there per
  `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §6.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — a pre-registration declared exploratory cannot later be claimed
  to have constrained a published conclusion. This is a deliberate choice to claim less, made
  because `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4 records the previous rule as having constrained nothing.

- **D-10:** The phase **promotes no substrate.** This reverses ROADMAP.md's "promote at most
  ONE". `02.5-10` inherits a ranking, a derivative-usability table, and the separating
  experiment's result — not a substrate decision.

- **D-11:** Four seeds minimum, **spread reported**, and the `SPREAD > mean - floor`
  disqualifying rule from `02.6-RESEARCH.md` (flagged there as unratified proposal A4) is
  **dropped**. With a non-gating criterion there is nothing for a disqualifier to disqualify.

### The separating experiment

- **D-12:** Fit a **single best-case net** — generously sized, not budget-matched — by regression
  on the analytic Swiss roll parameterization `(s, y) -> R^3`, then trace curvature through it
  with the existing `decoder_curvature` tracer. The surface is correct by construction, so the
  only remaining variable is whether a *net's* second derivatives survive. This is
  `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §8.3's experiment.

- **D-13:** PASS is defined as **measured degradation from the analytic decoder's own
  exactness**, not a threshold set in the abstract. `decoder_curvature`'s tests already pin the
  tracer as exact on an analytic decoder (`test_plain_decoder_curvature_sphere_known_answer`,
  `H_norm` within `1e-12` of `2/R`; `test_plain_decoder_curvature_flat_linear_decoder_is_exactly_zero`,
  exactly `0.0`). The bar is what replacing the analytic map with a net costs.
  `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §5 identifies this as the evidence the halted phase never had — the
  `8.882e-16` fixture pin it relied on compared two analytic formulas to each other and touched
  no net.

- **D-14:** The separator runs **first, and is non-blocking.** Its result is recorded; the screen
  proceeds regardless. The branch decision is made at findings time with the full table in hand.

- **D-15:** **Separator FAILS →** the PH ranking continues and is reported, but the phase records
  explicitly that **no curvature claim attaches to any substrate**, and `02.5-10` inherits "Arm B
  through a trained decoder is not viable" and must redesign stage 2 around it. **Separator
  PASSES →** nets can carry usable second derivatives when the surface is right, which means the
  halted phase's low scores were about architectures failing to learn the sheet.

### The derivative-usability bridge

- **D-16:** The bridge instrument is **autodiff versus finite-difference agreement** on the
  trained decoder's second derivatives — `torch.func` against a finite-difference stencil.
  Chosen over pullback-metric conditioning and over post-hoc four-read-out curvature. It needs no
  ground truth, so it transfers to PU unchanged.

- **D-17:** The bridge runs on **both the Swiss roll and PU, reported separately.**
  `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §2's reasoning applies: the roll is the only place a method
  can be checked against a correct answer, but the phase's question is about PU, and a bridge
  that only runs where truth is known proves nothing about where the decoder is used.

- **D-18:** 02.6 **measures and applies no bar.** The bridge emits agreement numbers with no
  threshold; `02.5-10` ratifies the threshold under its own discipline. This is the same shape as
  `02.6-05`'s runner, which measures and prints and applies no bar.

- **D-19:** A substrate that tops the PH ranking but fails the bridge is **not promoted** — 02.6
  hands `02.5-10` both results and `02.5-10` decides what it can use. Walking the ranking to the
  runner-up was rejected: that would turn the bridge into a second ranking axis applied after
  seeing results, structurally the same move §4 recorded.

### Candidate set and scope

- **D-20:** Screen the **three in-repo candidates only**: plain AE, TopoAE (sealed 02.4 fits),
  and CAE as the measured negative control. RTD-AE, Witness AE, and GRAE are recorded as a
  **scoped follow-on phase**, with the note that RTD-AE became *more* aligned the moment PH was
  chosen as the axis — it constrains the map induced on homology groups toward an isomorphism,
  which is close to the criterion itself. `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §5: adding a
  substrate not already in-repo is phase-sized work, not a substitution. TopoAE++ is already
  resolved **NO-GO** in `02.6-RESEARCH.md`.

- **D-21:** The phase's cross-cutting constraint **"Screening only — no PU fits" is amended** to
  permit new PU fits, for the D-17 bridge arm only. Without this the plain AE has no PU arm and
  the bridge is Swiss-roll-only, contradicting D-17.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — this widens the scope fence the phase was inserted with, and PU
  fits are the expensive operation in this project. Undoing it means dropping the PU side of the
  bridge and re-deciding D-17. The sealed 02.2 CAE and 02.4 TopoAE PU fits remain **read-only**;
  the amendment permits *new* fits, it does not reopen sealed ones.

- **D-22:** The Stage A (Swiss roll admission) / Stage B (PU decision) split from
  `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §3 is **kept, but admission is descriptive**: failing Stage
  A no longer bars a substrate from Stage B. This follows from D-09 — a hard gate inside a phase
  whose criterion is ratified non-gating would be a contradiction.

### Claude's Discretion

- Module layout for the new PH-agreement code. The codebase's established pattern at every prior
  insertion is a small independent sibling module rather than a shared refactor across a sealed
  phase boundary (`curvature_probe.py` beside `curvature.py`; `topoae.py` mirroring rather than
  calling `cae.py`). `02.6-RESEARCH.md` Open Question 1 reaches the same recommendation.
- Notebook decomposition — how many CLAUDE.md Swiss roll notebooks, and which read-outs land in
  a notebook versus in a `notebooks/diagnostics/` runner.
- Whether the separator (D-12) is its own notebook or a section of an existing one. It is
  CLAUDE.md-governed either way if it trains a model.
- Finite-difference stencil choice and step size for D-16.

<assumptions>
## Assumptions

- **A-01 — the bridge tests numerical consistency, not correctness.** Autodiff-versus-finite-difference
  agreement (D-16) establishes that the derivative *computation* is stable. A net that learned a
  smooth but *wrong* surface passes it cleanly. That is `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4's (1)-versus-(2)
  split reappearing inside the bridge, and the Swiss roll has ground truth that this instrument
  declines to use. **As composed, nothing in this phase demonstrates that a winner's derivatives
  are right — only that they are computable.** This was raised during discussion and the phase
  proceeds with it recorded rather than closed. The separating experiment (D-12..D-15) is the
  nearest thing to a cover, and it addresses the general question "can any net do this", not the
  per-substrate one.

- **A-02 — `02.5-NOTE-substrate-selection.md` §4's first objection stands.** "H0 preservation is
  not curvature fidelity. They are different properties measured by different instruments, and
  nothing yet measured connects them." D-03/D-04 address §4's *ambient-H1* objection; they do not
  address this one. A PH-ranked winner is not thereby a curvature-capable winner, and the phase
  must not be read as claiming otherwise.

- **A-03 — PH seed variance is unmeasured in this project.** D-11 keeps four seeds because that
  is the established protocol, but bottleneck and Wasserstein distances on small diagrams may be
  noisier than the curvature statistics four seeds were calibrated against. If the observed
  spread swamps the between-candidate differences, that is a finding about the criterion, and it
  should be reported as one rather than absorbed.

- **A-04 — a reusable plain-AE PU fit may not exist.** The quick tasks measured plain-AE H0
  retention on PU (`~0.53-0.63`), so an artifact plausibly exists, but its location and
  reusability were not verified during discussion. Under D-21 a new fit is permitted if none is
  found; the planner should check before budgeting one.

</assumptions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The Swiss roll's exact intrinsic coordinates are available and should be used as such:
  `make_swiss_roll` returns the arc-length parameter `t`, and arc length is closed-form, so the
  true `(s, y)` plane is computable exactly. Reference (a) in D-03 is therefore an exact
  reference, not a graph-geodesic approximation with a `k` to tune.
- D-13's framing — "what does replacing the analytic map with a net cost?" — is the intended
  reading of the separating experiment. It is a degradation measurement against a known-exact
  baseline, not a pass/fail against an invented threshold.
- The phase is expected to claim *less* than the halted attempt did, deliberately. D-09, D-10,
  D-18 and D-19 all move authority to `02.5-10`.

</specifics>
