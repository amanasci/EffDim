## Open decisions adopted as this plan's stated assumptions

- **A-05 (shared with plan 02.6-03). Fixture: `curvature_probe.make_swiss_roll_fixture(n=3000,
  seed=20260807)`.** **Alternative not chosen:** `02.4_swiss_roll_topoae_check.ipynb`'s own
  `make_swiss_roll(n_samples=3000, random_state=20260806)` draw. Rejected because the raw-point
  comparator's `0.6712` and the CAE negative control's four-seed table were both measured on
  `20260807`; a different draw makes this candidate incomparable to the only negative control the
  phase has. Reversible.
- **A-06 (shared with plan 02.6-03). Architecture and cfg reused verbatim from
  `02.4_swiss_roll_topoae_check.ipynb`:** `cae.PlainAutoEncoder(3, 2, hidden=(64, 64, 64),
  activation="silu")`; `CFG_COMMON = dict(lr=3e-4, weight_decay=1e-4, batch=64, max_epochs=150)`;
  TopoAE arm `dict(seed=SEED, lambda_topo=0.1, warmup_frac=0.25, ramp_frac=0.25, **CFG_COMMON)`;
  baseline arm `dict(seed=SEED, **CFG_COMMON)`. `early_stop_patience` is deliberately left unset in
  both arms so `train_topoae` and `_train_decoder_protocol` each default it to `max_epochs + 1` and
  both arms run the identical full 150-epoch budget. **This is load-bearing, not cosmetic:** Phase
  02.4-05 was reopened because `train_topoae`'s plateau early-stop fired against the non-stationary
  warm-up/ramp objective and left the two arms on different training budgets (15 versus 40 epochs) —
  "a confound T1/T2/T3's ratios cannot tolerate". Leaving patience unset makes that confound
  unreachable here. Reversible.
- **A-09. `LAMBDA_TOPO = 0.1` inherited as-is, with its recorded caveat carried into the markdown.**
  It is the value `02.4-PREREGISTRATION.md` froze, and `02.4-PREREGISTRATION-AMENDMENT-02.md`
  withdrew the "paper's own minimum searched lambda" justification for it after a fifth fidelity gap
  (EffDim sums the reconstruction term over features where the reference means it) put it roughly
  `D` times smaller in the paper's convention than stated. **Alternative not chosen:** re-sweep
  lambda for this phase. Rejected: a sanity check introduces no new tunable, and re-tuning a
  candidate's hyperparameter for its own admission test is exactly the sort of thing that makes an
  admission result unfalsifiable. The caveat is stated in the notebook's markdown rather than
  silently inherited. Reversible.
- **A-07 (shared). Notebook sanity bars are comparative against the arms computed in the same cell;
  the notebook introduces no numeric constant of its own.** The phase's screening bars live in
  `02.6-SCREENING-RULE.md` and are applied in `02.6-FINDINGS.md`, never inside a sanity notebook —
  CLAUDE.md bars threshold tables there. Reversible.
