## Findings from the human-verify gate, beyond the plan's own stated criteria

The plan's Task 2 asked the human to read the plots and the printed booleans and judge
pass/fail against four named criteria. The user's direct examination of the plots
surfaced three findings none of those criteria would have caught on their own -- exactly
the failure mode a blocking gate exists to prevent, and the standing precedent this phase
names (`02.6-03`'s gate, left open and permanently unverified) is what a silent pass would
have repeated.

**1. The reconstruction is visibly torn.** Cell 8's x-z scatter shows the CAE's
reconstruction fragmenting the spiral into disconnected, near-linearly-straightened
segments with visible gaps between them -- the multi-chart signature, each chart
reconstructing its own patch without joining its neighbours smoothly. Colour ordering is
preserved within and across fragments (which is why boolean (a)'s stated criterion --
relative error under the bound -- is satisfied and the plot does not trip the plan's
literal pass condition), but the surface itself is not continuous. **The two facts do not
resolve into one judgement: 5.2% held-out pointwise error and 1.87x better MSE than the
matched baseline, alongside a visibly discontinuous manifold.** Good pointwise
reconstruction and manifold continuity come apart here. `02.5-10` should not read one as
evidence of the other -- a low pointwise error does not establish that the decoder learned
a connected surface, and this notebook is the concrete instance showing why.

**2. The CAE's latent shows NO high-t collapse.** Plan `02.6-12` recorded that both the
plain-AE and TopoAE latents collapse the roll's highest-t end into a dense blob rather
than continuing the spiral (a small tight cluster in the plain-AE latent, a larger filled
region in TopoAE's). Cell 10 here shows the opposite: the CAE's 8-D-embedding-projected
latent is a clean, open, ordered spiral with the yellow (high-t) end spread out, not
collapsed. This is the third data point `02.6-12`'s carry-forward note asked `02.6-15` to
look for, and it answers the open question directly: **the high-t collapse is not
universal across this milestone's substrates.** It is a property of the single-chart 2-D
bottleneck architecture (shared by plain-AE and TopoAE, which differ only in loss), not
of the roll fixture or the reconstruction task in general -- the CAE's multi-chart
architecture, whatever else it does or does not do well, does not reproduce it.

**3. A phase-level confound in the ranking axis, found by re-reading the screening
runner's source during the gate.** `notebooks/diagnostics/decoder_substrate_ph_screen_run.py:294`
reads `latent_dim = CAE_EMBED_DIM if candidate == "cae" else LATENT_DIM` -- the CAE's
latent cloud is scored at **8 dimensions** while the plain-AE's and TopoAE's latent
clouds are scored at **2 dimensions**. Persistence diagrams built from clouds at
different ambient dimensions are not comparable on that basis alone: H0 lifetimes are
minimum-spanning-tree edge lengths, which grow systematically with ambient dimension at
fixed sample count, and Q1's isotropic per-cloud pre-scaling (`topoae.latent_unit_scale`)
normalises overall scale but does nothing about dimension.

**Consequence, disclosed here rather than fixed:** the ~6x gap the runner's own 192-number
matrix (`02.6-11-SUMMARY.md`, round 3) shows the CAE carrying on the `latent|*` cells is
**not, on its own, a trustworthy substrate difference** -- part or all of it may be a
dimensionality artifact of the read-out instrument, not a property of what the CAE
learned. On the **dimensionally-matched `decoder_image|intrinsic|*` cells**, where all
three candidates live in the same `R^3` ambient space regardless of latent dimension, the
CAE is measured **marginally best**, not worst: `decoder_image|intrinsic|H0|wasserstein`
mean `351.80` (CAE) vs. `365.15` (plainae) vs. `366.76` (topoae); `H1` mean `78.39` (CAE)
vs. `84.41` (plainae) vs. `86.44` (topoae) -- and per `02.6-11-SUMMARY.md`'s own spread
table, all three candidates' decoder-image-cell readings sit inside each other's seed
spread. The negative control does not look like an outlier at all on the one part of the
matrix where the comparison is dimensionally sound.

This is **not** a post-hoc criterion change of the kind `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4 already
records once for this phase. R1 (the CAE's encoder latent is the global `embed_dim`
embedding) was ratified **blind**, at plan `02.6-07`'s wave-1 checkpoint, before any
persistent-homology number existed in this repository under this phase's replan --
`02.6-SCREENING-RULE-02.md`'s own ratification stamp attests to this in ancestry. The
dimensional mismatch this notebook's gate surfaced is a **defect in the ratified design
itself**, one that only measurement -- not the original design discussion -- could have
exposed: nobody scoring option group R against isolated criteria (comparability to the
other candidates' latents, refusal of chart-local coordinates by `embedding_distortion`,
precedent from `02.2_swiss_roll_cae_check.ipynb`) had reason to check whether the *chosen*
option left the resulting persistence cells dimensionally comparable to the *other*
candidates' cells -- that comparability was implicitly assumed, not verified, when R1 was
selected.

It is also not clear there is a clean fix available. The declined alternative, R2
(chart-local `chart_dim`-dimensional coordinates at the argmax chart), would have been
dimensionally matched to the other two candidates' 2-D latents, but `02.6-SCREENING-RULE-02.md`
itself records why it was declined: chart-local coordinates are not a single global
coordinate system, so a diagram built from them is not the same *kind* of object as the
other candidates' diagrams either. **That tension -- dimensional comparability and
single-coordinate-system-hood pulling in opposite directions for a multi-chart model --
is itself the finding**, not a defect with an obvious repair.

**This SUMMARY records the finding. `02.6-SCREENING-RULE-02.md` is NOT edited** -- it
stays frozen, per this plan's own hard constraints and per the document's own "this
document's commit precedes every commit that records a persistent-homology number"
provenance guarantee. `02.6-15` inherits this finding intact, to read alongside (not
instead of) the runner's own `latent|*`-cell numbers when it assembles the phase's
reporting.
