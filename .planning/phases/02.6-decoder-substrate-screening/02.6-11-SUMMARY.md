## Protocol Repairs (post-completion)

This plan closed once, was reopened twice by the coordinator/user, and re-run each time
because a defect was found in the **measurement apparatus** -- never in the ratified
criterion. `02.6-SCREENING-RULE-02.md` pins the criterion statement, the option-group
dispositions, the sixteen read-out cells, the seed protocol, and the provenance conventions;
it does not pin learning rate, epoch budget, or early-stopping discipline, so neither repair
touches it, and neither repair is a "re-run because the numbers looked wrong" -- both are
responses to a specific, measured, named confound.

### Round 1: unequal convergence budgets across candidates (repaired in `d8aa0bd`)

**Defect.** The runner as first closed trained `plainae`/`topoae` under `CFG_COMMON`
(`lr=3e-4`, `max_epochs=150`, no early stop) and `cae` under a separate `CFG_CAE` (`lr=1e-3`,
`early_stop_patience=25`). Every `plainae`/`topoae` seed in the round-1 run ended
`epochs_run=150, early_stopped=False` -- still improving when the budget cut them off --
while `cae` early-stopped on 3 of 4 seeds at 31-47 epochs.

**Measured justification (controlled probe, same fixture and seed, same
`cae.PlainAutoEncoder`, run once at `CFG_COMMON` and once at the `02.2` reference config):**

```
SWEEP-cfg(as-run)  seed=0 epochs=150 early_stopped=False rel_err=0.1456
REF-cfg(02.2-like) seed=0 epochs=190 early_stopped=True  rel_err=0.0375
SWEEP-cfg(as-run)  seed=1 epochs=150 early_stopped=False rel_err=0.1417
REF-cfg(02.2-like) seed=1 epochs=192 early_stopped=True  rel_err=0.0465
```

A ~3.5x reconstruction-error reduction from finishing convergence, landing on the `02.2`
reference notebook's own ~4.8% figure. The round-1 192-number matrix therefore ranked one
converged model (`cae`) against two unconverged ones (`plainae`, `topoae`) -- the same class
of confound that halted this phase's first attempt (`02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4), now caught before
`02.6-15` could read it.

**Fix.** `CFG_COMMON` rewritten to match `CFG_CAE`'s convergence discipline verbatim
(`lr=1e-3`, `early_stop_patience=25`, `early_stop_min_delta=1e-4`) at a shared
`max_epochs=300` ceiling.

### Round 2: topoae alone still hit the ceiling at max_epochs=300 (repaired in `313fbbe`)

**Defect.** Under the round-1 fix, `plainae` and `cae` converged on every seed
(`early_stopped=True` 4/4 each, unchanged from what round 1 would have shown if it had run
long enough), but `topoae` hit the 300-epoch ceiling on 3 of its 4 seeds:

```
topoae epochs_run: 286 (early_stopped=True), 300, 300, 300 (early_stopped=False x3)
```

`topoae` carries an extra topological loss term plus a warmup/ramp schedule and legitimately
needs more epochs than a plain AE to finish converging under the identical patience rule --
so this was not a second instance of round 1's defect, it was headroom, not a rule mismatch.
The residual truncation landed on exactly the cells the ranking reads
(`latent | intrinsic | wasserstein`): at round 2, `topoae` placed **second** to `plainae`
there (H0 110.24 vs 99.57; H1 32.19 vs 25.85) -- the runner-up disfavoured by the one arm
still hitting a ceiling.

**Fix.** `max_epochs` raised to `800` for **both** `CFG_COMMON` and `CFG_CAE` -- one shared
ceiling across all three arms, never a per-candidate one (a per-candidate ceiling would
reintroduce round 1's defect with the sign flipped). `lr`, `weight_decay`, `batch`,
`early_stop_patience`, `early_stop_min_delta` all held unchanged from round 1's fix.

### Round 3 (current): every arm converges, and the check that mattered was run

At `max_epochs=800`, all twelve (candidate, seed) fits end by early stopping. `plainae`
(156/199/266/214 epochs) and `cae` (47/31/250/37 epochs) are **unchanged from round 2** --
both had already converged under the 300-epoch ceiling, which is an internal consistency
check that raising the ceiling altered nothing it should not have. `topoae` needed
447/538/607/450 epochs -- well past the 300-epoch ceiling that had truncated it.

**The ordering check that round 2 flagged as needing to be run was run, and the result is
recorded rather than assumed.** After giving `topoae` the epochs it needed, its
`latent | intrinsic | H0 | wasserstein` moved slightly **worse** (110.24 -> 113.10) and its
H1 moved slightly **better** (32.19 -> 31.41) relative to round 2's truncated reading. The
direction did not flip in a way that would suggest the round-2 numbers were an artifact of
truncation masquerading as a real difference -- but see "What the numbers say" below for what
this does and does not establish about the `plainae`-vs-`topoae` ordering itself.
