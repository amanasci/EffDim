## Separator's branch logic

**Clarification requested mid-area.** The user asked what the separator is before answering the
last question. Explained: it is `02.6-FINDINGS.md` §8.3's experiment, named for holding cause (1)
— did the architecture learn the right surface — fixed at *correct* by construction, by
regressing a net directly on the analytic parameterization instead of training an autoencoder on
roll points, then pushing the same tracer through it. Junk curvature then means the problem is
net second derivatives, not substrate choice; good curvature means the halted phase's axis was
measuring what it claimed.
