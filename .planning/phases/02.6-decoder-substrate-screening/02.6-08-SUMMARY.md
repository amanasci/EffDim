# Phase 02.6 Plan 08: The Separating Experiment Summary

**Fit a generously sized net to the Swiss roll's own analytic parameterization, trace curvature through it via the unchanged `decoder_curvature` tracer, and measure what replacing the closed form with a net costs relative to a `1.11e-15` exactness floor — result: nets carry usable second derivatives when the surface is correct by construction, with a calibration-slope shortfall (0.66-0.69) named rather than smoothed over.**

## Accomplishments

- **`notebooks/pu_manifold/analytic_param.py`** (+ tests): exact closed-form Swiss roll arc-length, intrinsic-plane, and ambient-reconstruction helpers, verified against `scipy.integrate.quad` (max abs deviation `9.237056e-14`, matching the plan's own pre-stated measurement) and against `sklearn.datasets.make_swiss_roll`'s own output (bit-exact, `0.0`). A parameter-free `AnalyticSwissRollDecoder` establishes D-13's exactness floor: pushed through the unchanged `decoder_curvature.plain_decoder_curvature` tracer, it reproduces `swiss_roll_analytic_H_vector` row for row at `1.110223e-15` max abs deviation — four orders of magnitude inside the `<1e-8` bound. D-12's `AnalyticParamDecoder`, generously sized and deliberately not budget-matched, is trained via `cae.train_mlp_decoder` reused byte-unchanged (`grep -c "def train_"` returns `0` in the new module).
- **`notebooks/02.6_swiss_roll_separator_check.ipynb`**: 15 cells, executed clean, 34.3s wall-clock. Both D-15 branch consequences are written in cell 0, before any measurement is shown. Reports the floor, the analytic-param net at both fixture points and a dense 1000-point held-out grid uniform in the spiral parameter (T1, ratified in `02.6-SCREENING-RULE-02.md`), and a matched `cae.PlainAutoEncoder` baseline — five read-outs per row, never collapsed into one score.
- **The separator's result, D-15's second branch selected (PASSES) — narrowly bounded.** The analytic-param net's median cosine similarity is `0.999870` (fixture points) / `0.999871` (held-out grid) against the floor's `1.000000000`, and Spearman rho `0.839304` / `0.855063` against the floor's `1.000000000` — a real, measured degradation, but the net clearly carries usable direction and rank information through the tracer when the surface it was fit to is correct by construction. **This licenses only that narrow claim: nets CAN carry usable second derivatives when the surface is right.** It says nothing about whether the three screened substrates (plain AE, TopoAE, CAE) actually learn that surface — that is the separate question `02.6-09`'s persistent-homology ranking answers, and this result must not read as a general endorsement of decoder-pullback curvature on trained substrates.
- **Calibration-slope shortfall, named explicitly and not folded into the pass.** The four boolean read-out checks (a)-(d) do not test calibration slope, and the net's measured slope is `0.659172` (fixture points) / `0.686983` (held-out grid) — meaningfully short of the ideal `1.0`, alongside a per-point magnitude-ratio coefficient of variation of `0.175600` / `0.166397`. Direction is near-perfect (median cosine `~0.9999`) and median magnitude is well-calibrated (ratio `~1.01`), but the net's curvature estimates are more scattered and systematically off-slope than the floor's exact `1.0`. **This is reported as a passing-criteria-(a)-(d)-as-defined result with a named, measured shortfall on calibration slope — not an unqualified pass on all five read-outs.**
- **Matched plain-AE baseline gap, recorded as measured evidence.** Spearman rho `0.280720` and magnitude-ratio CV `7.874163` against the analytic-param net's `0.839304` / `0.175600` — the baseline is markedly worse on every column of the four-row table, a real gap consistent with (but not proof of) the D-15 interpretation that architecture matters independently of tracer conditioning.
- **D-15's branch-ordering requirement was verified structurally, not merely asserted.** Both branch consequences are written in the notebook's cell 0 — the first cell, ahead of every printed number — confirmed by reading the executed notebook, not inferred from a stated intention.
