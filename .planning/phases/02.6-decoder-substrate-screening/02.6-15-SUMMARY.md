not reproduce from source data**
- **Found during:** Task 1, drafting §5 (the critical reading rule)
- **Issue:** The plan's `<what_the_phase_actually_found>` context offered a specific
  worked example ("plain AE latent H1 reads 25.85 intrinsic vs 10.89 ambient normalised
  (2.37x apart), but raw is 8.65 vs 7.84 — only 1.10x apart") to illustrate why `_norm`
  columns must not be compared across intrinsic/ambient references. Computing the same
  cell directly from `02.6-11-SUMMARY.md`'s Round-3 measurement table (the transcribed
  source) reproduces the `25.85`/`10.89` normalised figures exactly, but the corresponding
  raw figures compute to `3.89` (intrinsic) and `9.42` (ambient) — not `8.65`/`7.84`, and
  in the *opposite* direction (ambient raw larger, not smaller).
- **Fix:** Rather than assert the plan's literal figures (which could not be traced to
  the document's own transcribed table and would have been an unverifiable claim in a
  document whose entire purpose is measured, checkable numbers) or silently drop the
  worked example, substituted a source-verified illustration using the same cell and the
  same candidate, computed directly from §6's own transcribed rows and cross-checked
  against the round-3 spread table's published mean. The substituted example makes an
  equally strong (arguably stronger) version of the same point: normalising here does not
  merely rescale the intrinsic-vs-ambient gap, it inverts which reference reads larger.
- **Files modified:** `.planning/phases/02.6-decoder-substrate-screening/02.6-FINDINGS-02.md`
  only (§5).
- **Verification:** Recomputed the substituted figures twice from the transcribed §6a
