  path from `topoae_evaluate_run.py` and `cae_evaluate_run.py` unchanged: the cached subsample, the
  cached canonical holdout split, and the same preprocessing those runners apply before the ambient

  differentiates. For the two plain-autoencoder-family candidates that is `model.decode`. For the
  CAE it is the two-hop chart decode: chart `chart_idx`'s own decoder followed by the single shared
  embedding decoder, batched — the same composition `chart_curvature.chart_decoder_map` builds one
  point at a time, and deliberately not `cae._decode_through_chart`, which would differentiate
  through the chart-coordinate-producing encoder as well and measure the wrong map. **Pin the batched
  closure against the sealed single-point map**: evaluate both at a handful of chart coordinates and
  assert agreement below `1e-12` before any bridge number is computed, so a batched rewrite cannot
  drift from the sealed composition it mirrors.

  then call `derivative_agreement` at the calibrated step and return its dict plus the label, the
  latent dimension, the point count, the calibrated step and the wall clock. Never pass
  `DEFAULT_FD_STEP` without calibrating — assumption A-06 records that the optimum is
  decoder-scale-dependent and the PU decoders' weight and activation scales have never been measured
  against the toy fixture that value came from.

- `markdown_table(rows)` and `main()` — `main` runs the Swiss roll arm across `DEFAULT_SEEDS` for all
