## Accomplishments

- Built `notebooks/pu_manifold/derivative_bridge.py` with all eight named public symbols:
  `FD_STENCIL_CONVENTION`, `DEFAULT_FD_STEP`, `MAX_FD_ROWS`, `finite_difference_jacobian`,
  `finite_difference_hessian`, `reduce_to_H_vec`, `calibrate_fd_step`,

- `finite_difference_hessian` chunks its stacked stencil points at `MAX_FD_ROWS` (8192 rows,
  ~50 MB per chunk of float64 output at the PU regime's `out_dim=768`), issuing
  `ceil(batch * n_offsets / MAX_FD_ROWS)` calls to the decoder callable -- pinned by a
  counter-based test at two batch sizes.
- `reduce_to_H_vec` mirrors `chart_curvature.chart_mean_curvature`'s trace-first-then-project
  `d x d` solve verbatim (never re-derived) and is pinned to reproduce
  `decoder_curvature.plain_decoder_curvature`'s own `H_vec` to exact bit-identity (`0.0` max
  deviation, well inside the `< 1e-10` bound) on both the analytic `_SphereDecoder` fixture
  and a small trained `cae.PlainAutoEncoder`.
- `calibrate_fd_step` reproduces the measured float64 U-shaped error curve
  (`02.6-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 5) on the sphere fixture and returns a step in `[1e-5, 1e-3]`.
- `derivative_agreement` -- the bridge's reporting unit -- returns `full_hessian_agreement`
  and `reduced_mean_curvature_agreement` (itself split into `H_vec`/`H_norm`) under separate
  keys, plus `fd_step_used`, `metric_condition_number`, and `activation`. No key combines the
  two levels; no boolean judgement is returned anywhere (verified by negative grep and by a
  direct dict-key assertion in the test suite).
- Full test suite grew from 318 to **331 passed**, zero failures; no sealed module,
  `src/effdim/`, or `pyproject.toml` touched (`git status --porcelain` over all six guarded

## Decisions Made

- **Flat-linear-decoder known-answer test uses `h=1e-2`, not `DEFAULT_FD_STEP`.** Measured
  directly this session: at `h=1e-4` (the module's calibrated default, tuned for the sphere's
  genuine curvature signal) the `_LinearDecoder` fixture's finite-difference Hessian has max
  abs entry `~8.9e-8` -- roundoff-cancellation noise scaling as `O(eps/h**2)`
  (`eps=2.22e-16`), not a correctness defect. An exactly-flat map has zero truncation error to
  trade against a larger step, so `h=1e-2` was chosen for this one test specifically
  (measured `8.88e-12`, comfortably under the plan's stated `<1e-8` bound), with the
  reasoning documented in the test's own docstring rather than silently loosening the bound
  or silently changing `DEFAULT_FD_STEP` itself.
- **`derivative_agreement`'s two reduced comparisons share one autodiff Jacobian.** Both
  `reduce_to_H_vec(J_autodiff, Hess_autodiff)` and `reduce_to_H_vec(J_autodiff, Hess_fd)` use
  the identical `J` from the autodiff side, so any measured disagreement between the two
  reduced levels isolates the effect of swapping the Hessian source alone. D-16 asks
  specifically whether second derivatives are trustworthy; letting an independent
  finite-difference Jacobian into the comparison would answer a different, confounded
  question.
- **`MAX_FD_ROWS=8192`**, chosen to keep one chunk's float64 decoder output around 50 MB
  regardless of batch size or latent dimension, with the `1 + 2*d + 4*d*(d-1)/2`
  per-point-evaluation arithmetic recorded in the constant's own docstring (`9` at the Swiss
  roll's `d=2`, `3201` at the PU control's `d=40`).
