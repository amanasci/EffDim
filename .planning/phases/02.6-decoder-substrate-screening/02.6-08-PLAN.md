confound that halted this phase.

`02.6-FINDINGS.md` §4 established that the halted axis measured a composite of three separable
properties, one of which — *are a trained net's second derivatives trustworthy* — was never tested.
§8.3 names the experiment that separates it: fit a net to the **analytic** Swiss roll
parameterization, so the surface is correct by construction, then trace curvature through it. The
only remaining variable is whether a net's second derivatives survive.

§5 is the reason a floor has to be measured rather than borrowed: the `8.882e-16` fixture pin the
halted phase relied on compared two analytic formulas to each other and touched no net. The two
tests that *do* bear on the estimator pin it on an analytic **sphere** decoder, not on this
manifold. So this plan builds the roll's own closed-form decoder, measures the tracer's exactness on
it, and then measures what replacing that closed form with a net costs.

Purpose: `02.5-10` cannot design stage 2 without knowing whether curvature through a trained decoder
is viable at all, independently of which substrate is chosen.
Output: `notebooks/pu_manifold/analytic_param.py` + tests, and the CLAUDE.md-mandated Swiss roll
notebook `notebooks/02.6_swiss_roll_separator_check.ipynb`, executed with outputs.

02.6-09 consumes `swiss_roll_arc_length` / `swiss_roll_intrinsic_plane` from this module as code,
not as a result; the persistent-homology screen proceeds whatever the separator returns.
