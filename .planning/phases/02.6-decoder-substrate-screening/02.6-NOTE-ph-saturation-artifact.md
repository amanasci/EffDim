# Note — the PH prescale sqrt(d) artifact, checked against this phase

**Written:** 2026-08-14, during the Phase 3 defect-fix pass (`03-08-DEFECTS-01.md` /
`03-08-SUPPLEMENT-02.md`). Additive only — nothing in `02.6-SCREENING-RULE-02.md`,
`02.6-FINDINGS-02.md`, or any other sealed 02.6 artifact is edited or reinterpreted here.

## What the artifact is

`persistence_probe.cloud_distance_matrix(points, prescale=True)` rescales each cloud by
`topoae.latent_unit_scale(cloud)` — mean per-dimension variance fixed to 1, isotropic and
neighbour-preserving. That normalizer fixes cloud *variance*, not cloud *distance scale*: the
resulting pairwise-distance magnitude still grows as `sqrt(d)`. When a bottleneck or
Wasserstein distance is computed between two clouds living at different ambient dimensions,
the comparison inherits that `sqrt(d)` gap, and bottleneck distance in particular can pin at
its saturation ceiling (`0.5 * max_persistence(reference)`) purely as a consequence of the
dimension gap, not of any topological difference. `03-08-DEFECTS-01.md` names this defect 3
and diagnoses it directly against the Phase 3 PU sweep, where a 40-dim latent was compared
against a 768-dim ambient reference (`sqrt(768/40) ≈ 4.4x`) and every affected cell saturated
in all 12 recorded grid/control rows.

**The measured evidence (from `03-08-DEFECTS-01.md`), reproduced here for this note's own
record:**

```
 ambient dim  applied_scale  H1 max_pers  median dist
          10         1.0079       0.5510       4.3278
          40         2.0159       1.1020       8.6555
         128         3.6061       1.9714      15.4835
         768         8.8331       4.8288      37.9267
```

Observed persistence ratios `1, 2.00, 3.578, 8.763` match `sqrt(d/10)` exactly. Identical
intrinsic structure isometrically embedded at d=40 vs d=768 (i.e. the SAME structure, only
the ambient dimension changes) gives a bottleneck distance that equals the saturation ceiling
exactly — `15.2054 == 15.2054` — confirming the mechanism is dimension, not topology. The fix
verified in that document — and now landed in `persistence_probe.cloud_distance_matrix` as
the opt-in `prescale="median_distance"` mode (median pairwise distance fixed to 1, a
dimension-invariant statistic by construction) — gives `bottleneck=0.0000` on the same
identical-structure pair where the current default gives the saturation ceiling.

## This phase's own exposure, checked directly

`notebooks/diagnostics/decoder_substrate_ph_screen_run.py` is the runner behind this phase's
recorded 16-cell read-out matrix (`02.6-FINDINGS-02.md` §6). It calls
`persistence_probe.readout_matrix(spaces, references, prescale)` with `PRESCALE_CLOUDS = True`
at every seed and every candidate — the same `prescale=True` mode defect 3 concerns.

Per candidate, the four `(space, reference)` pairs and their dimension match/mismatch
(`latent_dim = CAE_EMBED_DIM=8` for `cae`, `LATENT_DIM=2` for `plainae`/`topoae`; ambient=3,
intrinsic=2, decoder_image=3 for every candidate — read directly off
`decoder_substrate_ph_screen_run.py:294` and `02.6-FINDINGS-02.md` §6a's own `latent_dim` /
`space_scale` columns):

| candidate | `latent\|intrinsic` | `latent\|ambient` | `decoder_image\|intrinsic` | `decoder_image\|ambient` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| plainae (latent_dim=2) | **matched** (2 vs 2) | mismatched (2 vs 3, `sqrt(3/2)≈1.22x`) | mismatched (3 vs 2, `≈1.22x`) | **matched** (3 vs 3) |
| topoae (latent_dim=2) | **matched** (2 vs 2) | mismatched (2 vs 3, `≈1.22x`) | mismatched (3 vs 2, `≈1.22x`) | **matched** (3 vs 3) |
| cae (latent_dim=8) | mismatched (8 vs 2, `sqrt(8/2)=2.0x`) | mismatched (8 vs 3, `sqrt(8/3)≈1.63x`) | mismatched (3 vs 2, `≈1.22x`) | **matched** (3 vs 3) |

So: 8 of 16 cells per (plainae, topoae) seed, and 12 of 16 cells per `cae` seed, are built from
a cross-ambient-dimension comparison under `prescale=True` — the trigger condition for defect
3's mechanism. Only `decoder_image|ambient|*` (all three candidates share ambient dimension 3
regardless of latent dimension) is dimensionally matched for every candidate.

**`02.6-FINDINGS-02.md` already independently found and named a closely related confound.**
§7b ("Confound 1 — the `latent | *` cells are not dimensionally comparable") states, from
measurement rather than from this note: *"Persistence diagrams built from point clouds at
different ambient dimensions are not comparable on that basis alone: H0 lifetimes are
minimum-spanning-tree edge lengths, which grow systematically with ambient dimension at fixed
sample count, and Q1's isotropic per-cloud pre-scaling (`topoae.latent_unit_scale`) normalises
overall scale, not dimension."* That is the same root mechanism defect 3 names, independently
discovered by this phase's own analysis before Phase 3's diagnosis existed. §7a's response was
to base the phase's one clean cross-CANDIDATE comparison on `decoder_image | intrinsic | *`,
reasoning that because all three candidates' decoder image lives in the same `R^3` regardless
of latent dimension, a comparison across candidates on that cell is not *differentially*
confounded — even though, per the table above, `decoder_image|intrinsic` is itself a
mismatched-dimension PH cell (3 vs 2) in the absolute sense defect 3 concerns.

## What this does, and does not, settle

**Evidence against defect 3 being the dominant cause of this phase's one observed
saturation.** The only cell that ever reports `saturated=True` in the recorded Round-3 data
(`02.6-FINDINGS-02.md` §6, confirmed by reading the table directly) is
`latent|ambient|H1|bottleneck`, for `plainae` (3 of 4 seeds) and `topoae` (4 of 4 seeds) — and
it is NEVER saturated for `cae` at that same cell (values `0.82`–`1.20` across all four seeds,
well above the saturation ceiling of `0.5`), despite `cae`'s `latent|ambient` pair carrying the
LARGER dimension mismatch (`8` vs `3`, `1.63x`) against `plainae`/`topoae`'s smaller one (`2`
vs `3`, `1.22x`). If defect 3's `sqrt(d)` mechanism were the dominant driver of this
saturation, the larger-mismatch candidate would be expected to saturate at least as readily as
the smaller-mismatch ones, not less. This pattern instead matches
`02.6-SCREENING-RULE-02.md`'s own already-documented explanation for this specific cell — the
ambient reference itself carries a genuine (if partly ambient-embedding-driven) topological
divergence from a correctly-unrolled 2-D latent that the CAE's coarser reconstruction does not
show — independent of defect 3's ambient-dimension mechanism.

**What is NOT settled, and should not be assumed either way.** The mismatches at this phase's
scale (`1.22x`–`2.0x`) are far smaller than the Phase 3 PU case's `4.4x`, which was large
enough to drive an exact, provable saturation. A modest mismatch at this phase's scale would
not necessarily saturate a cell outright (consistent with the one observed exception above),
but it can still contribute a smaller, non-saturating systematic bias to the RAW magnitudes on
every cross-dimension cell — including the specific numbers behind §7b's "Confound 1" analysis
(the `latent|intrinsic|H0|wasserstein` gap between `cae` and the other two candidates) and
§7a's "clean comparison" (`decoder_image|intrinsic`, itself a `1.22x`-mismatched cell). Whether
that contribution is large enough to move any of this phase's qualitative conclusions — none
of which claim a clean winner (§7f) — has not been checked and this note does not claim it
either way.

**What would settle it.** Re-run `decoder_substrate_ph_screen_run.py`'s `readout_matrix` call
with `prescale="median_distance"` (now available in `persistence_probe.cloud_distance_matrix`,
landed by the Phase 3 defect-3 fix) on the same fixture, seeds, and trained models, and diff
the resulting matrix against the recorded Round-3 numbers — in particular whether
`latent|ambient|H1|bottleneck`'s saturation pattern (plainae/topoae saturate, cae does not) is
preserved, changes magnitude, or disappears, and whether §7a's already-inside-seed-spread gaps
remain inside seed spread under the new normalizer.

## What is unchanged

- The fix landed in `persistence_probe.py` is opt-in (`prescale="median_distance"`); the
  existing `True`/`False` default behaviour is byte-identical, so every number
  `02.6-FINDINGS-02.md` records on disk — the full Round-3 measurement and spread tables,
  every ranking discussion in §7 — is unchanged by this note or by the Phase 3 fix.
- `decoder_substrate_ph_screen_run.py` is not edited by this note. `PRESCALE_CLOUDS = True`
  remains its default.
- This phase's own PH-derived conclusions (§7f: "no candidate is separated... this phase does
  not produce a clean winner, and does not claim to") were already stated as non-gating,
  non-conclusive, and hedged by two independently-discovered confounds (§7b, §7c). This note
  adds a third candidate contributor (defect 3's ambient-dimension mechanism) to that existing
  hedge, with the specific caveat above (evidence against it being the dominant cause of the
  one saturation actually observed) rather than asserting it changes anything.
