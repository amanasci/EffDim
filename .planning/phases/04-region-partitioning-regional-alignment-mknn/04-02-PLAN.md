<objective>
Re-run the PU split-half reliability sweep with D4-15's density-corrected estimator configuration,
extend it past k = 231 per D4-06, and freeze the curvature-field `k` by D4-07's spacing-free
absolute-increment rule — with the rule written into committed source before the first corrected
number is measured.

Purpose: this is the upstream half of the ROADMAP's Ordering constraint. The field `k` is the last
free hyperparameter in the chain, and D4-07 exists specifically because Phase 1's plateau rule for
`k*=15` failed on uneven spacing (`WINDOWS.md`: `STAGE2_K` was unevenly spaced, so the plateau was
maximal in *index* space, not `k` space). An absolute increment never compares gaps across unevenly
spaced points and is immune to that defect. Freezing `k` mechanically, by a rule declared first,
is what makes every downstream number un-tunable.

Output: `pu_curvature_rankability_run.py` extended additively; `04_pu_curvature_rankability_corrected.jsonl`
carrying the corrected sweep; `04_k_freeze.json` carrying `k_frozen`, the rule text, `rule_fired`,
and the per-k table.
</objective>
