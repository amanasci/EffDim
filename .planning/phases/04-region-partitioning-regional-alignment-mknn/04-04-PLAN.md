<objective>
Compute the density-corrected PU curvature field at the frozen `k`, apply the pre-registered
diametrical sign split, freeze the resulting `v` and labels as an auditable artifact, and report the
density confound in full — all before a single regional MKNN number exists.

Purpose: REGN-01, REGN-02, REGN-05 and REGN-06 in one pass, in the order the Ordering constraint
requires. The density diagnostics come *before* the split is trusted, and the split is frozen to
disk *before* anything downstream reads a label. Under D4-14 this phase reports the density confound
rather than controlling it, so the REGN-02 correlations and the region-level density comparison are
the only evidence bearing on whether a later regional difference is curvature or density — which is
precisely why they are computed and printed here, ahead of the number they qualify.

Output: `--mode partition` in the runner; `notebooks/.cache/04_region_partition.npz` (REGN-06);
`notebooks/.cache/04_density_diagnostics.json`; notebook sections 3-6 executed.
</objective>
