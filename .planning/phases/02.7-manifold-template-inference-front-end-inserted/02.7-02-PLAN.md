<objective>
Turn the tracer's single-`k` geodesic call into D-06's **sweep** and D-07's **first-class
disconnection read-out**. `k` is not a chosen value: the Betti vector must hold over a
non-trivial contiguous range of `k` or abstain condition (d) fires, and the whole curve is
reported. Component count at each `k` is its own number — a direct `beta_0` estimate — and
disconnection is never repaired.

Purpose: Phase 1's frozen `k* = 15` is explicitly **not** inherited here (D-06). It was frozen
for `n = 10,000` PU points at `D = 768`, while this phase's benchmark varies `n`, `D` and
density by design, so a fixed `k` means a different effective neighbourhood radius in every
cell. Sweeping is the only way the same instrument means the same thing across the grid.

Output: the k-sweep and component-curve surface on `geodesic_graph.py`, plus the
disconnection regression test.

(visible skip).*
</objective>
