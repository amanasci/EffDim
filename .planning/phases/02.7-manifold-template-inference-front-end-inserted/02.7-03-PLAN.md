<objective>
Build the dimension layer: all eight frozen estimators over a `k` sweep (D-09), the
plateau-and-consensus resolution that emits no number when the estimators do not agree (D-10),
and the anchor-neighbourhood local estimates whose dispersion feeds abstain condition (b)
(D-12). `src/effdim/geometry.py` is FROZEN — every estimator is called, none is edited,
duplicated or reimplemented.

Purpose: `d_frozen = 5` is **not** inherited. `02-FINDINGS.md` §6.4 records it as the outlier
of four estimates, three of which cluster at 18-25, and D-09's whole rationale is that
**disagreement is the signal** — averaging it away is what produced the suspect number. D-10
exists so that a bimodal `5/5/5/20/20/22/24/25` split yields *no* `d_hat` rather than a median
of ~20 that no estimator individually supports.

**Two planning-time corrections, verified against `src/effdim/geometry.py` source and measured
this session, both carried into this plan and both to be pinned as regression tests.** They
change what D-09's "eight estimators" and D-10's "majority of the eight" can honestly mean:

1. **`tle_dimensionality` is mathematically identical to `mle_dimensionality`.** MLE computes
   `(k-1) / (sum_j [log r_k - log r_j] + 1e-10)`; TLE computes `u_j = r_j / r_k` then
   `(k-1) / (-sum_j log u_j + 1e-10)` — the same expression, the same epsilon, the same
   `np.mean` reduction. Measured bit-identical in three regimes, including at `D = 768`:
   `3.3268496990203857` / `7.156801223754883` / `5.362279415130625`-class agreement to the last
   digit in every trial. The library's TLE is Levina-Bickel MLE under another name, not
   Amsaleg's Tight Localities Estimator. Consequence: the reported spread is over **seven**
   distinct estimators with one vote doubled, and D-10's majority always receives two
   guaranteed-correlated votes.
2. **`two_nn_dimensionality` and `mind_mli_dimensionality` are invariant in `k` by
   construction.** `two_nn` reads only columns 0 and 1 of `precomputed_knn_dist_sq`
   (`geometry.py:111-113`); `mind_mli` reads only column 0 (`geometry.py:227`). Slicing the
   precomputed array to different widths returns bit-identical values — measured constant across
   `k in {5, 10, 20, 30}`. Together with `gmst_dimensionality`, which RESEARCH.md already flagged
   as accepting neither `precomputed_knn_dist_sq` nor `k`, that is **three of eight estimators
   that cannot vary with `k` at all** — and two of them register a *perfect* plateau at every
   `k`, trivially satisfying D-10's plateau criterion for reasons that have nothing to do with
   the data.

Neither is a defect this phase may fix: `src/effdim/` is frozen. Both are facts the ratified
consensus rule (plan `02.7-08`) must be written against, blind, before measurement.

Output: `local_dimension.py`, its test file, and the two corrections pinned so they cannot be
silently rediscovered downstream.

(visible skip).*
