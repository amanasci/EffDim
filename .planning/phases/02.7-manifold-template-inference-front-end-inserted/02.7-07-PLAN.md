<objective>
Answer the phase's single hardest sizing question with measurement before anything is ratified.

`02.7-RESEARCH.md` measured, this session, that an unthresholded `ripser(..., maxdim=2)` call on
a genuine 2-manifold blows past a 90-second wall clock between `n = 400` and `n = 500`: `S^2`
costs `1.85 s / 8.59 s / 26.55 s` at `n = 200/300/400` and did not finish at `n = 500`; `T^2`
reaches `74.66 s` by `n = 800`. Cost grows roughly as `n^4` and is **template-dependent** —
`S^2` is the expensive one at matched `n`, so the budget must be sized against it and not
against an average. Those numbers were taken at `D = 3` on flat fixtures; the interaction with
a random orthogonal lift plus warp at `D = 768` is explicitly flagged unverified (RESEARCH.md
assumption A2).

This lands on assumption **A-05**: the ratified `n_ph` may not be large enough to resolve `H_2`
on `S^2`/`T^2` at all, in which case the benchmark measures the budget rather than the front
end. RESEARCH.md's own worked arithmetic puts the `H_2` degree alone at roughly **8.25 hours**
for a 3x3 `(D, noise)` factorial plus OFAT arms, 3 draws, `B = 10`, 2 metrics — a real, large
number that its Open Question 2 leaves unresolved and hands to planning.

Purpose: convert that estimate into a measurement, so plan `02.7-08` ratifies `n_ph`, `B` and
the grid levels against **numbers**, blind to any benchmark outcome. This plan measures compute
feasibility only. It scores no cell, computes no tally, and sets no decision threshold.

Output: the calibration runner and `02.7-BUDGET-CALIBRATION.md`.

*Planning note: spec-less probe fallback skipped: phase has no requirement IDs to probe
(visible skip).*
</objective>
