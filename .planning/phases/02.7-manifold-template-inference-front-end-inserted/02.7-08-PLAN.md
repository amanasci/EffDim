## Assumption-Delta Decision

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Detector signal:** pluralization — `"**SC-2 — Intrinsic dimension is estimated across multiple
neighbourhood sizes and several estimators…"`. The signal is real and runs deeper than the
single hit: `d_hat` moves from one number to eight estimators over a `k` sweep, global and
local; the distance metric moves from one to two, with D-03(c) making their **disagreement** a
first-class abstain condition.

**The noun that is now primary:** the **labelled read-out family** — concretely, an
`estimator-spread-over-k` rather than a *dimension estimate*, and a `(metric, diagram)` **pair**
rather than a *persistence diagram*.

**Decision: `promote`.** The general representation becomes primary and the specific one is
demoted to a derived variant. `d_hat` is no longer a stored quantity: `local_dimension` emits
eight named values at every swept `k` with no aggregation (D-09), and a single `d_hat` exists
only as the **conditional output** of `plateau_consensus`, which returns `None` plus a reason
whenever the estimators do not agree (D-10). Likewise no diagram is stored or reported without
its metric label; `template_decision.assert_labelled` raises on any unlabelled per-metric
read-out and is called at the end of every `decide`.

**Rationale in one line:** D-09 ("no aggregation") and D-03(c) (disagreement is a first-class
outcome) both point the same way — the plural form carries information the singular form
destroys, and `d_frozen = 5` is this project's own record of what that destruction costs.

**Invariant test encoding the generalized intent:**
`test_no_readout_without_metric_label` (plan `02.7-06`, Task 3) goes red the instant a later
phase reintroduces the singular, unlabelled-diagram assumption. Two supporting invariants:
`test_no_aggregation_in_global_estimates` (plan `02.7-03`) and `plateau_consensus`'s
`d_hat is None` branch, both of which fail closed rather than substituting a median.

**Accepted debt:** none — this is a promote, not an add-alongside.
</assumption_delta_decision>
