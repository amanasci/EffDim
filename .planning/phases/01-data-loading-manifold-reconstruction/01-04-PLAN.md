load from, and write the concrete machine-readable interface Phase 1 hands Phase 2.

Purpose: This is where the phase becomes irreversible. `k*` is baked into the ~1 GB
`isomap_{fit_key}.joblib` and into the `fit_key` itself, so a later change invalidates the Phase 2
gate verdict, the Phase 3 decoder and curvature field, and every Phase 4 MKNN number. Plan 03's
terminal checkpoint already confirmed the candidate against the evidence; this plan applies the rule,
writes the artifact, and — critically — makes sure the two qualifications Phase 1 cannot resolve
travel forward rather than dying at the phase boundary: the above-30 short-circuit-risk flag (D-11)
and the no-headroom `n_components` that may force a Phase 2 re-fit (D-12).

Output: `isomap_{fit_key}.joblib` (the only artifact in this phase carrying `dist_matrix_`, which is
what Phase 2's hand-rolled full spectrum needs), `phase1_handoff_{fit_key}.json`, section 5 of
`notebooks/01_manifold_and_gate.ipynb`, and the reserved §6 placeholder that gives Phase 2 an
unambiguous append point.

Task budget: 3 tasks, of which 1 is a zero-implementation verification gate. The two implementation
tasks are the freeze-and-fit (~25% context) and the handoff-and-close (~15%) — roughly 40% total.

overwritten nor silently reused — there is no manual cache invalidation step and no risk of a stale
pickle. That fact is recorded in `notes_for_phase2` explicitly, because it is what turns "elbow
exceeds the Phase 1 fit dimension" from an alarming edge case into a routine branch.
