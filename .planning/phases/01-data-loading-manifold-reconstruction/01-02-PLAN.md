### Open item 3 — the provisional `d` for the D-10 leading-eigenvalue metric

**Answer: `d_provisional` IS `n_components`. No new quantity is introduced.**

CONTEXT.md flags a real circularity: D-10's second stability metric needs a `d` to define "leading"
eigenvalues, but `d` is not frozen until Phase 2's residual-variance elbow (SPEC-04/SPEC-05). The
resolution is to reuse the value D-12's rule already produces — the ceiling of the median over the
eight geometric keys — as the provisional `d`, and to say so explicitly in the notebook.

Three reasons this is a resolution rather than a fudge, all of which must be stated in the notebook
cell that prints `D_PROVISIONAL`:

1. **It is not circular in the harmful direction.** `compute_dim` runs on the raw normalized 768-d
   embeddings and never sees the geodesic graph. Using a number derived from ambient-space
   estimators to judge geodesic-graph stability does not let the sweep's own output influence the
   criterion that judges it.
2. **It removes an arbitrary knob rather than adding one.** Every sweep fit uses
   `n_components=N_COMPONENTS`, so `Isomap.kernel_pca_.eigenvalues_` has exactly `N_COMPONENTS`
   entries and "the leading `d`" means "all of them." There is no truncation choice left to make,
   and therefore nothing to tune.
3. **It is frozen before the sweep exists.** `N_COMPONENTS` is computed in §3 of this plan; the
   sweep is §4 in plan 03. The pre-registration ordering is structural, not a matter of discipline.

The notebook must print, in the same output as the value: `d_provisional = n_components = <N>`,
sourced from the ceiling of the median over the eight geometric keys per D-12 with no headroom; that
Phase 2's residual-variance elbow may differ; and that if the elbow exceeds `<N>` the Isomap fit must
be re-fit — the accepted risk the user chose over adding a headroom constant.

Cross-references for the other five `Claude's Discretion` items: plan 01 answers item 2 (the D-08
margin) and item 4 (the output-hygiene policy); plan 03 answers item 1 (the three plateau
thresholds); plan 04 answers items 5 and 6 (the section-numbering convention and the written
Phase 1 -> Phase 2 interface).
